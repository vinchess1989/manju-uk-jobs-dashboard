St Ives Primary Care Network
Data Coordinator

The closing date is 11 December 2026

Apply for this job
Job summary

Join our friendly and supportive Primary Care Network team as a Care Coordinator and help us improve the health and wellbeing of local people. This is a varied role that combines administrative coordination, patient-focused work and the use of clinical systems to support healthcare projects across our member practices.

Working closely with practice teams and healthcare professionals, you will help identify patients who may benefit from additional support, contribute to targeted health initiatives, and ensure information is accurately recorded and managed. A key part of the role involves using practice systems to run searches, gather patient information and support projects that help improve patient care and reduce health inequalities.

We're looking for an organised, motivated and detail-oriented individual who enjoys problem solving, working with data and helping teams deliver excellent care. Whether your background is in healthcare administration, care coordination or a similar role, this is an opportunity to develop your skills while making a real difference to local communities.

If you enjoy working as part of a collaborative team and want to contribute to projects that have a positive impact on patients and GP practices, we'd love to hear from you.

Main duties of the job

This is an exciting opportunity to join our Primary Care Network team and support projects that help improve patient care across our member practices. Working closely with the PCN Manager, Clinical Director and colleagues from across the network, you will assist with a range of PCN-wide initiatives by using clinical systems to identify patient groups, run searches and collate information to support targeted approaches to care.

A significant part of the role involves working with patient data and practice systems to support planned projects and programmes. You will help gather and manage information, maintain accurate records and provide administrative support that enables the network to deliver proactive healthcare initiatives.

This role would suit someone who enjoys working with data, has excellent attention to detail and takes satisfaction from knowing their work helps improve patients' experiences and access to care. While some patient contact may be required, the majority of the role focuses on supporting the delivery of PCN projects within an agreed scope of work.

About us

We are a small, supportive team that values collaboration, professionalism and a practical approach to getting things done. You will work closely with the PCN Manager, Clinical Director and colleagues from across our member practices, including Grove Medical Practice, Moat House Surgery, Riverport Medical Practice and Spinney Surgery. The role combines practice-based and home working, providing flexibility while maintaining close links with the teams and projects you support.

We recognise the importance of supporting our staff and offer a range of benefits, including membership of the NHS Pension Scheme, 27 days annual leave plus bank holidays, and hybrid working arrangements. In return, we ask for enthusiasm, reliability and a willingness to work as part of a team committed to delivering high-quality support to practices and patients across the network.

If you are organised, enjoy working with information and systems, and would like to be part of a friendly team, focused on improving healthcare services, we would love to hear from you.

Please note: This vacancy may close before the advertised closing date if a high volume of suitable applications is received.

Job description
Job responsibilities

CARE COORDINATION AND PATIENT ENGAGEMENT

Support the identification of patients and patient cohorts who may benefit from additional support, proactive intervention or improved access to health, care, wellbeing and community services.
Proactively contact patients as part of agreed PCN projects and care coordination pathways.
Explain available services, schemes and interventions to patients clearly and sensitively and support informed participation and consent where required.
Support patients to understand and navigate relevant health, care, wellbeing and community services.
Coordinate agreed referrals, appointments, interventions and onward support.
Follow up patients where further contact or action has been identified as part of an agreed pathway or project.
Take a personalised approach to patient contact, recognising individual circumstances and barriers which may affect engagement or access to services.
Support patients to participate in shared decision-making and personalised care and support planning where appropriate.
Support patients to access appropriate information, self-management support, peer support and other relevant interventions.
Support continuity and coordination where patients are receiving input from different services or organisations.
Accurately record patient contacts, consent, referrals, interventions, actions and outcomes within the appropriate clinical systems.
Recognise where clinical, safeguarding or more complex support is required and escalate appropriately in accordance with established pathways.
Work within the boundaries of the Care Coordinator role and not provide clinical advice.

CLINICAL SYSTEMS, COHORT IDENTIFICATION AND DATA

Develop and maintain a high level of competence in SystmOne and other systems used by St Ives PCN and its member practices.
Run agreed searches and reports to identify patient cohorts for PCN, population health, personalised care and neighbourhood initiatives.
Collate identified cohorts across member practices and coordinate review by Practice Managers or nominated practice colleagues where required.
Support practices and PCN colleagues to ensure relevant activity, interventions and outcomes are appropriately coded and recorded.
Review agreed project data for completeness and accuracy and follow up missing information through appropriate practice contacts.
Maintain accurate spreadsheets, trackers and monitoring systems for PCN projects and services.
Collect and collate information from member practices and PCN services for agreed reporting requirements.
Maintain oversight of activity within assigned projects, including patient identification, contact, referrals, interventions and outcomes.
Identify gaps, inconsistencies, anomalies or outstanding actions within project data and take appropriate follow-up action or escalate where necessary.
Support routine reporting against agreed PCN, commissioned service and project requirements.
Collate end-of-project or end-of-cycle activity and outcome information, including changes in uptake or engagement where relevant, for review by the PCN Manager and Clinical Director.
Maintain accurate and timely records to support contractual reporting, evaluation and assurance requirements.

PCN PROJECTS AND POPULATION HEALTH

Provide care coordination and administrative support to agreed PCN projects and workstreams.
Support proactive population health initiatives aimed at improving access, reducing health inequalities and increasing engagement with healthcare services.
Support projects involving targeted patient cohorts, which may include personalised care, women's health, cancer screening, prevention, long-term conditions, frailty and other priorities identified by the PCN.
Coordinate the operational elements of agreed projects, ensuring relevant actions and patient activity progress through established processes.
Support patient recall and engagement activity associated with agreed PCN projects.
Monitor project activity and follow up outstanding information, patient contacts, coding or actions with nominated practice or PCN colleagues.
Support evaluation of interventions by collating baseline, activity and outcome information and providing clear summaries for review.
Contribute to the development and improvement of administrative and care coordination processes based on experience of delivering PCN projects.

PERSONALISED CARE AND PCN SERVICES

Support the delivery of personalised care initiatives, including relevant elements of Neighbourhood Initiatives.
Support the identification and coordination of agreed project cohorts.
Coordinate practice review of identified patient cohorts before patient contact where required.
Contact eligible patients to explain the support available and obtain appropriate consent.
Coordinate referrals into relevant PCN services, including Social Prescribing Link Workers and other personalised care services.
Maintain oversight of referral activity and follow-up within agreed projects.
Support accurate recording and coding of patient contact, referrals and outcomes.
Liaise with the Social Prescribing Link Worker team and other relevant services regarding operational delivery of agreed work.
Arrange operational meetings relating to assigned projects or services where required and support appropriate action tracking and follow-up.
Support the coordination and delivery of multidisciplinary working where relevant to assigned projects and patient cohorts.

INTEGRATED NEIGHBOURHOOD AND PARTNERSHIP WORKING

Support agreed Integrated Neighbourhood Team (INT) and neighbourhood health projects where care coordination, patient engagement, cohort identification or administrative support is required.
Work collaboratively with general practice, community services, social care, voluntary and community sector organisations and other relevant partners.
Support the coordination of agreed patient or population health interventions involving more than one service or organisation.
Liaise directly with agreed external services where required for established projects and pathways.
Support the collection and collation of information required to understand activity and outcomes from neighbourhood initiatives.
Attend relevant operational or project meetings where required for the delivery of assigned workstreams.

ADMINISTRATIVE AND PROJECT COORDINATION

Maintain clear and organised records for assigned PCN projects and workstreams.
Coordinate meetings associated with assigned projects or services where required.
Prepare agendas, take notes or minutes and maintain action logs for relevant operational or project meetings where appropriate.
Follow up agreed project actions with nominated individuals or teams.
Produce clear activity summaries and routine reports for the PCN Manager and Clinical Director.
Respond to routine enquiries relating to assigned projects and direct queries to the appropriate person where required.
Support effective communication between PCN services and nominated practice colleagues in relation to assigned workstreams.

PCN-WIDE WORKING

This is a PCN-wide role and the postholder will work flexibly across all St Ives PCN member practices and remotely according to service and project requirements.

The postholder will be expected to develop strong working relationships with PCN and practice teams and develop a good understanding of the systems, processes and working arrangements within each member practice.

Requests for information, review or action from member practices will be coordinated through Practice Managers or other nominated practice colleagues in accordance with agreed PCN processes.

An initial induction and embedding period will support the postholder to understand each member practice, its teams, systems and local processes and to establish effective working relationships across the PCN.

INFORMATION GOVERNANCE, CONFIDENTIALITY AND QUALITY

Maintain confidentiality at all times and handle patient and organisational information in accordance with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act, NHS information governance requirements, PCN and practice policies.
Access patient information only where there is a legitimate requirement to do so as part of the postholder's role.
Maintain accurate and contemporaneous patient and project records.
Follow agreed PCN and practice policies, procedures and information governance requirements.
Identify and escalate data quality, confidentiality, safeguarding or information governance concerns appropriately.
Support accurate coding and recording to enable reliable reporting and evaluation.
Participate in relevant audits, reviews and assurance activity associated with assigned projects and services.
Complete all statutory, mandatory and role-specific training required for the post.

Experience of communicating effectively with a range of people, including by telephone, email and in person.
