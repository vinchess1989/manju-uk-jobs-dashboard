Job description

Build partnerships with purpose


Could you bring organisations together around shared ambitions and turn those ambitions into activity that makes a difference?


As Corporate Partnerships Manager, you’ll manage relationships across an allocated portfolio of partners and help shape how those partnerships develop. You’ll work closely with partner contacts and colleagues across SOGB to plan and coordinate activity, keep commitments on track and create thoughtful experiences that work for everyone involved. The role spans relationship management, events, campaigns, employee engagement and reporting, with scope to help existing partnerships grow.


We’re looking for someone who enjoys building trusted relationships and making things happen. You’ll be organised, collaborative and confident coordinating people and priorities across different teams. You’ll bring good judgement, curiosity about what partners value, and a practical approach to turning shared ideas into clear plans and strong delivery.
