OFFICE ADMINISTRATOR 

Sawston, Cambridge | Full-time, Permanent | £ 22,000 to £ 26,000 depending on experience 

Intracat is an established, family-run commercial catering equipment design and supply business. Our clients include some of the best-known restaurant names on the High Street. 

We are growing and have an opening for an Office Administrator at our offices in Sawston, Cambridge. You don’t need years of experience. We are looking for a bright, motivated person who wants to learn, take on responsibility and grow with the business. 

What the Office Administrator will be doing: 

Acting as one of the first points of contact for clients, by phone and email 

Arranging and managing after-sales service visits 

Supporting our pre-sales administration 

Processing call data, preparing quotations, placing supplier orders and raising sales invoices 

Providing health and safety information where needed 

Keeping client records and databases up to date 

The Office Administrator will have: 

A clear, logical mind and good communication skills 

Someone who handles enquiries promptly and enjoys solving problems 

A proactive, customer-focused, “can do” attitude 

A good team player who is also happy working on their own initiative 

Confidence with everyday IT, including MS Word and Excel 

Experience in a similar role is helpful but not essential. If you’re early in your career and have the right attitude, we’ll give you full training. 

What we will offer our new Office Administrator 

Full training and real scope to progress within a growing family business 

Competitive salary, depending on experience 

20 days’ paid holiday plus bank holidays 

Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm, with some out-of-hours phone cover on a rota (just 2 days per month working remotely] 

Free on-site parking
