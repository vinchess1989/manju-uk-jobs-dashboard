Executive Assistant

Ref: BCR/JP/32545

Salary: £27,000 - £32,000

Hybrid

Cambridge

Bell Cornwall Recruitment is looking for a motivated Executive Assistant to join the Corporate and Commercial team at a leading national law firm in Cambridge. This is a great opportunity for an organised and proactive individual to provide high-quality support in a professional environment.

Executive Assistant Responsibilities:

Managing diaries, meetings, travel, and appointments
Providing administrative and executive support to senior professionals
Supporting client communications and business development activities
Assisting with billing, expenses, invoices, and financial administration
Managing workloads, deadlines, and multiple priorities effectively

Ideal Candidate:

MUST HAVE PREVIOUS EXPERIENCE WITHIN A LEGAL SETTING
Highly organised with excellent attention to detail
Strong written and verbal communication skills
Confident, proactive, and able to use initiative
Ability to build strong relationships and work well within a team

If you're an experienced Executive Assistant looking for your next opportunity in Cambridge, get in touch now!
