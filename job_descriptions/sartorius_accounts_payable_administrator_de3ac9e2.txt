Join our Finance team as an Accounts Payable Administrator and play a key role in maintaining an accurate purchase ledger and supporting the preparation of monthly management accounts and year-end statutory accounts. By processing invoices and payments, reconciling accounts and working closely with suppliers and internal stakeholders, you will help create reliable financial processes that enable our teams to focus on what matters most: better health for more people. Based at our site in Royston with hybrid working available, you will become part of a supportive team of eleven professionals who look forward to shaping the future together with you.

This is an opportunity to grow your purchase ledger experience in a friendly, busy environment at an expanding and innovative company.

Grow With Us – Your Responsibilities


Take full day-to-day responsibility for the purchase ledger, verify the accuracy of all postings and maintain complete year-end records across eight entities in the UK and Ireland
Set up and amend vendor records in SAP, including bank-detail changes in line with the Sartorius Fraud Control Policy
Process planned purchase-order and unplanned non-purchase-order supplier invoices through SAP X-Flow in accordance with the Sartorius authorization and approval structure
Manage staff expenses and company credit-card expenses through Concur, including finance approvals, employee queries, Corporate and Virtual Credit Card issuance, and payment of external card-provider invoices
Build and maintain effective supplier relationships, respond promptly to supplier queries by telephone and email, and resolve internal queries in collaboration with Procurement and other departments
Prepare month-end accounts-payable and supplier-statement reconciliations, manage the GR/IR account, down payments and debit balances, clear intercompany vendor and customer balances where appropriate, and participate actively in monthly balance-sheet reconciliation meetings
Process daily supplier BACS payments and ad hoc manual or urgent payments, while providing holiday cover for other team members


What Will Convince Us


You may have A levels in Maths and English
Previous experience in purchase ledger or accounts
Previous experience in managing expenses and credit cards
Intermediate Excel skills; experience with SAP and Concur is desirable
Strong communication skills and the ability to build effective relationships with internal and external stakeholders
A highly organised approach, with the ability to take ownership of varied processes, manage priorities and work independently
A willingness to learn new skills and processes, combined with a collaborative, team-focused approach


In order to commence working with us, the successful candidate must have the right to work in the UK.
