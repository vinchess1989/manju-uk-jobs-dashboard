Technician – Support Services (Procurement)

Circa £28,080 Dependent On Skills And Experience

Permanent, full time, 37 hours per week

Huntingdon

Keep our laboratories supplied, supported and ready to make a difference.

Are you organised, approachable and someone who enjoys keeping things running smoothly? Join Anglian Water as a Technician – Support Services (Procurement) and put those strengths to work supporting our laboratories and field-based teams.

From ordering laboratory supplies and liaising with suppliers to welcoming visitors and answering queries, you’ll help colleagues get the goods, services and day-to-day support they need. This is a varied role combining purchasing, administration and facilities support, with plenty of opportunities to work with people across the business.

What You’ll Be Doing


Support the Operational Support Manager with the day-to-day running of laboratory support services.
Welcome visitors to site, ensuring they are signed in and supervised throughout their visit.
Prepare meeting rooms and keep them stocked with the supplies colleagues need.
Let colleagues know when their deliveries arrive.
Place orders using SAP, our purchasing system, monitor temporary staff contracts and orders, and support the day-to-day operation of contracts for laboratory chemicals and equipment.
Be the first point of contact for questions about ordering and the delivery of goods and services, liaising with suppliers and colleagues.
Respond to general laboratory service queries, helping colleagues access the support they need.
Keep SAP records up to date, following the training and guidance provided.
Maintain accurate, up-to-date spending records.
Maintain regular monthly reports to support the team’s work.



We invest in our people - as a valued employee, you’ll be entitled to:


Personal private health care  
Generous double match pension – contribute 7% and Anglian Water will contribute 14%, giving a total contribution of 21%
24-hour Virtual GP service for you and your household 
25 days annual leave – rising with length of service and the ability to buy more
Life assurance (up to 8 x salary)
Personal accident cover (up to 5 x salary)
Excellent family friendly policies, such as 26 weeks full pay for maternity / adoption leave, as well as 4 weeks paid paternity / partner leave. Opportunity for shared parental pay
Bonus scheme  
Flexible benefits and working culture to support your wellbeing and lifestyle.



What You’ll Bring


Clear communication skills and the ability to build effective working relationships with colleagues and suppliers at all levels.
A reliable, committed approach and a willingness to support the team.
The ability to handle account queries, process payments and provide helpful support as a first point of contact.
Ideally, training in SAP, a system used to manage purchasing and business records.
A helpful, customer-focused approach, whether you’re speaking to someone over the phone or in person.
Good organisational skills, with the ability to prioritise tasks and manage competing requests.
Attention to detail and accuracy when entering information into databases and spreadsheets.
A confident, professional telephone manner.



Ready to put your organisational skills to work?

If you enjoy bringing people, information and practical support together, we’d love to hear from you. Apply and tap into your potential with Anglian Water.
