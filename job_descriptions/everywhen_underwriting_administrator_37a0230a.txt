Full job description

We're looking for a highly organised Underwriting Governance Administrator to join our MGA team. This is a fantastic opportunity for someone who enjoys coordinating processes, managing data, keeping things organised, and working with stakeholders across the business.

You'll play a vital role in supporting the Underwriting Governance Team and Central Underwriting Office (CUO), helping to ensure governance activities, reporting, audits, product reviews and committee actions are managed effectively.

This role would suit someone with previous administration, governance, compliance, risk, audit, insurance, or business support experience who enjoys working in a structured environment and has excellent attention to detail.

What you'll be doing

As Underwriting Governance Administrator, you'll provide administrative and coordination support across a range of governance activities, including:

Managing governance action logs and tracking actions through to completion.

Supporting governance committees and forums, including preparing agendas, papers and meeting packs.

Attending meetings and producing accurate minutes.

Maintaining governance documentation, policies, standards and records.

Producing regular governance reports, dashboards and management information.

Maintaining trackers covering product reviews, fair value assessments, audits, incidents and committee actions.

Supporting internal, external and insurer audit activity.

Monitoring governance review deadlines and coordinating stakeholder updates.

Arranging meetings, coordinating diaries and supporting ad-hoc projects across the Central Underwriting Office.

What we're looking for

We're interested in hearing from candidates who can demonstrate:

Previous experience in an administrative, governance, compliance, audit or business support role.

Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities.

Excellent attention to detail and a high level of accuracy.

Confidence producing reports, tracking information and maintaining records.

Strong written communication skills, including taking meeting minutes.

Good Microsoft Office skills, particularly Excel, Word and PowerPoint.

The ability to work independently while building positive relationships with stakeholders across the business.

Why join us?

Home-based role with flexibility.

Opportunity to develop experience within underwriting governance and product oversight.

Exposure to senior stakeholders and governance committees.

Varied role with responsibility across reporting, audits, governance and product review activities.

Supportive team environment with opportunities to develop your career within the insurance sector.

If you're highly organised, detail-focused and enjoy bringing structure and efficiency to important business processes, we'd love to hear from you.

Join us and help ensure robust governance, oversight and continuous improvement across our MGA business.
