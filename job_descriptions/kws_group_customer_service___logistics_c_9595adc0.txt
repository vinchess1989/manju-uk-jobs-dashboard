KWS UK Ltd. is looking for a proactive and detailed-oriented candidate as a Customer Service & Logistics Coordinator (m/w/d) in Thriplow. In this role you will support the efficient delivery of UK seed sales and providing a high standard of service to customers. The position is responsible for accurate order processing and administration within SAP S/4HANA, coordinating deliveries and maintaining clear communication between customers, sales, production and external processors. The role requires a proactive, organised approach to managing enquiries, resolving issues and keeping orders moving, while taking ownership of daily tasks and contributing to improved accuracy, teamwork and operational efficiency. This job is full-time (35h/week), hybrid mode (40% remote) is possible.

Your Tasks:


Process customer orders, amendments, invoices and credits accurately in SAP S/4HANA.
Manage the shared orders inbox, ensuring all orders and enquiries are actioned.
Provide timely customer updates on stock, orders, deliveries and issues.
Liaise with Sales, Production, Multiplication and toll processors to coordinate fulfilment.
Maintain accurate stock, order and delivery records, escalating risks promptly.
Support trial and multiplication seed administration, samples and certification as required.
Administer returns, rebates and credits in line with agreed procedures.
Produce accurate sales-versus-budget and operational reports.
Take ownership of tasks and contribute to efficient processes and teamwork.


Your Profile:


Experience in customer service, logistics or sales administration;
Confident Microsoft Office skills; SAP desirable;
Ability to manage competing priorities and detailed transactions;
Commercial awareness and willingness to learn;
Licensed crop inspection and certification is a plus;
Agricultural or seed knowledge advantageous;
Driving licence is essential for external visits;
Customer focus, accuracy and accountability;
Strong written and verbal communication, organisation and prioritisation skills.
