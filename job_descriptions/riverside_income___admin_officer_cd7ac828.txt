The difference you will make as an Income and Administration Officer

You would make a real difference by helping people sustain their tenancies and manage their rent accounts. At Riverside, we're looking for an Income and Administration Officer to play a vital role in helping customers maintain successful tenancies while maximising rental income across our services.

This is a rewarding role for someone with experience in rent collection, arrears management, debt recovery, income management or housing-related customer services who enjoys building positive relationships, having supportive but sometimes challenging conversations, and achieving successful outcomes.

If you're motivated by helping customers, find sustainable solutions while ensuring income is collected effectively, we'd love to hear from you.

Housing, social housing, local authority or customer account management environments.

Handling customer accounts, payment plans or debt recovery processes.

Why Riverside?

At Riverside, we’re a housing association with a difference – enhancing the everyday for all our customers. For 90 years, we’ve been revitalising neighbourhoods and supporting communities by providing the homes they need to live full, fulfilling and rewarding lives.

We have a portfolio of over 75,000 affordable residential and retirement homes across the UK. Our work ranges from homelessness services to social care, employment support to retirement living, and we need the best people on board to help us.

Role Profile

Responsibilities

The primary focus of this role is managing rent and service charge accounts, supporting customers to meet their payment responsibilities and taking proactive action to minimise arrears. In this role you will:


Manage rent and service charge accounts across a portfolio of properties.
Work directly with customers to agree realistic repayment arrangements, including home and scheme visits as required.
Monitor arrears and take timely action to recover outstanding debt.
Support customers to understand their tenancy obligations and payment responsibilities.
Promote available payment methods and encourage sustainable payment habits.
Work closely with colleagues to identify and resolve income-related issues early.
Liaise with internal specialists and external agencies where additional support is required.
Produce rent statements, maintain accurate records and complete income-related administration.
Support legal and enforcement processes where appropriate, including court attendance.


This role combines customer engagement, income recovery and administration, making it ideal for someone who enjoys balancing people-focused work with achieving measurable results.
