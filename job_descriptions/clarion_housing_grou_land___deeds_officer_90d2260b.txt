Location: Beacon House, March

Salary: £28,339 to £38,483 per annum

Hours: 36 hours per week

Contract Type: Secondment/fixed term contact - 12 months to cover maternity leave

This is a great opportunity to join our team, Employer of the Year Housing Heroes awards 2025 and Winner 2026 Inspiring Places to Work™ UK & Ireland

We are currently recruiting for a Land and Deeds Officer to join our team based in March.

Working alongside the Land and Deeds Manager, you will support the effective management of Clarion's physical and digital Deeds Library, ensuring accurate records are maintained and a high-quality service is delivered to internal and external customers.

The role will involve managing and auditing legal deeds, supporting the digitisation of records, maintaining accurate land and property information, and responding to land ownership and legal document queries.

You will also work closely with internal teams, external solicitors and other stakeholders to ensure the effective management of land and property documentation.

The ideal candidate will have:

Good working knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.
Strong administrative skills, with the ability to maintain accurate records and systems.
The ability to work independently, manage competing priorities and contribute effectively as part of a team.
A professional and customer-focused approach, demonstrating commitment to Clarion's values and acting as an ambassador for the Land and Deeds team.

If this sounds like an opportunity for you then please review the full role profile here and Behaviours & Mandatory Accountabilities OD level 5 before applying.

Salaries are just the starting point. Here at Clarion, we're dedicated to rewarding hard work and commitment, and providing benefits that support you and your lifestyle - dive in and find out more here or please visit our website for more information.

Closing Date: Wednesday 18th November 2026 at midnight.

This is a hybrid role with a base location at our Beacon House office in March. Candidates will be expected to work from the office at least two days per week and attendance to one of our London offices for team meetings as an when required.
