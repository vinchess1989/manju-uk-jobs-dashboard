We are delighted to be recruiting for two full-time Private Care Administrators to join the Royal Papworth Private Care administration team.

Royal Papworth Private Care is the private healthcare service of Royal Papworth Hospital, delivering specialist heart and lung care to private patients from across the region, the UK, and internationally.

The Private Care Administrator role is varied and rewarding, providing administrative support across a range of specialties, including Cardiology, Cardiac Surgery, Thoracic Surgery, Respiratory Medicine, Radiology, and Diagnostic Services.

At the heart of the role is a commitment to delivering an exceptional customer experience to patients, consultants, and other stakeholders through the provision of a high-quality, efficient, and professional administrative service. You will coordinate outpatient and inpatient bookings, support patients throughout their care pathway, and help ensure a seamless patient journey within Royal Papworth Hospital.

As part of a growing and developing service, you will have a real opportunity to contribute to and support an exciting programme of service improvement, helping to shape the future of private healthcare services at Royal Papworth Hospital.

The role profile is subject to job evaluation panel agreement.
