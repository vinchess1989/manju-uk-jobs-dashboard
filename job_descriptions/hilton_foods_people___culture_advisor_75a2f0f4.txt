About The Role

We are looking for an experienced People and Culture Advisor to act as the first point of contact between People & Culture and the wider business, providing advice and guidance, partnering managers with operational People & Culture support including employee relations and employment law advice. To support the People & Culture Business Partner in the implementation of the ongoing People & Culture Strategy.

As a People and Culture Advisor some of the responsibilities will be to:


Provide a customer focused People & Culture advisory service which supports and guides all aspects of the employee lifecycle.
Provide advice and guidance to all managers on a range of People & Culture policies, procedures and People & Culture related issues (including ER cases, probations, performance management etc
Provide regular updates to the People & Culture Business Partner on ER case loads and trends, with proposed solutions and actions.
Review and update internal People & Culture policies and communicate when required to the wider business
Absence management – Daily updating and monitoring of absence records, managing long term and short-term absences, chairing welfare meetings and coordinating Occupational Health referrals with the welfare meetings.
Providing general People & Culture administrative support – processing contractual changes, e-filing, responding to enquiry emails, maintaining the People & Culture inbox together with the People & Culture Co-ordinator
Support the People & Culture Co-ordinator in the management of all family/friendly policies
Support as needed, with the onboarding process such as introduction of new starters, preparation of relevant correspondence including job offers, issuing of new starter packs, processing employment contracts and setting up on internal database
Maintain accurate records using TMS and e-files
Providing and updating weekly KPI information
Attendance at factory daily review meetings as required.
Coordinate and deliver employee engagement activities – reward and recognition, employee forums, employee surveys, internal communication systems (internal app)
Supporting new and on-going People & Culture Projects as agreed


To succeed as a People and Culture Advisor, we ask that you have the following experience/expertise:


Ideally qualified, or studying towards CIPD level 3, along with People & Culture administration/advisory experience
Experience of dealing with a multitude of ER cases, from day to day to complex
Ability to build rapport and service at all levels of colleagues (from factory floor to senior stake holders), providing an excellent customer service experience
Computer literate including MS Office packages, in particular Excel. Intermediate Excel experience would be an advantage
Extremely organised attitude with the ability to deal with ever changing priorities
Experience with Time and Attendance and HRIS platforms would be an advantage
Any other European language other than English would be desirable
