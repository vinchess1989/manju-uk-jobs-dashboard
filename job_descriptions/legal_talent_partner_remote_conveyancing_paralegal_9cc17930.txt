Remote Conveyancing Paralegal

£30,000-40,000




Quality. Technology. Flexibility. A Better Way to Do Conveyancing.

We’re partnering with a fast-growing, forward-thinking residential conveyancing firm that is using technology, smarter processes and a collaborative team structure to change the way property law is delivered.

The firm is built around a simple idea: conveyancing should be simpler, clearer and faster — for clients and the people delivering it.

If you’re an experienced conveyancing paralegal looking for greater autonomy, better technology, a sustainable workload and the flexibility of a fully remote role, this could be an excellent next move.

Why This Role Stands Out

This is not a traditional conveyancing environment where paralegals are left to manage overwhelming caseloads with little support.

You’ll work within small, collaborative teams, with regular check-ins, structured 1-to-1s, feedback and personalised training.

The firm takes a quality over quantity approach to workload, maintaining sustainable caseloads and giving teams capacity to deal with peaks without compromising client service.

You’ll benefit from:

Sustainable caseloads and a strong focus on work-life balance
Small, collaborative teams
Regular support, feedback and development
Bespoke technology designed around legal teams
A culture where asking for help is encouraged
The opportunity to influence how technology and processes develop
Fully remote working with genuine flexibility

The result? A more manageable workload, better support and the freedom to focus on delivering excellent conveyancing rather than simply processing as many files as possible.




The Role

As a Conveyancing Paralegal, you’ll be an integral part of a residential conveyancing team, working alongside lawyers and other paralegals to deliver an excellent client service.

You’ll work across freehold and leasehold sales, purchases and remortgages, taking ownership of your matters while working collaboratively with the wider team.

Your responsibilities will include:

Progressing residential sales, purchases and remortgages
Handling freehold sales with minimal supervision
Managing most leasehold sale enquiries with minimal supervision
Managing most freehold purchase enquiries with minimal supervision
Liaising with clients, solicitors, estate agents and other third parties
Proactively identifying issues and keeping transactions moving
Reviewing and responding to enquiries
Maintaining clear, jargon-free communication
Using the firm's technology to manage your caseload efficiently
Identifying and resolving potential issues before they become blockers
Providing feedback to improve systems and processes

The firm's technology has been developed specifically around its conveyancing process, and legal professionals are encouraged to provide feedback on the tools they use every day.




About you

You’ll ideally have:

Strong experience in residential conveyancing
Experience handling freehold sales with limited supervision
Experience dealing with leasehold sale enquiries
Experience dealing with freehold purchase enquiries
Confidence communicating with clients and third parties
A proactive approach to progressing transactions
Strong organisation and attention to detail
Excellent communication and problem-solving skills
Confidence working independently in a remote environment
An interest in using technology to improve legal work

You don't need to be qualified. This role is suited to an experienced paralegal with strong technical conveyancing skills who is ready to take greater ownership of their work.

Although fully remote, you won’t be left alone with a laptop and a pile of files. Regular team check-ins, 1-to-1s and ongoing feedback are built into the firm's approach.




The Culture:

This is a firm deliberately doing things differently.

Rather than chasing maximum utilisation, it takes a sustainable approach to workload, helping teams maintain consistent service levels while giving people room to deal with the unexpected.

Its technology helps allocate work, streamline processes and keep teams informed, while its remote-working model is built around communication, supervision, feedback and development.

There’s also a strong culture of transparency and continuous improvement. People are encouraged to share ideas, challenge processes and help shape the future of the business.




The Package

£30,000–£40,000 depending on experience
Fully remote – work from anywhere in the UK
Flexible working hours
25 days' holiday + 3 days at Christmas + Bank Holidays
Full home-working setup and equipment provided
Comprehensive private healthcare
Free access to Spill mental health and wellbeing support
Company pension scheme
Enhanced maternity, paternity and adoption leave
Growth Shares, giving employees an opportunity to share in the long-term success of the business
Fully paid-for annual company weekend
Structured development and genuine progression opportunities

The firm's flexible approach is a key part of its culture, with many employees working flexible hours or part-time. The focus is on creating working patterns that work for both the individual and the wider team.

If you're an experienced conveyancing paralegal looking for a genuinely modern, supportive and flexible place to build your career, we'd love to hear from you.
