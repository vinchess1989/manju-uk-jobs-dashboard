Who is Mick George?

One Man, One Tipper, One dream, may seem a distant memory, but the dream is very much a reality, as we find ourselves approaching 40 years since the business started trading.

Now operating over 600 HGV vehicles from over 40 separate sites, employing more than 1,000 local people, gives an indication of the scale to which the business has evolved over the years and explains why the Mick George Group has grown to become one of the leading suppliers to the Construction Industry in the heart of East Anglia and East Midlands.

Our aim is to ensure that we provide the highest possible standards to each and every stakeholder that comes in to contact with any aspect of our business, whether that be as an employee, supplier, customer or other.

Role Purpose:

The Skip Stock & Asset Coordinator will be responsible for maintaining effective control and utilisation of Mick George's skip and container stock across the business. The roll will provide a central point of ownership for skip availability, location, condition, maintenance and utilisation, working closely with Logistics, Sales, Customer Service and operational sites.

Responsibilities:

Maintain an accurate understanding of skip and container stock across all Mick George Sites
Monitor daily stock levels by site, container type and size
Identify potential stock shortages before they impact customer service or sales
Working closely with the Recycling Logistics Director and Transport teams to ensure sufficient stock is positioned to meet daily and forecast demand
Coordinate the movement of stock between sites where required
Provide regular reporting on stock availability, shortages and areas of concern
Support forecasting of future skip requirements based on sales volumes, seasonal demand and business growth
Conduct regular customer site visits to identify opportunities to optimise skip sizes, improve asset utilisation and generate additional revenue through cross-selling of complementary services
Skip Utilisation - A key part of the role will be ensuring Mick George achieves maximum utilisation from its existing skip fleet
Damaged Skip Management - Take ownership of the damaged skip pipeline from identification through to return to service
Site Management - The role will require regular visits to Mick George operational sites

Skills & Experience:

The successful candidate should be:

Highly organised with strong planning skills
Comfortable working with data and spreadsheets
Confident communicating across multiple departments
Proactive rather than reactive
Comfortable challenging existing processes where improvements can be made
Able to prioritise in a fast-moving operational environment
Commercially aware and able to understand how stock availability impacts revenue and customer service
Comfortable spending time both in an office environment and across operational sites
Able to build strong relationships with operational, transport and commercial teams

Experience within waste management, transport, logistics, fleet management, plant hire or asset management would be advantageous but is not essential.
