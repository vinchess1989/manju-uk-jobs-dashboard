At HDC we want to employ staff who embody our icare values (Inspiring, Collaborative, Accountable, Respectful, Enterprising), all of which support our ultimate purpose of putting our customers first.

We are looking for people who are motivated by public service, embrace innovation, and want to play a part in building the organisation of tomorrow.

Job details

Are you organised, accurate and someone who enjoys helping customers and getting things right?

We are looking for an enthusiastic Accounts Receivables & Payable Assistant to join our Finance Team at Huntingdonshire District Council.

This is a varied role at the heart of our day-to-day financial operations, helping us make sure that money received by the Council is recorded accurately, customers receive the information and support they need, and supplier invoices are processed correctly and efficiently.

You do not need to be an accountant to succeed in this role. We are looking for someone with strong administrative and IT skills, excellent attention to detail and a genuine commitment to providing good customer service.

About The Role

The role is primarily focused on Accounts Receivable, which will make up approximately 70% of your work, with the remaining 30% supporting Accounts Payable.

Your day-to-day work will include:


allocating daily cash receipts accurately within the Council’s finance system;
raising invoices and credit notes;
processing authorised write-offs, refunds and customer credit balances;
responding to customer queries by telephone and email;
running regular reports for Council services;
issuing customer statements and weekly reminder letters;
helping colleagues resolve queries relating to customer accounts;
carrying out checks associated with Accounts Payable set-ups; and
matching supplier invoices ready for payment, making sure information is complete and accurate.


You will work with colleagues across the Council as well as residents, businesses, customers and suppliers, so being able to communicate clearly and build positive working relationships will be important.

About You

We are looking for someone who:


has 5 GCSEs at grade 5 or above, including Maths and English;
has good IT skills, particularly using Excel, Word and Outlook;
is confident communicating clearly with customers and colleagues;
is organised and able to manage their own workload;
has a high level of accuracy and attention to detail;
enjoys working as part of a team;
has a positive and enthusiastic approach;
is comfortable adapting when priorities change; and
takes pride in providing a helpful and professional service.


Experience of financial systems or financial transaction processing would be helpful, but it is desirable rather than essential. What matters most is your ability to learn, your attention to detail and your commitment to doing a good job.

Why Join Us?

This is a great opportunity for someone who wants to be a part of an important Council service.

The accuracy and timeliness of our financial processing affects residents, businesses, suppliers and services across the Council. Getting it right helps ensure that customer accounts are accurate, queries are dealt with promptly and suppliers are paid correctly and within agreed payment terms.

You will therefore be joining a team whose work may often happen behind the scenes, but which plays an important part in keeping the Council running effectively.

At Huntingdonshire District Council, our icare values – Inspiring, Collaborative, Accountable, Respectful and Enterprising – sit at the heart of how we work, and we are looking for someone who can demonstrate these values in the way they lead the team and deliver the service.
