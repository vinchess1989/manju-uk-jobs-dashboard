Public Affairs Officer




As the UK’s leading dementia research charity, we’re determined to stop this from becoming a reality. We’re working to revolutionise the way we treat, diagnose and prevent dementia. But we will not stop there. With your support, we will keep going until every person is free from the heartbreak of dementia. We exist for a cure.




The Public Affairs Officer will support the development and delivery of Alzheimer’s Research UK’s political engagement and influencing activity. The role will build relationships with parliamentarians and other political stakeholders, monitor and respond to parliamentary developments, and deliver briefings, campaigns, events and influencing activity that advance our priorities for dementia research, diagnosis, treatment and prevention




We are currently recruiting two, Public Affairs officers, one permanent and one 12-month fixed term contract. As part of your application please include which role you would like to be considered for. We are also happy to accept applications for those wanting to be considered for both




Key Responsibilities:




Public Affairs:

Develop and deliver political and stakeholder influencing plans to secure our key policy asks on dementia research, treatment and prevention.
Build productive relationships with parliamentarians through 1-1 meetings and our Dementia Research Champions programme for MPs and Lords Dementia Research Network for Peers and through other opportunities (e.g. constituency engagement, visits etc).
Develop and deliver proactive and reactive parliamentary activity to grow ARUK’s network of engaged, informed and supportive political stakeholders including through oral and written parliamentary questions, debates, Select Committee inquiries, party conferences and other opportunities.
Keep abreast of the latest developments in dementia research and the science and health policy environments and build expertise and confidence in communicating key points to political audiences through different channels both in-person and digitally.
Work with colleagues to provide tailored briefings, correspondence and other material for key decision-makers including Ministers, Special Advisers, Select Committee members and others.
Represent ARUK at coalition meetings, stakeholder forums and external events, building relationships and promoting our priorities.
Provide analysis, insight and regular reporting on parliamentary activity and opportunities to help further and inform ARUK’s policy and public affairs work.




Campaigns and awareness raising

Contribute to the development and delivery of public-facing campaigns and help shape campaign messaging for a political audience.
Work with the communications and digital teams to help develop digital content and plans including drafting articles for the website and social media.




Project management and events

Take responsibility for managing cross-organisational projects, such as parliamentary events, and co-ordinate input and delivery from colleagues across the organisation and externally.




Knowledge, skills and experience needed:

Degree-level qualification or equivalent relevant professional experience.
Experience of working with parliamentarians
Knowledge of the UK political system and an interest in politics, public affairs and campaigning
Experience of building positive relationships with decision-makers, influencers or supporters
Experience of working effectively with senior colleagues and adapting advice or communications for internal audiences
Excellent personal communication skills with the ability to communicate persuasively with people at all levels
Ability to write professionally and adapt communications for different audiences
Ability to work with a high level of accuracy and attention to detail
Ability to work collaboratively with colleagues and contribute positively to shared objectives
Ability to use initiative, identify practical solutions and progress work with appropriate guidance
Good project management skills and the ability to prioritise workload
Ability to use Microsoft 365 applications and databases or customer relationship management systems to maintain records and support project delivery
Commitment to ARUK’s vision, mission and values




Additional Information:

Ways of working:




As part of our Agile ways of working you will be required to work approximately 2 days a week from the office, which is subject to the requirements of the role and the business needs. Flexibility on where you work can be split between working from home and our office.

Roles that are classed as part of the Agile ways of working are not able to claim any costs for Mileage/Travel on Public Transport, Accommodation and/or Meals. This includes when attending the office for various meetings/events.




Our Office: Our office is at 3 Riverside, Granta Park, Great Abington, Cambridge, CB21 6AD.

Salary: Circa £32,000 per annum, plus benefits
