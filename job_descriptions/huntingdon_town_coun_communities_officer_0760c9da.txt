Communications Officer at Huntingdon Town Council
Communities Officer




Location: The Coneygear Centre, Huntingdon

Hours: 25 per week (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday, 9:30am to 3pm and Wednesdays 11:30am to 5pm; minimum of a half hour unpaid lunch break)

Grade: SCP 18-23 (actual salary £22,012 to £24,033)

Responsible To: Coneygear Centre Manager




Huntingdon Town Council is looking for an enthusiastic and community-focused Communities Officer to join the team at the Coneygear Centre.




Working alongside the Centre Manager, you’ll play an important role in supporting local community development and helping bring activities, events and opportunities to life.




What you’ll be doing:




Supporting the Community Garden
Helping organise and support community events, activities and guided sessions.
Supporting the community café and Homework Club.
Taking bookings and helping promote the Centre hire.
Working with local partners, community groups, hirers and members of the public.
Assisting with the smooth day-to-day running of the Coneygear Centre.




The role is mainly based at the Coneygear Centre, with occasional work at other Town Council offices.

If you’re organised, friendly and passionate about making a positive difference in the local community, and you have a passion for horticulture, we’d love to hear from you!




What we offer

Membership of the Local Government Pension Scheme
Generous annual leave and flexitime
Support for professional development
A positive, collaborative culture where your leadership will make a visible impact




In return, you’ll join a supportive, forward‑looking council with a strong civic tradition, a clear sense of purpose and a genuine commitment to its community. You’ll shape the next chapter of a town that is proud of its heritage and ambitious for its future.




This role does not meet the salary threshold for Skilled Worker visa sponsorship. Applicants must have an existing right to work in the UK.




To apply please email Shilpa for an application form (Please note thast CVs will not be accepted) and submit it by email or post by Friday 30th October to the email / postal address below:




Contact: Shilpa Desai-Sakaldip (Coneygear Centre Manager), Coneygear Centre, Buttsgrove Way, Huntingdon, PE29 1PE

Telephone: 01480 388677

Email: Shilpa.DesaiSakaldip@huntingdontown.gov.uk
