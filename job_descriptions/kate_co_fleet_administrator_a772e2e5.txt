Full job description
KC Group is recruiting on behalf of a leading healthcare organisation for an experienced Fleet Administrator on a temporary, fully remote basis. You will provide essential administrative support to the fleet management team, ensuring smooth operations, accurate record-keeping, and efficient vehicle coordination.

This is a Temporary, full time role, working remotely, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Monday to Friday and offering an hourly rate of £12.82 per hour.

Key Responsibilities Fleet Administrator role:

Fleet Administration: Manage day-to-day fleet coordination tasks, maintenance logs, and vehicle documentation.
Database & Record Management: Maintain up-to-date and accurate fleet records, tracking vehicle usage, servicing schedules, and compliance documents.
Stakeholder Communication: Liaise with internal departments, external suppliers, and drivers regarding vehicle bookings, repairs, and enquiries.
Process Support: Assist with processing invoices, resolving discrepancies, and generating routine fleet activity reports.
Key Requirements for the Fleet Administrator role:
Strong administrative experience, ideally within a logistics, transport, or fast-paced corporate environment.
Excellent organisational skills with high attention to detail.
Proficient IT skills, including MS Office and database management.
Ability to work effectively and autonomously in a fully remote setting.
