About the Company




Quell is a British defence and security AI technology start-up developing solutions to counter drone, vehicle, and human threats across the military, civilian and CNI sectors.




About the Role




To coordinate and manage the company’s business travel arrangements and employee expense processes, ensuring that bookings are organised efficiently, expenditure is controlled and financial records are accurate and up to date.




The role will be responsible for arranging domestic and international business travel, processing employee expense claims, reconciling company credit card transactions and maintaining supporting documentation for travel-related expenditure.




Working closely with the Finance Manager and employees across the business, the Travel & Expenses Coordinator will ensure that travel and expenses are managed in accordance with company policies, approval procedures and budgetary requirements.




The successful candidate will be highly organised, detail-oriented and proactive, with a practical, cost-conscious approach and the ability to manage multiple requests and deadlines.




Key Responsibilities




Business Travel




Arrange domestic and international business travel, including flights, hotels, rail, car hire and transfers.
Research and compare options to secure suitable, cost-effective arrangements within approved budgets.
Coordinate travel for exhibitions, conferences, customer meetings and other business activities.
Prepare and distribute travel itineraries and booking confirmations.
Manage changes, cancellations, refunds and travel-related queries.
Coordinate group bookings and accommodation where required.
Check relevant travel documentation and visa requirements, escalating any issues where necessary.




Employee Expenses and Company Credit Cards




Review and process employee expense claims, checking receipts, supporting documentation, business purpose and compliance with company policy.
Follow up on missing receipts, incomplete claims, discrepancies and unusual expenditure.
Reconcile company credit card transactions against receipts, supporting documentation and purchase orders.
Prepare travel purchase orders in accordance with approval requisitions, budgets and company purchasing procedures.
Ensure transactions are appropriately categorised and coded for accounting purposes.
Prepare approved expense claims and reconciliations for payment processing and review.
Maintain accurate records of claims, card transactions and outstanding items.




Finance Administration and Reporting




Check travel and other relevant supplier invoices against purchase orders, booking confirmations and supporting documentation.
Ensure invoices, expense claims and credit card transactions are correctly coded and submitted for approval or payment.
Maintain accurate and organised financial records to support month-end reporting and audit requirements.
Monitor travel expenditure and purchasing activity against agreed budgets where required.
Follow up on outstanding receipts, purchase orders, invoices and reconciliations.
Produce basic reports on travel costs, expenses and company card transactions activity.
Identify opportunities to improve administrative processes, cost control and record keeping.
Support the Finance Manager with other routine finance administration as required.




Skills and Experience

Essential




Experience in finance administration, expenses processing, purchasing administration, travel coordination or a similar role.
Strong organisational skills and excellent attention to detail.
Good numerical skills and confidence checking financial information.
Competent in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel and Outlook.
Ability to manage competing priorities and meet deadlines.
Strong communication skills and confidence liaising with employees, budget holders and suppliers.
A proactive and methodical approach to resolving queries and following up outstanding information.
Ability to handle confidential financial and personal information appropriately.




Desirable




Experience booking domestic and international business travel.
Experience processing employee expenses and reconciling company credit cards.
Experience preparing purchase orders and working with purchasing approval processes.
Familiarity with accounting software, purchase order systems and expense management platforms.
Experience supporting month-end finance processes.




Personal Attributes




Highly organised, reliable and detail-oriented.
Commercially aware, with a focus on cost control and value for money.
Proactive, practical and able to take ownership of tasks.
Approachable and professional when dealing with colleagues and suppliers.
Comfortable working independently within established procedures and escalating issues when necessary.




Scope and Financial Controls




The role is responsible for the day-to-day administration of travel, expenses and company credit cards, ensuring that transactions are accurately recorded and appropriately supported.




The Finance Manager retains overall responsibility for financial oversight, review of reconciliations and compliance with the company’s financial controls.




Details




Location: North Uttlesford / Greater Cambridge area, on-site
Five days a week on-site (ie: this is not a hybrid role)
Full UK driving licence required. If this isn't held, candidates will need to commit to obtaining within 6 months of their start date
Applicants must be fully eligible to work in the UK without restriction, time limit, or the need for current or future sponsorship.
