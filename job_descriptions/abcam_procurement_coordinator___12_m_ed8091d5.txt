The Procurement Coordinator – Operational Procurement is responsible for supporting the end-to-end procurement process by managing purchase orders and management accurate supplier and procurement records. The coordinator ensures data accuracy across procurement systems and provides reporting and analytical support to help the procurement team make informed decisions. Additionally, they help ensure compliance with company policies and contribute to process improvements.

This position reports to the Procurement Manager and is part of the Procurement Team and will be an on-site role.

The role is expected to last at least 12 months, with the possibility of extending to 15 months depending on demand.

In This Role, You Will Have The Opportunity To


Manage procurement activities within Oracle ERP, including item creation and supplier maintenance, requisition processing, purchase order updates and administration of blanket purchase agreements to ensure data accuracy and transactional efficiency.
Identify opportunities to improve continuously improve any and all procurement processes and cost efficiencies
Support Procurement Team with decision-making through reporting and data analysis.


The Essential Requirements Of The Job Include


Previous experience within Procurement or Administration is advantageous
Proficiency with ERP systems (Oracle) and Microsoft Excel – preferred, not required
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail with a growth and flexible mindset


It would be a plus if you also possess previous experience in:


Capable to manage the complexities of operating with corporate/global oriented suppliers
Experience in biotechnology/ pharmaceutical/ life sciences is preferred although not essential
