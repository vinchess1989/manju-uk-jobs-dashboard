Analyst – Due Diligence & Investigations

iSanctuary | Cambridge | Entry level




We’re looking for an Analyst to join our Cambridge team, working mainly on due diligence assignments, with some involvement in our specialist investigations and crypto tracing work.

iSanctuary helps clients understand the people and organisations they do business with. We research backgrounds, examine business interests and identify risks that may affect a commercial relationship, investment or other decision.

The role would suit someone who enjoys detailed research, asks good questions and can turn their findings into clear, well-written reports.

 

What you’ll be doing

Researching individuals and companies using corporate records, public information, media and specialist databases.
Examining company ownership and business relationships, including across different jurisdictions.
Carrying out sanctions, politically exposed persons and adverse media checks.
Identifying potential concerns, such as links to fraud, corruption, litigation or regulatory action.
Checking the reliability of information and following up on gaps or conflicting findings.
Writing research summaries and contributing to client reports.
Supporting investigations, asset tracing and cryptocurrency research where needed.
Keeping accurate records of your research and handling confidential information carefully.

 

What we’re looking for

You’ll need strong research skills, good written English and close attention to detail. We’re looking for someone who can weigh up information, distinguish facts from allegations and explain why a finding matters.

You should be organised, comfortable working through unfamiliar subjects and willing to ask for help when needed. An interest in current affairs, business, financial crime or international issues would be useful.

Previous experience in due diligence or investigations is not essential. A relevant degree may be helpful, but we’re equally interested in applicants who can demonstrate their ability through work, study or independent projects. Additional languages or familiarity with corporate databases and open-source research would be an advantage.

 

Working with us

You’ll work alongside experienced analysts and investigators on UK and international matters, learning how to conduct thorough research and produce reports clients can rely on. As your experience grows, you’ll take on more responsibility and have opportunities to contribute across our three service areas: Risk Advisory & Due Diligence, Specialist Investigations, and Crypto Tracing & Recovery.

 

How to apply

Please send your CV and a short cover letter to info@isanctuary.io, with “Analyst Application” in the subject line. Tell us why you’re interested in the role and what you would bring to the team.
