We are looking for a professional, organised, and proactive individual to join our Critical Care Administration Team, supporting both the Intensive Care Unit (ICU) and Elective Recovery Unit (ERU).

This varied and rewarding role provides comprehensive administrative support to the Critical Care Unit, including the management of staff rosters through the electronic rostering system (Allocate Optima). The successful candidate will support colleagues with annual leave requests, contractual changes, flexible working arrangements, and workforce administration, while working closely with a wide range of multidisciplinary teams.

The role requires the ability to build and maintain strong working relationships with a large and diverse Critical Care workforce, as well as key support departments such as Human Resources, Temporary Staffing, and the E-Rostering Team. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills are therefore essential.

The successful applicant will be highly organised, demonstrate exceptional attention to detail, and be able to prioritise their workload effectively within a busy environment. Working alongside a small team of administrative colleagues, they will also become an integral part of the wider Critical Care team, demonstrating the Trust's values through their daily interactions.
