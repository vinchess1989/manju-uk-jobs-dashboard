Job Title: Receptionist

Location: Duxford, Cambridgeshire

Contract: Zero Hours, Casual

Hours: Ad-hoc holiday and sickness cover

We're looking for a professional and approachable Receptionist to join our Duxford Head Office team on a zero-hours basis to cover holidays and sickness.

As the face of SMT GB, you'll be the first point of contact for visitors, customers, contractors and employees, providing a welcoming and efficient reception service while supporting the smooth day-to-day running of our head office.

This is a varied role that would suit someone who enjoys working with people, can multitask effectively and takes pride in delivering excellent customer service.

What You'll Be Doing


Welcoming visitors, customers and contractors and ensuring sign-in procedures are completed correctly.
Managing incoming calls and directing enquiries to the appropriate departments.
Monitoring and responding to the reception email inbox.
Processing incoming and outgoing mail, including preparing post for Royal Mail collection.
Maintaining stationery supplies and placing orders within budget guidelines.
Supporting colleagues and managers with office equipment requests.
Coordinating employee rental vehicle bookings.
Assisting with the scheduling of employee health checks and other appointments.
Providing overflow telephone support for busy departments.
Managing call-back requests and communicating them to the relevant teams.
Supporting meeting arrangements, including room bookings, refreshments and deliveries.
Arranging taxi bookings for visitors and employees as required.
Completing shift handovers and communicating outstanding actions.
Securing the reception area at the end of shift.
Providing general administrative support across the business.


What You'll Bring

You'll be a confident and professional individual who enjoys providing excellent customer service and creating a positive first impression. With strong communication and organisational skills, you'll be comfortable managing a variety of administrative tasks while maintaining a welcoming and efficient reception service. You'll be proactive, dependable and able to adapt to changing priorities in a busy office environment.

Essential


Previous experience in a receptionist, front-of-house, administration or customer service role.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
A professional, friendly and customer-focused approach.
Strong organisational skills with excellent attention to detail.
The ability to manage multiple priorities in a busy office environment.
Good IT skills, including Microsoft Office applications.
A reliable, flexible and proactive attitude.


Desirable


Experience working within a busy office environment.
Experience handling high volumes of telephone calls.
Knowledge of visitor management systems.


What's In It For You?

At SMT GB, we're committed to supporting our people and helping them succeed. In return for your contribution, you'll receive:


Competitive hourly rate.
Flexible working arrangements.
Employee Assistance Programme (EAP).
Training and development opportunities.
Friendly and supportive team environment.
