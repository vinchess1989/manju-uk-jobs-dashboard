We are delighted to offer an excitingfull-time opportunity for an enthusiastic and dedicated Medical Secretaryto join our friendly and supportive Clinical Administration Secretarial Team within Cardiology.

Medical Secretaries play a vital role in supporting our clinicians and ensuring the delivery of high-quality care and an excellent patient experience. With strong keyboard skills and excellent attention to detail, you will undertake a range of essential administrative duties, including transcribing clinical correspondence, adding patients to waiting lists, maintaining accurate records and inputting data.

As an effective communicator, you will be confident in providing clear and timely information to both internal and external stakeholders, while always maintaining patient confidentiality and professionalism.

Our team supports the Trust from two locations:Royal Papworth Hospital on the Cambridge Biomedical Campus and Kingfisher House in Huntingdon. This post will primarily be based at Kingfisher House, with an expectation that you will also attend Royal Papworth Hospital, as required. Once you are fully trained and established in the role, there will be an opportunity to work remotely for part of the week.

If you are looking for a new challenge, enjoy variety in your work and want to be part of a team that makes a real difference to our patients' journey, we would love to hear from you.
