Role Description




The Executive Assistant role is a full-time, on-site position based in Cambridge. The Executive Assistant will provide high-level administrative and organizational support to senior leaders, including managing calendars, scheduling meetings, and coordinating travel and expense reports. Daily responsibilities include preparing correspondence and presentations, organizing documents, maintaining records, and supporting executive communication with internal and external stakeholders. The role also involves handling confidential information, prioritizing tasks, and ensuring that executives are prepared for meetings, events, and deadlines. The Executive Assistant will collaborate with other administrative staff to support smooth office operations and efficient workflows.







Candidates should possess strong Executive Administrative Assistance and Executive Support skills, including calendar management and meeting coordination.
Candidates should possess experience with Expense Reports and related financial or travel administration tasks.
Candidates should possess excellent Communication skills, both written and verbal, to support executives and liaise with stakeholders.
Candidates should possess solid Administrative Assistance skills, including document preparation, filing, and general office coordination.
Proficiency with office productivity tools (e.g., Microsoft Office or similar software).
Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple priorities.
Experience supporting senior leadership or working in a professional services environment is beneficial.
Relevant education or training in business administration, office management, or a similar field is an advantage.
