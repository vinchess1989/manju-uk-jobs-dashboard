Job Description

Connells Group, the Home of Opportunity, is looking for a highly organised and motivated Sales Administrator to join our successful Kevin Henry residential sales team, based in Saffron Walden, with support provided to our Hertford office.

A quick look at the role

As Sales Administrator, you'll play an important part in supporting the smooth running of our sales offices, providing essential administrative support to the team and helping deliver a great experience for our customers.

Your responsibilities will include uploading property details onto various property portals, handling customer enquiries both in branch and over the telephone, maintaining accurate records and supporting the day-to-day administration of the business.

You'll be someone who enjoys being organised, takes pride in getting the details right and is happy to get involved wherever support is needed.

What's in it for you?

Industry-leading training and development
A clear career ladder with opportunities for progression
A supportive, rewarding and fun working environment
Team incentives and recognition
The opportunity to gain a great understanding of the property industry
Career pathways across Sales, Lettings, Mortgage Services and other areas of Connells Group

Skills and experience required

Customer-focused, with a friendly and professional approach
Resilient, positive and able to use your initiative
Numerate and detail-oriented, with good accuracy
Highly organised, with the ability to prioritise a varied workload in a fast-paced environment
Excellent verbal and written communication skills
IT literate, with experience using Microsoft Office, email and internet systems

Previous experience in administration or customer service would be beneficial, although we're just as interested in finding someone with the right attitude, enthusiasm and willingness to learn.

We're ideally looking for someone to join us on a full-time basis, although we would be happy to consider part-time hours for the right candidate.
