Trade Waste Business Support Officer

Salary: £15.74 to £17.06 per hour
Location: Waterbeach
Job Type: Full-time, Temp - 6 Months

REED Business Support are representing our client who are seeking a dedicated Trade Waste Business Support Officer to join their team. This role is crucial in supporting the Waste and Environment Service by providing robust business support. The successful candidate will be instrumental in facilitating project management processes and maintaining high standards of administrative and operational support.

Day-to-day of the role:

Produce various reports, correspondence, presentations, and documentation with accuracy.
Input data and interrogate systems effectively.
Provide advice, guidance, and information to a diverse range of audiences.
Maintain precise records using the organisation's admin/finance/management systems.
Understand and facilitate project management processes.
Occasionally travel to off-site destinations to support events and conferences.
Support officers at evening meetings very occasionally.

Required Skills & Qualifications:

A good level of general education, including GCSEs or equivalent in English and Maths (at grade C or above).
NVQ level 3 in Business Administration, Customer Services, or a related subject.
Excellent keyboard and mouse skills.
Strong verbal and written communication skills.
Experience with Tascomi Software and Microsoft Office packages (Word, Excel, etc.).
Knowledge of South Cambridgeshire District Council's organisation, structure, and the inter-linkages within the Corporate Areas.
Demonstrable experience in office practices and procedures, including financial, administrative, and operational aspects.
Understanding of food safety and health and safety regulations.

To apply for the Trade Waste Business Support Officer position, please submit your CV and cover letter detailing your relevant experience and why you are interested in this role
