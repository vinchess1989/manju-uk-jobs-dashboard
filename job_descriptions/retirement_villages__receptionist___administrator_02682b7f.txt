At Retirement Villages Group, we create vibrant communities where people can live well, stay independent and feel connected. We are looking for a friendly and organised Administrator / Receptionist to join our team at Debden Grange, our beautiful retirement village in Saffron Walden. This is a part-time position working 24 hours per week, Tuesday to Friday, 9.30am to 3.30pm.

As the first point of contact for residents, visitors and contractors, you'll play a key role in creating a warm welcome while providing essential administrative support to the village team. If you enjoy building relationships, staying organised and helping a community thrive, we'd love to hear from you.

About The Role

This is a varied role that combines front-of-house responsibilities with administrative support. You'll be at the heart of village life, ensuring residents and visitors receive an exceptional experience while helping to keep day-to-day operations running smoothly.

What you'll be doing


Providing a professional, warm and welcoming reception service for residents, visitors and contractors
Greeting guests, managing enquiries and directing visitors appropriately
Answering and transferring telephone calls in a professional manner
Managing guest suite, meeting room and village space bookings
Maintaining visitor records, site directories and village communications
Supporting building security procedures and ensuring access protocols are followed
Assisting with payroll administration, timesheets and payroll-related enquiries
Maintaining accurate records and administrative systems
Providing general administrative support to the General Manager and wider village team
Supporting health and safety procedures, including emergency and evacuation processes
Helping maintain a safe, tidy and welcoming reception environment at all times


About You

You'll be an organised, approachable and customer-focused individual who enjoys working with people and can confidently manage a busy and varied workload.

We're looking for someone with:


Previous administration and/or reception experience
Excellent customer service and communication skills
Strong organisational skills and attention to detail
Good IT skills, including Microsoft Office
The ability to prioritise tasks and work independently
A proactive and positive attitude
Experience supporting payroll administration would be advantageous, but is not essential


Why join RVG?

At Retirement Villages Group, you'll be part of a team that genuinely makes a difference to people's lives. Every day you'll contribute to creating a welcoming, supportive environment where our residents can live independently and age well.

Our values guide everything we do:

Age Well - We create a world where everyone has the opportunity to age well.

Community - We believe in the power of community.

Keep Improving - We get things done and find ways to keep improving.

Invest Wisely - We treat every penny as if it were our own.

Planet Positive - We recognise the climate emergency and work hard to have a positive impact on the planet.

One Team - We support one another, learn together and celebrate success together.

Are looking for a rewarding role in a vibrant village community where no two days are the same, we'd love to hear from you.

Join Debden Grange and help create a place where people can age well, stay connected and truly feel at home!
