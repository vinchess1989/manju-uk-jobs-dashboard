Events Coordinator – Cambridge

Up to £35k| Permanent | 40 Hours per Week

Looking for your next opportunity in events? This is your chance to join a well-established team in a varied, customer-facing role where no two days are the same.

We’re looking for an organised, proactive and personable Events Coordinator to help deliver exceptional events and hospitality experiences. You’ll coordinate bookings, manage event administration and ensure everything runs smoothly from the initial enquiry through to delivery.

What we’re looking for:

Experience in events coordination, event management or a similar administrative role.

Excellent organisational skills and attention to detail.

Confident communication skills and a professional, customer-focused approach.

Good IT skills, including Microsoft Office.

Experience creating engaging social media content.

The ability to manage competing priorities and work independently or as part of a team.

Flexibility to support occasional evening and weekend events.

What’s on offer?

Highly competitive salary.

26 days’ annual leave plus bank holidays.

Defined benefit pension scheme.

Free on-site parking.

Free meal during working hours.

Access to on-site gym and sporting facilities.

Birthday day off and additional loyalty benefits, subject to qualifying criteria.

Discretionary bonus scheme and other staff benefits.

Time off in lieu for additional event working hours.

This is a fantastic opportunity for someone who enjoys bringing people together, thrives on organisation and wants to play a key role in delivering memorable events.

Interested? Apply today and take the next step in your events career.
