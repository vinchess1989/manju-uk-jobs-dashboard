12 Month Fixed Term Contract

Are you passionate about supporting people through life’s financial challenges? Do you thrive in a caring, customer focused environment where your empathy, resilience, and problem solving skills truly matter? If you're looking for a role where you can make a meaningful impact and be part of a team that puts people first, we’d love to hear from you!

We’re looking for a confident and compassionate Financial Support Adviser with excellent telephone skills and a genuine desire to help others. You’ll be part of a close knit team, working together to support mortgage customers through times of financial difficulty. This role involves having honest and sometimes challenging conversations, so being calm, empathetic, and solution focused is key.
