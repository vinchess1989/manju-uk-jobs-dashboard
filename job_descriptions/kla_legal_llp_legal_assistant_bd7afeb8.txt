About the job

KLA Legal Ltd is a boutique law firm providing strategic legal counsel for an evolving digital world. We partner with organizations navigating complex legal, regulatory and operational challenges, delivering sophisticated counsel with the responsiveness, accessibility and personal attention that define the boutique model.




KLA’s practice spans cybersecurity, data privacy, incident response, technology, life sciences, regulatory compliance and business risk. Our lawyers bring specialized expertise to the challenges facing organizations today and act as trusted advisers on the broader legal issues affecting their businesses.




Our approach is built on strategic partnership, specialized expertise, practical excellence, integrity and forward thinking.




Role description

This is a full-time, fully remote Legal Assistant position (non-lawyer, support role) in KLA Legal’s Cybersecurity, Data Privacy & Incident Response practice. You will provide administrative, organizational and research support to our lawyers as they help clients respond to cybersecurity incidents and data breaches, meet their privacy obligations and manage regulatory investigations.

This role suits someone who is highly organized, detail-focused and interested in the intersection of law, technology and data protection. No legal qualification is required. It offers excellent exposure to fast-moving, high-profile matters and the chance to build specialist knowledge in a growing area of law.




Responsibilities

Support lawyers on cybersecurity incident and data breach matters from initial notification through to resolution
Help track incident timelines and statutory deadlines, 
Organize, manage and maintain case files, document bundles and matter records
Coordinate communication and scheduling with clients, forensic investigators, insurers and other third-party advisers
Assist with preparing materials for regulatory inquiries and correspondence with authorities such as the ICO
Handle diary management, meeting notes, billing support, and general practice administration
Keep up to date with developments in cybersecurity, data protection law, and regulatory enforcement




What we’re looking for

Previous experience as a legal assistant, paralegal, legal secretary or in a similar administrative role (law firm or in-house experience is an advantage)
Interest in cybersecurity, data privacy, technology, insurance or regulatory compliance
Excellent organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to handle urgent, time-sensitive work and competing priorities
Strong attention to detail and accuracy
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Confidence with Microsoft Office and document management systems
Discretion and sound judgement when handling confidential and sensitive information
Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively with remote colleagues
A law degree, LLB, GDL, CILEx or paralegal qualification is welcome but not required
Right to work in the UK




Why KLA Legal

KLA offers the chance to work alongside experienced practitioners on sophisticated matters at the intersection of law, technology, cybersecurity and innovation. Our legal assistants are given real responsibility, direct involvement in live matters and the opportunity to develop specialist knowledge in an evolving area of law.
