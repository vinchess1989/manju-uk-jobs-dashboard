Description

At Envirovert we take the pressure off UK businesses by supporting their packaging compliance needs - so they can focus on what they do best. We offer tailored cost projections, personalised buying strategies, and risk assessments to ensure our clients feel confident that their compliance is in expert hands.

Our expertise in PRN (Packaging Recovery Note) trading, packaging regulatory proficiency, and waste and resource knowhow, spanning more than two decades, we’re proud to help our clients reduce costs and improve efficiency through practical, informed strategies.

Our clients come in all shapes and sizes—brewers, retailers, builders’ merchants, bottlers, manufacturers—and we thrive on that variety. No two clients are the same, and that’s exactly how we like it.

We’re big believers in building strong, honest relationships - with our clients, each other, and the world around us:


Colleagues: We collaborate, support, and push each other to grow.


Customers: We work closely with our clients to power a more circular, sustainable economy.


Community: We believe in doing right by the people and networks we’re part of.


Environment: We’re committed to using resources wisely to protect our shared future.


Here’s where you come in.

As part of our small, dynamic team, you’ll be right at the heart of the business. Every team member plays a vital role in shaping our mission and delivering real results - while gaining hands-on experience in a fast-paced, highly regulated field.

We’re proud to be an equal opportunities employer and foster a friendly, inclusive workplace where individuality is valued and encouraged.

If you're motivated, curious, and looking to grow your career in a purpose-driven, people-first company—we’d love to hear from you.

About The Role

Are you someone who enjoys building strong client relationships while ensuring everything runs smoothly behind the scenes? We're looking for a Compliance & Account Manager to join a growing team where you'll play a key role in supporting members, managing accounts, coordinating compliance activities, and helping organisations meet their regulatory obligations.

This is a varied and rewarding position that combines account management, compliance coordination, and operational support. You'll be the day-to-day point of contact for a portfolio of members, developing trusted relationships, providing guidance on reporting requirements and compliance processes, and ensuring queries are resolved efficiently. You'll support member onboarding, coordinate audits and compliance reviews, maintain accurate records, and work closely with internal teams to deliver an excellent customer experience.

What We Need From You

We're looking for an organised and proactive professional who can combine excellent account management skills with a strong understanding of compliance requirements. You'll be comfortable building relationships with clients, providing guidance, and managing multiple priorities while maintaining exceptional attention to detail.

You'll be a strong communicator who can explain complex processes in a clear and practical way, build trusted relationships with stakeholders, and take ownership of issues through to resolution. A high level of organisation, excellent record-keeping skills, and the ability to work collaboratively across teams are essential. Experience with compliance audits, regulatory submissions, PRNs, or packaging compliance legislation would be highly advantageous.



Most importantly, we're looking for someone who enjoys working with clients, has a solutions-focused approach, and is passionate about delivering a high-quality service while ensuring compliance standards are consistently met.

What We Offer

25 days annual leave plus recognised bank holidays


hybrid working structure


auto enrolment into company pension scheme


Monday – Friday with no shifts


4 p.m. finish on a Friday


Access to a savings platform that includes discounts and money-off promotions from 800+ retailers
