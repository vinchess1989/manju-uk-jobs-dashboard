Full Time | Permanent Post




We are seeking an experienced, enthusiastic and highly motivated Compliance Officer

to support both The Leys School and St Faith’s School to help ensure that the Schools 

and the Charity meet their legal and regulatory obligations, while promoting a practical, 

proactive and collaborative approach to compliance. 




Reporting to the Senior Legal and Compliance Officer and working primarily at The 

Leys, you will support both schools, as required, and help shape and deliver a 

consistent compliance framework across the two schools as they work towards 

becoming a single, integrated through school in September 2028. 




The successful candidate will:

Support compliance monitoring and processes, policy management, inspection readiness and data protection activities
Work collaboratively and proactively with colleagues across all areas of the operation.
Be able to follow direction but use initiative and experience to make appropriate decisions.
Have excellent organisational and communication skills, strong attention to detail, and proven experience in a compliance, governance or risk management role. 




For full details of the role and responsibilities please see the Compliance Officer Job 

Description and Person Specification at Vacancies - The Leys




How to apply:




If you are interested in applying, please submit a completed application form including a covering letter, to the Recruitment Team (recruitment@theleys.net), The Leys School, Cambridge, CB2 7AD by 12.00pm on Monday 19 October 2026.




Interviews are provisionally scheduled for the week commencing Monday 26 October 2026.




This role has the opportunity for regular contact with pupils and therefore is exempt from the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974.
