Operations & Events Co-ordinator

Posted yesterday by Darling Moo Talent Ltd

Cambridge, Cambridgeshire
£26,000 per annum
Contract
Easy Apply
Apply Now

We’re partnering with a well-established Cambridge based, not for profit organisation.
They are looking for an enthusiastic Operations & Events Co-ordinator to join their friendly and collaborative team.

This is a fantastic opportunity for someone who enjoys variety, thrives on keeping things organised and loves being at the heart of a busy organisation. You'll be involved in everything from coordinating events and projects to supporting customer relationships, business operations and finance administration.

Key Responsibilities

Coordinate the logistics and administration of events, training programmes and projects.
Manage calendars, project trackers, documentation and event communications.
Act as a key point of contact for customers, clients and external partners, delivering excellent customer service.
Support business development activities, including managing enquiries, preparing proposals and updating CRM systems.
Assist with the administration of online professional development services, including subscriptions, agreements and customer queries.
Provide day-to-day finance administration, including invoicing, purchase and sales ledgers, reconciliations and credit control.
Support surveys, data collection and reporting following events and activities.
Opportunity to get involved in HR administration, staff training and internal team events.

Desired Background

Previous experience in an administration, operations, project coordination or events support role.
Experience managing multiple projects, activities or priorities simultaneously.
Strong IT skills, particularly Microsoft Office, Excel, Teams and Zoom.
Experience using CRM systems and finance software such as Xero would be advantageous.
Experience supporting training, learning and development or events would be beneficial.
A background in education, professional services or the not-for-profit sector would be welcomed but is not essential.

About You

Exceptionally organised with excellent attention to detail.
A confident, friendly communicator who enjoys building relationships.
Proactive, adaptable and comfortable juggling a variety of responsibilities.
A natural team player who is equally happy working independently.
Customer-focused with a genuine desire to deliver a great service.
Someone who enjoys learning new skills, taking ownership and getting stuck in!
Happy to travel occasionally within the UK to support events and meetings.

The Environment
You'll be joining a welcoming, purpose-driven organisation where collaboration, professional development and making a positive impact are genuinely valued.
Flexible working arrangements will be considered, making this a great opportunity for someone looking for a role that offers both variety and a healthy work-life balance.

How to Apply
If this sounds like the kind of role where you'd thrive, we'd love to hear from you. Apply directly or reach out to Darling Moo Talent for a warm, honest conversation about the opportunity.
