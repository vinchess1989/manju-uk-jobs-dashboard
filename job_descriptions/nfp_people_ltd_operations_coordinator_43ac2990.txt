Operations Coordinator

Join an international environmental charity as an Operations Coordinator, helping a busy remote team stay organised and making everyday processes simpler.

Position: Operations Coordinator
Salary: £30,000 per annum full-time or £24,000 per annum for 30 hours
Location: Fully remote within the UK, with occasional in-person team meetings
Hours: 37.5 hours per week, with a 30-hour option (0.8 FTE)
Contract: Permanent
Closing date: 7 December 2026. The vacancy may close early, so early applications are encouraged.

About the role

As Operations Coordinator you will report to the Head of People and work closely with the finance team, you’ll be a first point of contact for colleagues with day-to-day operational questions. The role is varied, one day you might be setting up IT access for a new starter, and the next you could be checking expenses, booking international travel or gathering data for the charity’s annual carbon footprint report.

Your key responsibilities will include:

Coordinating recruitment administration, onboarding, offboarding and training, and keeping employee and compliance records up to date.
Checking and processing expenses, coordinating purchases and flagging potential overspends.
Booking UK and international travel, finding cost-effective, lower-carbon options and tracking costs and emissions data.
Supporting digital tools such as Google Workspace and Slack, and liaising with the external IT provider on equipment and system access.
Helping organise occasional team meetings, keeping policies and trackers up to date, and finding practical ways to simplify processes.

About you

You will need to be organised, reliable and comfortable managing competing priorities independently in a remote team. You will have a good eye for detail, communicate clearly with colleagues and suppliers, and handle confidential information with discretion.

You’ll bring practical experience with Google Workspace and Microsoft Office, confidence with numbers and spreadsheets, and sound judgement about when to act and when to ask for support. You’ll also share the charity’s commitment to equity, diversity, inclusion and safeguarding.

Experience in administration, HR, finance or operations would be helpful. Formal HR or finance qualifications are optional.

Applicants must already have the right to work in the UK. Visa sponsorship is unavailable for this role.

About the organisation

This environmental charity works with Indigenous Peoples and local communities to protect rainforests. It supports communities with funding and data so they can make decisions about how to protect their land.

Benefits include 25 days’ annual leave plus bank holidays, rising to 30 days with service and pro rata for part-time hours, a £1,000 annual training budget, two paid volunteering days and enhanced family and medical leave.

Other roles you may have experience of could include Operations Administrator, Operations Assistant, Business Support Coordinator, Business Support Officer, People Operations Coordinator, HR and Operations Assistant or Office Coordinator. #INDNFP

Please note this role is advertised by the recruitment agency acting for the client - Not For Profit People.
