Legal PA – Cambridge

Successful international law firm seeks a Legal PA to work in their growing Cambridge office, the firm offers exceptional benefits, agile working, and a great collaborative environment. The Legal PA role provides comprehensive support to fee earners, ensuring smooth operations through effective client/matter management, document management, diary coordination, business development, finance support, and general assistance.

What’s on Offer:

Competitive salary
Hybrid working (3 days in the office / 2 days remote)
Outstanding benefits & fantastic culture
9.30am to 5.30pm

Legal PA – What you will be doing:

Manage fee earners' schedules; coordinate and arrange meetings to best utilise time
Ensure that calls to the switchboard are answered and directed in a prompt, efficient and professional manner
Acting as ‘front of house’, welcoming clients
Arrange travel effectively and efficiently
Carry out advanced research / request information from relevant support teams
Attend meetings / conference calls and take minutes to action follow up where required
Develop and maintain knowledge of fee earners’ workload
Prepare and manage billing process using the Aderant front office software

Legal PA- What we are looking for:

Previous Legal PA / Secretary experience or Legal Administration experience
Fast, accurate typing skills
Highly skilled on all MS Office applications
Ability to be flexible to meet the demands of the work
