Job Title: Client Operations Control Executive

Location: UK Remote – With travel to meetings in Milton Keynes as and when required.

Salary: £26,500

Contract Type: Permanent

About The Role –

This is an exciting opportunity to join our Control function in a pivotal support role. Working closely with the Control Manager, you'll provide administrative and operational support across Client Operations and Sales, ensuring activities are delivered accurately, efficiently, and to a high standard.

You'll also play an important part in client engagement, helping to build positive relationships and deliver a consistent, professional service.

What You’ll Be Doing –

Provide day-to-day administrative and operational support to the Control Manager.

Support the coordination and management of requests from the Sales team, ensuring they are accurately recorded and progressed.

Assist with the planning and coordination of client engagement visits and related activity.

Make outbound calls to clients where required, providing a professional and consistent client experience.

Support the Control Manager with daily performance monitoring, reporting and data gathering.

Identify and escalate issues, trends or concerns to the Control Manager where appropriate.

Support the delivery of process improvements, identifying opportunities to improve efficiency and ways of working.

What Success Looks Like –

Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the confidence to communicate effectively with a range of audiences.

Intermediate knowledge of Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook.

Experience of administrative or coordination-based work would be advantageous.

Experience in a customer service, sales or client-facing environment.

A desire to learn and enhance your skills.

So, what’s in it for you?


Minimum of 25 day holiday rising with service and option to purchase more
Private medical insurance and practical health plan
Free travel insurance
Enhance maternity and paternity leave
Online GP
