Job Description

Are you ready to help ensure the effective delivery of planned maintenance services.

The Role

Working within our Delivery Assurance Team, you will assist with the development and maintenance of comprehensive contract and asset information to support the effective delivery of planned maintenance services.

You will be responsible for maintaining accurate and up-to-date records, reviewing and verifying work programmes, and ensuring that our property-based data management systems accurately reflect completed works.

The role involves responding to enquiries, liaising with contractors and key stakeholders, and carrying out a range of general business support tasks that contribute to the successful delivery of the service.

About You

Our ideal candidate will have strong interpersonal and communication skills, together with practical experience of working to deadlines in a busy environment.

You will be able to demonstrate experience in data collection, input, analysis, and interpretation, with the ability to work accurately and efficiently when handling information. With excellent attention to detail, you will possess good written and numerical skills, alongside an understanding of the basic technical terminology used within the building maintenance and construction sectors.

You will enjoy working as part of a team and thrive in a fast-paced environment where no two days are the same. If you have the skills, knowledge, and experience we are looking for, we will welcome your application. This is an excellent opportunity to play an important role in supporting the delivery of planned maintenance programmes. You'll work with a range of stakeholders, contribute to high-quality services, and help ensure that accurate asset information supports effective decision-making and service delivery.

Hours: This is a full-time role, for 37 hours per week.

Although the role is advertised as full time, if you believe you have the skills and experience to join us and would like to discuss different working patterns, please do get in touch.

Working Pattern: Monday to Friday

Contract: Permanent x 2

Salary: £30,515 - £33,119 per annum (Band 3) pro rata for part time.

Location: This is flexible – working in Cambridge on site or at one of our offices or working from home. You will be able to balance your work location accordingly.
