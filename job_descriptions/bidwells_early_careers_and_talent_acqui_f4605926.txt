Head of Talent Acquisition - Bidwells - 🏡 We’re Hiring Property Professionals across the UK 🏡



Are you passionate about developing future talent and looking for an opportunity where you can really make your mark?




If so, we're looking for an Early Careers & Talent Specialist to lead and develop our Early Careers offering at Bidwells, shaping how we attract and engage the next generation of talent.




From graduates and apprentices to work experience and placements, you’ll take ownership of our Early Careers activity, build relationships with schools, colleges and universities, and create new and engaging ways to showcase the opportunities available within real estate.




You’ll also be part of our wider Talent team, supporting the recruitment of experienced professionals and partnering with the business to attract great people to Bidwells.




Whether you’re currently working in Early Careers, Graduate Recruitment or Talent Acquisition, this is an opportunity to take the next step, bring your own ideas and genuinely shape our approach.




With plenty of scope to develop and progress, you’ll have the opportunity to grow your career while playing an important part in Bidwells’ exciting growth plans.




What you'll be doing

Leading our Early Careers strategy

We want our Early Careers offering to be about more than filling graduate vacancies.

You’ll help us create meaningful opportunities for people to experience Bidwells and the property industry earlier in their career. That could mean developing relationships with new universities, creating insight days, expanding work experience or finding new ways to reach people who may never previously have considered a career in property.

You’ll also support our commitment to creating more accessible routes into the profession through apprenticeships and degree apprenticeships. Bidwells has an ambition for apprentices to represent 6% of the business by 2030, making this a genuinely important area of focus for our future growth.




Duties:

Lead our annual graduate and apprenticeship recruitment campaigns, from attraction through to offer.
Work with teams across Bidwells to identify future graduate, apprentice and placement requirements.
Build strong relationships with universities, colleges, schools, careers services and community interest companies.
Represent Bidwells at careers fairs, university events, insight days and industry events.
Develop, source and organise our work experience, summer placement and industrial placement programmes.
Build stronger pathways from work experience and placements into apprenticeships and graduate opportunities.
Create engaging campaigns and content that bring Early Careers at Bidwells to life.
Explore new partnerships and initiatives that help us reach a broader and more diverse pool of talent. This will include maintaining ongoing partnerships that support NEET individuals.




Supporting the wider Talent team

While Early Careers will be your main focus, you’ll also be part of our wider Talent function and will support experienced professionals’ recruitment when required.

This could include:

Supporting recruitment campaigns across the business.
Direct sourcing and candidate attraction.
Screening and interviewing candidates.
Advising hiring managers on recruitment and selection.
Creating engaging adverts and attraction campaigns.
Helping us continually improve candidate experience and inclusive recruitment practices.




What we're looking for

We’d love to hear from you if you have:

Experience in Early Careers or in-house Talent Acquisition (real estate or professional services experience advantageous).
A genuine passion for developing and supporting people at the start of their careers.
Experience building relationships with universities, schools, colleges and other Early Careers partners.
Confidence managing recruitment campaigns, events and multiple priorities.
Strong communication, stakeholder management and organisational skills.
Strong candidate searching ability using Linkedin recruiter and extensive screening and interviewing experience.
A creative approach to candidate attraction, employer branding and digital/social media.
A proactive mindset, with fresh ideas for developing our Early Careers offering.
A commitment to inclusive recruitment and widening access to careers within our industry.




Why Bidwells?

We’re a different kind of business. Whilst we’re a major player in the property sector, Bidwells remains a privately-owned, independent Partnership, with an inclusive, entrepreneurial culture. We give our people autonomy and take the time to listen. As a certified B Corporation (B-Corp), we are dedicated to using our business as a force for good, driving innovation, and creating long-term value for our clients, communities, and the environment.




What’s in it for you

Competitive Salary: We recognise and reward talent with generous salary and group bonus scheme. As a Real Living Wage employer, we’re committed to fair pay that reflects the true cost of living.

Comprehensive Benefits: Including up to 8% matched pension, 28 days holiday (+ bank holidays) as a minimum, along with your birthday off work, a wellness day, paid sabbaticals, private medical cover, medicash plan, lifestyle discounts and perks, and more.

Professional Development: Continuous learning, study support and promotion opportunities.

Innovative Culture: Be part of a forward-thinking team at the forefront of industry trends.

Family Friendly: We offer enhanced family leave policies to support individuals close to you.

Work-Life Balance: We value your well-being and offer agile working to support it.




Bidwells is dedicated to fostering a truly inclusive culture of belonging, where everyone can thrive and succeed as their true and authentic selves at every level. We are committed to equal employment opportunity and positively encourage applications from all suitably qualified and eligible candidates regardless of race, sex, gender, gender identity or expression, disability, age, sexual orientation, transgender status, religion or belief. Flexible working requests are considered from day one.




Recruitment agencies: Bidwells only pay agency fees where we have a signed agreement in place and that agency has been contacted and directed by a member of our Talent Acquisition Team to work on a specific vacancy. We do not pay agency fees when speculative and unsolicited CVs are submitted to Bidwells or any of our employees other than via our Applicant Tracking System and through our recruitment process. If this is not adhered to, agency fees will not be paid. Submission of any unsolicited CVs or proposals to Bidwells will be deemed evidence of full and unlimited acceptance of this approach.
