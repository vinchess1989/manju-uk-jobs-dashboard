Biologics Administrator - Dermatology

The closing date is 16 November 2026

Apply for this job
Job summary

To provide biologics prescription database administration support ensuring high quality and accurate database management. Assisting Consultants, Specialist Nurses and Pharmacists and liaising with patients, to maintain accurate and timely database entry and developing a working knowledge of all the software packages used within the services.

To coordinate the collation of data for audit and research purposes, maintaining a high standard of data quality within the service that includes providing standard reports as required. The Biologics service within the Dermatology Department is supported by a Clinical Lead and 2 Biologics nurses who require administration support to ensure smooth running of the Biologics Service within Dermatology. Including setting up and maintaining clinics using trust systems and ensuring leaves are actioned with any concerns or gaps escalated appropriately.

Supported by the Admin and Pathways Coordinator, the post holder will develop the biologics service, which will lead to good booking practice and the provision of an excellent support mechanism for both patients and clinical teams.

Main duties of the job

Provide a comprehensive administrative service to the Dermatology service including participation in a variety of projects as required to aid service developments and/or redesigns.
Support service meetings, arrange venues, prepare agendas, compile and distribute minutes and notes of meeting.
Act as a point of contact for attendees, accepting and noting apologies, collation of information for discussion, co-ordination of equipment and hospitality as delegated. Book venues, organise equipment and hospitality as required.
Prepare relevant documentation and reports for the MDT and other meetings.
Post holder is required to deal with incoming calls/messages to the Biologics helpline.
Deal with patient queries where appropriate or ensure the query is dealt with by the appropriate team member.
Keep up to date all Biologic databases that contain patient information; ensuring all information is correct and recorded accurately.

About us

We are committed to supporting colleagues through organisational change. Priority will be given to staff at risk within North West Anglia NHS Foundation Trust and Cambridgeshire & Peterborough Integrated Care Board.
