About the Role

We are looking for a dynamic, enthusiastic and customer-focused individual to join our growing team within Customer Services.

This is an exciting opportunity for someone who enjoys helping customers, has a keen interest in telecommunications and technology, and is looking to develop their skills and experience within a growing business.

The successful candidate will play an important role in supporting our customers with their day-to-day mobile, IoT and internet connectivity requirements. They will be responsible for providing first-line support, assisting with new service setups and ensuring customer enquiries are handled efficiently and professionally.

Previous experience in telecommunications, customer services or general administration would be advantageous, although a positive attitude and willingness to learn are equally important.

Strong organisational skills, consistency, attention to detail and a proactive approach will be essential to success in this role.




Key Responsibilities

Customer Support

Provide day-to-day first-line support for customer mobile phone SIM services across O2, Vodafone, EE and Three.
Assist customers with queries relating to IoT connectivity and internet services.
Respond to customer enquiries professionally, efficiently and in a timely manner.
Investigate basic service issues and work towards effective resolutions.
Escalate more complex technical issues where appropriate.
Maintain a high standard of customer service and customer satisfaction.

Service Setup and Administration

Set up and activate new mobile phone SIM connections.
Arrange and manage the setup of new IoT connections.
Coordinate the setup of new internet services.
Ensure customer and service information is accurately recorded and maintained.
Carry out general administrative duties to support the smooth running of the Customer Services department.
Manage daily workloads and priorities to ensure tasks are completed consistently and within required timescales.

Skills, Experience and Qualifications

Essential Skills and Attributes

Excellent organisational skills with strong attention to detail.
Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities effectively.
A professional, friendly and customer-focused approach.
Good verbal and written communication skills.
Ability to work independently and use initiative.
A positive attitude and willingness to learn new systems and technologies.
Reliable, consistent and able to work effectively in a fast-paced environment.
A strong team player with a flexible and proactive approach.

Desirable Experience

Previous experience in telecommunications, mobile phone services or internet connectivity.
Understanding of mobile phone SIM technology and network services.
Knowledge or experience of IoT connectivity.
Previous customer service or general administration experience.
Experience using Microsoft Office applications, including Teams, Excel, Word and PowerPoint.

Personal Attributes

The ideal candidate will be:

Enthusiastic and passionate about delivering excellent customer service.
Well organised, methodical and consistent in their approach.
Approachable, helpful and confident when communicating with customers.
Adaptable and comfortable learning new technologies and processes.
Proactive, dependable and able to take responsibility for their workload.

Salary and Benefits

Competitive salary of £26,000–£30,000 per annum, dependent on age and experience.
Additional bonus and commission opportunities.
Opportunities for career development and progression within the company.
Company pension scheme.
On-site parking.

Working Arrangements

Full-time, permanent position.
Monday to Friday, 8:30am–5:00pm.
No weekend working.
Based at our Cambridgeshire location, PE29 6XU.

Why Join Us?

If you are passionate about customer service, interested in telecommunications and technology, and keen to develop your knowledge and experience within an exciting and growing company, we would love to hear from you.

We offer an opportunity to become part of a supportive team, develop valuable industry knowledge and build a rewarding career with opportunities for future progression.

If you are organised, enthusiastic and customer-focused, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity within our Customer Services team.
