BOLD Magazine

Independent Fashion, Lifestyle, Culture & Technology Publication

About Us

BOLD Magazine is an independent digital publication covering fashion, lifestyle, culture, and technology. We spotlight emerging voices, innovative ideas, and forward-thinking creativity from around the world.

We are growing and looking for a highly organized, detail-oriented Virtual Assistant to support our editorial and operational team.

Role Overview

The Virtual Assistant will provide administrative, editorial, and digital support to ensure smooth day-to-day operations. This role is ideal for someone who is proactive, tech-savvy, and passionate about media, fashion, and culture.

This is a remote, flexible role with growth potential.

Key Responsibilities

Editorial Support


Manage and update the content calendar
Coordinate with freelance writers and contributors
Schedule interviews and follow-ups
Upload and format articles in CMS (e.g., WordPress)
Proofread basic content before publication


Administrative Support


Manage email inbox and organize communications
Maintain Google Drive/Notion systems
Prepare meeting notes and summaries
Track invoices and contributor payments


Social Media Assistance


Schedule posts across social platforms
Assist with captions and hashtags
Monitor comments and messages
Track engagement analytics


Business Support


Research potential brand partnerships
Assist in outreach emails
Maintain media contact databases


Requirements


1+ year experience as a Virtual Assistant, Admin Assistant, or similar role
Excellent written and verbal English
Strong organizational and time-management skills
Experience with:
Google Workspace
Canva
Social media platforms
WordPress or similar CMS
Ability to work independently and meet deadlines

Bonus


Background in fashion, media, marketing, or digital publishing
Basic SEO knowledge
