Project Administrator

Posted just now by Coyles

CB2, Cambridge, Cambridgeshire
£21 per hour
Contract
Easy Apply
Apply Now

One of my clients are looking for a Project Admminsitrator. This is a 6 months contract with possible further extension. 

Must have previous experience working on projects.

Assist with the production of specifications and project documents in accordance with the organising Standing Orders, best practise, and all relevant standards.
Assist with and lead in some areas of tenant and leaseholder engagement throughout duration of retrofit projects, helping to ensure excellent customer service. This may include a variety of communications such as writing letters and producing fliers, organising access for surveys and home visits, telephone calls, emails, helping to organise in person and online engagement events. 
Support the preparation and issue of tender documents. 
Assist with preparation and submission of planning and Building Regulation applications. 
Provide monthly reporting data for funded projects, taking a lead in appropriate areas. 
Administrative support with the gathering of pre and post EPC data from Domestic Energy Assessors. 
Assist with preparation of bid or grant applications. 
Carry out any other duties as required by your line manager from time to time in accordance with the grading of the post.

If interested in this role, please send your cv to Jahker Miah from Coyle Personnel
