About Us

Qureight's mission is to accelerate clinical trials and ensure breakthroughs in lung and heart disease reach patients without delay. Our AI-powered data and imaging curation platform enables the analysis of clinical imaging and other healthcare data, helping our customers bring treatments to market, faster.

We're looking for talented people who want their work to matter. With offices in Cambridge and London, you'll join our multidisciplinary team of clinicians, scientists and engineers. What unites us is our open culture, continuous learning mindset and a shared mission to help biopharma run faster, smarter trials.

Our Commercial team works with pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies and clinical research organisations (CROs) around the world. We identify where Qureight's imaging, data and AI capabilities can support clinical development and help customers run better trials.

About The Role

We are looking for a detail-oriented and commercially minded Commercial Associate to support Qureight's continued growth.

Working closely with the Proposals Manager, you will help prepare high-quality proposals, budgets and RFx responses for pharmaceutical, biotechnology and CRO customers. You will coordinate input from colleagues across clinical, imaging, data science, operations and quality teams, and make sure every submission is accurate, compliant, on time and clearly shows Qureight's value.

You will also help maintain our proposal content, templates and pricing tools, track opportunities through the commercial pipeline and support the Business Development team when needed.

Around half of your time will be spent on proposals. The rest will be split roughly equally between (i) inside sales, where you will research target accounts, identify decision-makers and help qualify new opportunities, and (ii) marketing, where you will support our Head of Marketing with targeted campaigns, events and content.

This is a great opportunity for someone early in their commercial career who wants to build expertise in proposals and bid management, alongside business development and marketing, within the life sciences industry.

What You Will Do

Proposals (around 50%)


Support the preparation of proposals, quotes and RFP/RFI responses.
Review customer requests, including protocols, synopses and RFP specifications, to extract key requirements, specifications, nuances and deadlines.
Build and check proposal budgets using Qureight's pricing models, making sure assumptions are accurate and consistent with the scope.
Coordinate input from subject matter experts across clinical, imaging, data science, operations, QA and regulatory teams.
Manage proposal timelines, trackers and version control so submissions are delivered on time.
Proofread and format proposals so they are clear, consistent, compliant and on brand.
Maintain and improve the proposal content library, templates, boilerplate and case studies.
Support budget revisions, re-bids, change orders and contract handover documents such as work orders.
Maintain accurate, timely proposal and opportunity records in Qureight's CRM, and help report on proposal metrics.
Develop a strong understanding of Qureight's services, pricing and value proposition.


Inside sales (around 25%)


Research pharmaceutical and biotechnology companies, clinical trials and CRO partners to identify new business opportunities.
Identify relevant customer stakeholders and decision-makers, and support outreach through email and LinkedIn.
Qualify leads and support their progression through the commercial pipeline.
Support the Business Development team with account research, meeting preparation and follow-up.


Marketing (around 25%)


Help keep marketing data in the CRM accurate and up to date, including tracking lead sources and campaign performance.
Spot proposal wins and customer successes that could make good case studies and help pull them together.
Share useful insights from proposals and outreach with Marketing, such as common customer questions and competitor information.
Support the Head of Marketing with targeted campaigns, LinkedIn and email content, and conferences and events, including following up leads.


Requirements

What we need


A degree or equivalent experience in life sciences, business, marketing or another relevant discipline.
An interest in business development and marketing, and in how campaigns and outreach turn into new business.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain technical information clearly and persuasively.
Exceptional attention to detail, both in writing and numbers.
Confidence in working with Microsoft applications.
Strong organisation and the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.
A proactive approach and willingness to take ownership of tasks.
Ability to work effectively in a fast-moving, collaborative environment.
Ability to work collaboratively with colleagues across different functions and levels of seniority.


Even better if


You have experience in proposals, bid management, contracts or another commercial role.
You have worked within pharmaceutical, biotechnology, CRO or another life sciences organisation.
You have researched clinical trials or pharmaceutical development pipelines.
You have built or reviewed commercial budgets, quotations or pricing models.
You have experience in inside sales, lead generation or B2B marketing, ideally in life sciences.
You have used marketing or social media tools such as HubSpot or LinkedIn Campaign Manager.
You have experience using/developing CRMs.
You understand clinical trial design, phases and documentation
