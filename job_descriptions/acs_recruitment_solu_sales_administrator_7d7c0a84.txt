Sales Administrator

Location: Huntingdon

Salary: £28,000 per annum

Hours: Monday – Friday, 8:30am – 5:00pm

Job Type: Full-time, Permanent



Are you an organised, customer-focused individual who enjoys working in a busy, fast-paced environment? Do you have strong administration skills and a knack for delivering excellent customer service?



We are recruiting for a Sales Administrator to join a friendly and supportive team based in Huntingdon.



This is a fantastic opportunity for someone who enjoys variety in their working day, takes pride in getting things right first time and loves building positive relationships with customers.



What You'll Be Doing


Processing customer orders accurately and efficiently, from initial enquiry through to completion.
Handling incoming customer enquiries via telephone and email, providing a professional and helpful service.
Supporting the sales team with day-to-day administration and order processing.
Keeping customers updated on their orders and responding to any queries or issues.
Maintaining accurate customer records and updating internal systems.
Liaising with colleagues across the business to ensure orders are processed smoothly.
Providing general administrative support to help keep the department running efficiently.


What We're Looking For


Previous experience in an administration or customer service role.
Ideally, some experience within sales administration, sales support or order processing.
Excellent communication skills and a confident, friendly telephone manner.
Strong organisational skills with excellent attention to detail.
Good IT skills and confidence using Microsoft Office and internal systems.
The ability to manage multiple tasks and prioritise workloads effectively.
A positive, proactive attitude and a genuine passion for helping customers.




What's on Offer?


£28,000 annual salary
Monday to Friday working hours – no weekends!
Permanent, full-time opportunity.
A varied role combining customer service, administration and sales support.
The opportunity to become a valued member of a supportive team.


Interested?

If you're an experienced administrator or customer service professional looking for your next opportunity in Huntingdon, we'd love to hear from you!



Apply today to find out more about this exciting opportunity.
