Our Incora Story is really taking off – we'd love you to join us for the journey!

We are currently recruiting for a Customer Account Coordinator to join us at our customer site in Cambridge.

Salary: £28,000 anually

As the leading supply chain solutions business in the aerospace industry, customers are at the heart of everything we do.

As a Customer Account Coordinator, you will provide first-class customer service while ensuring customer needs are understood, managed and supported effectively. You will be the key point of contact for customer enquiries, helping to build strong relationships and deliver an excellent customer experience.

Responsibilities


Provide excellent customer service and support to customers on-site.
Build and maintain positive relationships with customers and internal stakeholders.
Manage customer orders and maintain accurate records within company systems.
Process transactions and update information in internal databases.
Monitor and respond to customer enquiries and emails in a timely manner.
Support day-to-day administrative activities and ensure accurate documentation.
Coordinate with internal teams to resolve customer queries and provide effective solutions.
Ensure customer requirements are met while maintaining a high level of service.


Requirements


Previous experience in an administration, customer service, coordination or support role.
Driver’s licence.
Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.
A proactive approach and strong attention to detail.
Good IT skills, including Microsoft Office applications such as Outlook, Excel and Word.
A DBS check will be required as part of the pre-employment screening process.


About Us

Incora™ is a leading, global provider of innovative supply chain solutions. Our promise to our customers is to reduce complexity and increase reliability so they can deliver mission-critical solutions that improve our world. We exist to enable the mission critical.

With a strong foundation in the aerospace and defence markets, we manage all aspects of supply chain from procurement to warehouse management, from logistics to on-site customer services. We are expanding our team of skilled Engineers to ensure right-first-time every time.
