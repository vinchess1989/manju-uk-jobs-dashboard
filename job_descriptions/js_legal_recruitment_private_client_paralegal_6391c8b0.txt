Private Client Paralegal

JS legal Recruitment is working with a multi office firm of Solicitors , who is looking for a talented Private Client Paralegal to join their busy Private Client Department.

If you’re a Private Client Paralegal with experience in Wills, Trusts, Estates, Tax planning, Deputyship and LPAs, IHT matters, then we would like to hear from you.

The Role

You will assist with a varied caseload of Private Client Matters.
To include drafting legal documents, including Wills, Lasting Powers of Attorney, and HMRC Inheritance Tax forms
Court of Protection, Probate and the Administration of Estates and Trusts.
Tax Planning, IHT matters, Estate Planning, Trusts
Elderly client matters  
Costing the court of protection files
Preparing Estate Accounts
Dealing with client queries

The candidate

The successful candidate will ideally possess a minimum of 1 year of Private Client Paralegal experience
Experience dealing with a broad caseload of Private Client matters
Excellent organisational skills
Working knowledge of case management systems and Microsoft Office Applications
A strong team player
Able to work on your own initiative
An excellent listener with a sympathetic ear
Long-term career progression
A friendly and supportive working environment

Benefits

Pension
Parking
Monday to Friday 9am – 5pm
22 days annual leave, plus Bank Holidays and Christmas closure
Friendly working atmosphere
