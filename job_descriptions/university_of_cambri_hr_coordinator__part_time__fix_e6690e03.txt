The Department of Clinical Neurosciences is seeking to appoint an experienced and highly organised HR Coordinator to support the delivery of a professional and responsive HR service. This is an excellent opportunity for an HR professional who enjoys working in a complex and varied environment, supporting a diverse community of academic, research, clinical and professional services staff.

Reporting to the Senior HR Coordinator, the postholder will play a central role in the administration and coordination of the full employee lifecycle. Acting as a key point of contact for managers and staff, you will provide clear and accurate advice on a broad range of HR matters, including terms and conditions of employment, annual leave, sickness absence, family leave provisions, employee wellbeing, disciplinary and grievance procedures, and other employment relations matters. You will work closely with colleagues across the Department and University, escalating complex cases where appropriate and ensuring that guidance is provided in accordance with University policies and procedures. The successful candidate will have excellent attention to detail and strong, clear communication skills, with a proactive approach to their work. They will be confident in identifying gaps, inconsistencies and opportunities for improvement within existing HR processes and will work closely with the Senior HR Coordinator to suggest and implement practical process improvements. They will be comfortable taking initiative, raising potential issues and contributing ideas that help make HR processes more efficient, consistent and effective.

A key part of the role will involve coordinating recruitment activity for academic, research and professional services posts. You will support managers through all stages of recruitment, including drafting role descriptions, advertising vacancies and administering pre-employment checks. You will also assist with the recruitment of senior and clinical appointments.

The postholder will administer probation, appraisal, payroll and contract-related processes, maintain accurate HR records and systems, coordinate visitor administration, and support contract-end and leaver procedures. You will also prepare routine HR reports, contribute to process improvements and provide administrative support, including organising meetings, diary management and preparing confidential correspondence.

We are looking for candidates with experience working in an HR administrative environment and providing HR advice to managers and employees. Experience of managing multiple processes in a regulated environment and working with clinical contracts is essential. Applicants should demonstrate excellent organisational, communication and interpersonal skills, strong attention to detail, and confidence using Microsoft Office applications. A sound understanding of HR administration and employment processes is required.

This is an excellent opportunity to join a world-leading department and contribute to an environment that supports internationally recognised research, education and clinical excellence.

The postholder will work part-time at a minimum of 0.4FTE, and a maximum of 0.6FTE. The working days for this position are to be Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

Fixed-term: The funds for this post are available for 18 months in the first instance.

For questions relating to the post, please contact Georgia Marceau at gm762@cam.ac.uk
