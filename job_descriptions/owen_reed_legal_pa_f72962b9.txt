Full job description

Owen Reed is looking for a proactive and organised Legal PA to join the Private department of a law firm in Cambridge.

Working as part of the Legal Operations team, you will provide timely, high-quality administrative and coordination support to a group of lawyers. This varied role includes client contact, matter management, billing, diary management and travel coordination, working within a friendly and high-performing team.

The successful candidate will be self-motivated and reliable, with the ability to manage their workload, plan ahead and adapt to changing priorities while consistently delivering accurate work to deadlines.

Key Responsibilities

Matter Management

Support fee earners and Senior Legal PAs throughout the client and matter lifecycle, following documented procedures.
Work closely with client onboarding and compliance specialists to ensure all required information and documentation is obtained, issued and correctly stored.
Support compliance with policies covering new matters, retainer letters, conflict checks, searches, Client Due Diligence and Know Your Client requirements.
Maintain electronic and hard-copy documents in accordance with the firm’s filing protocols.
Ensure inactive matters are closed, client funds are returned and files are archived promptly.

Financial Management & Billing

Develop a strong understanding of billing processes and systems to support the preparation of accurate bills and financial information.
Ensure matters are set up with the correct rates, discounts and billing details, and that Money on Account is collected or files are recorded on the exempt list.
Help monitor and address unpaid bills, liaising with Credit Control where required.
Support the accurate and timely processing of disbursement invoices and payments.

Fee Earner Support

Act as a first point of contact for assigned fee earners, handling calls, emails, correspondence and meeting requests, responding on their behalf where appropriate.
Manage calendars across multiple time zones, scheduling calls and meetings and following up as needed.
Assist with expense claims and other business-related requirements.

Travel Coordination

Manage travel arrangements through the firm’s in-house travel provider.
Prepare itineraries and ensure business expenses are captured, approved and processed in accordance with policy.

Business Development

Organise business breakfasts, lunches and dinners, including associated expenses.
Record referrals and contacts in InterAction and maintain CRM lists.

Delegation & Coordination

Work with Business Administrators to coordinate filing, archiving, scanning, photocopying, contact updates and courier arrangements.
Delegate document formatting, production, typing, amendments, house styling and proofreading to the Document Production Unit as appropriate.
Liaise with Legal Support Services, the Global Capability Centre and other departments to obtain specialist input or delegate tasks, retaining ownership and following up where required.

Team Support & Collaboration

Contribute to a cohesive working environment and provide cover for Senior Legal PAs and other Legal Operations colleagues.
Work flexibly across Legal Operations teams to meet business needs.
Take responsibility for your workload, communicate working arrangements clearly and follow through on commitments.
Demonstrate the firm’s core values and support its Equity, Diversity and Inclusion strategy.
Develop a focus area or specialism to build skills and contribute to service improvement, innovation and engagement across Legal Support Services.

Skills & Experience

Previous administrative experience supporting a team within a similar professional services environment, ideally legal.
Strong time management skills, with the ability to balance multiple tasks, plan ahead and respond positively to changing requirements.
Excellent Microsoft Office skills and a willingness to learn new systems.
Exceptional attention to detail and the ability to maintain accuracy under pressure.
An organised, methodical approach and confidence working within structured processes and compliance frameworks.
Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work effectively with different people and working styles.
A collaborative approach, with the flexibility to support additional fee earners and wider team requirements.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills, including drafting professional business correspondence.
Discretion and sound judgement when handling sensitive or confidential information.
