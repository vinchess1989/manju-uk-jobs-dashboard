Operations Support

Location: Peterborough
Salary: £28,000 - £30,000 per annum
Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00am - 5:00pm (1-hour lunch)
Flexibility may be available for the right person, including a later start time where required.

The Opportunity

Our client is looking for an Operations Support Administrator to join their friendly, close-knit team in Peterborough.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys customer interaction, administration and learning technical products. Full training will be provided on the company's systems, products and processes, allowing you to become an integral part of the business.

Initially, your focus will be on learning the systems, products and procedures while supporting the team with administration and customer service tasks. As your knowledge grows, you will become a key point of contact for customers, supporting quotations, certification, order processing and operational administration.

This role would suit someone who is organised, personable, eager to learn and enjoys building customer relationships through excellent service rather than hard sales.

Key Responsibilities

Respond to customer enquiries via phone and email
Chase quotations and follow up with customers
Process customer orders and bookings
Create certificates and customer documentation
Maintain accurate records on internal systems
Learn product ranges and technical information
Support customers with product enquiries
Assist with general office administration
Work closely with colleagues to deliver excellent customer service
Support operational activities as required

About You

We're looking for someone who:

Has excellent communication and telephone skills
Is confident using computers and learning new systems
Has strong written and spoken English
Has a good typing speed and accuracy
Is organised and able to manage multiple tasks
Has a positive attitude and sense of humour
Enjoys working in a small team
Is willing to learn and develop
Takes pride in delivering excellent customer service
Has a proactive and helpful approach

Previous experience in administration, customer service, sales support or operations would be beneficial, but attitude and willingness to learn are more important than industry experience.

Training & Development

Full training will be provided, with opportunities to develop your skills, gain industry knowledge and build a long-term career within the business.

Benefits

£28,000 - £30,000 salary
3-month probation period
Flexible start times may be considered
Private healthcare after 12 months' service
20 days annual leave plus bank holidays
Additional paid Christmas shutdown
Fully equipped staff kitchen
Regular company social events
Friendly, supportive working environment
Genuine long-term career prospects

Additional Information

Earliest anticipated start date: 30th November
Ideally suited to candidates living within a 30-minute commute of Peterborough
Stable business offering long-term career opportunities
