Legal Secretary | Fully Remote | Up to £44,000

A fantastic opportunity for an experienced Legal Secretary to join a highly regarded international law firm in a 100% fully remote role.

You'll support 2 Partners within a Private Wealth team, with a straightforward workload and the flexibility of working entirely from home.

What's on offer:

Fully remote - work from home 100% of the time
Up to £44,000
35-hour working week
Monday-Thursday: 12pm-8pm
Friday: 11am-7pm
25 days' holiday + option to buy/sell up to 5 days
Discretionary bonus
Private medical insurance from day one
Pension scheme

The role:

Diary and inbox management
Preparing documents and correspondence
Client and matter administration
General support to 2 Partners

This is a brilliant opportunity for someone looking for genuine work-from-home flexibility, no daily commute and the chance to work for a prestigious international law firm.
