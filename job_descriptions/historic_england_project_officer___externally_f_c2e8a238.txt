Summary




Historic England is the public organisation that brings new life to heritage to build a better future for every place and everyone.




Historic England has a fantastic opportunity for you to join us as our Project Officer – Externally Funded Projects (Maternity cover).




This is a Maternity Cover contract.




The location of this role is Cambridge, inclusive with hybrid working.




We offer a wide benefits package including a competitive defined benefit pension scheme to which we make 28.97% employer contributions, a generous 28 days holiday, corporate discounts, free entry into English Heritage sites across the country and development opportunities to ensure you achieve your goals.




To view our full range of benefits please follow the link here.




What you will be doing




As part of Historic England’s Regions Group, you will be a member of the regional Historic Places Regeneration Team, providing support in the delivery of our externally funded projects, including our Heritage at Risk Capital Fund and Places of Worship Renewal Fund, to March 2030.




This involves coordinating delivery of new Places of Worship and Heritage at Risk capital repair grant projects across your specific Historic England Region. You will support projects through liaison, initiation, development, delivery, monitoring, closure and evaluation, both internally and with external stakeholders.




Who we are looking for




Knowledge of the historic environment and experience of managing and monitoring projects whilst maintaining a focus on delivery and quality
Experience of electronic data management, analysis and presentation
Good ICT skills including experience of varied systems including Microsoft applications
A proven track record in developing positive and effective working relationships with a wide range of others
Proven experience of internal and external stakeholder engagement
