Role: Office Administrator (Temporary)

Offer: Competitive hourly rate

Location: Cambridge

Our client, based just outside Cambridge City Centre are looking
for an Office Administrator to join their professional team on a
temporary basis to assist with general administration support
tasks to assist with the smooth running of the company. The role
will be office based.

Main duties will include:
* Arranging meetings and preparing meeting rooms

* Manage email inbox

* Booking travel and accommodation for colleagues

* Liaising with suppliers and contractors

* Assisting with project coordination

* Junior finance administration

* General ad-hoc administration duties

The successful candidate will have:

* Experience within an office position

* IT proficiency

* Good numerical and literacy skills

* The ability to work well within a small team

* Attention to detail

If this role looks like the new challenge that you are looking
for, please apply via the advert or contact Megan at Eclectic
Recruitment.

We endeavour to reply to every candidate, every time but if you
haven’t heard back within 10 days, please understand that you
have unfortunately been unsuccessful for this position, or the
position has been filled. Please call the office or send an email
to discuss other potential positions.
