Who is Mick George?

What started as one man, one tipper truck, and a dream has grown into a successful business over nearly 40 years. Today, the company operates more than 600 HGV vehicles across 40+ sites and employs over 1,000 people, making it one of the leading suppliers to the construction industry across East Anglia and the East Midlands.

The company is committed to providing the highest standards of service and support to everyone it works with, including employees, customers, suppliers, and other stakeholders.

Job Purpose:

Provide admin support to all functions within the Land and Mineral Resources (LMR) team in order for the planning operations to achieve aims and objectives.

Main Responsibilities:

Assist in the development and submitting planning applications
Undertaking checks on land constraints
Producing planning applications for new sites or development on existing sites and assist with the required supporting documents
Attending meetings, taking and circulating minutes
Review reports from periodic MPA monitoring visits and prepare responses to action points
Producing presentations for meetings
Working closely with the LMR team, Senior Geologist, quarry staff, Senior Management in the Operations and Permitting teams and MD
Producing correspondence on behalf of the Planning Manager
Track consultation responses on live application and ensure responses are actioned
General administration to include: raising Purchase Orders, signing off invoices, data entry and monitoring, filing

Skills & Competencies

To be able to work under pressure and plan and prioritise workload
Excellent literacy skills and attention to detail with a high level of accuracy
Ability to work under instruction whilst using own initiative where necessary
A polite and confident telephone manner
Highly organised with the ability to work in a pressurised environment
Demonstrate best practice at all times
Excellent MS Office skills, in particular Excel and Word
Ability to be methodical and flexible in approach to tasks
A pro-active team member
Competence to work alone
Full UK Driving License
