About Us

Stone King is an ambitious and purpose-driven firm. Our collaborative culture and core values are intertwined with our ethos of doing the right thing by our clients, communities, and our people. Our passion for the sectors we work in and the purpose-driven work we do with our clients has resulted in SK being recognised as national leaders in our specialist fields across the country.

The opportunity

This is a fantastic opportunity for an individual looking to progress their career to join Stone King's highly respected Business Development & Marketing (BD&M) Team.

The role can be based in any of our six offices (Bath, Birmingham, Cambridge, Leeds, London, or Manchester) and hybrid working will be available.

To provide proactive support for business development initiatives, ensuring smooth delivery of growth plans and client engagement activities. This role focuses on event follow-up, client communications, sales enablement, and coordination between fee-earners and the marketing team, helping the firm convert opportunities and strengthen relationships.

Reasons to apply


Opportunity to gain invaluable experience across our BD&M Team, you'll have the opportunity to work closely with our specialists across the firm.
To be part of an ambitious and collaborative national firm where our culture and values are at the heart of everything we do. We believe our people are our greatest asset and strive to create the best place to work
Excellent benefits for our people, with a focus on wellbeing and development at the forefront
Hybrid working - Stone King are proud to be signatories of the Mindful Business Charter as part of our commitment to mental health and wellbeing. We want to support all of our people in achieving a healthy work/ life balance and would be happy to speak with prospective candidates about flexible working arrangements


Key Responsibilities

General duties include, but are not limited to:

Client & event follow-up


Own the follow-up process for events and webinars, ensuring timely communication, effective lead management, and accurate tracking of opportunities arising from attendee engagement.
Advise on invite data selection and audience targeting to maximise event attendance, engagement, and pipeline generation.
Support the development and progression of pipelines identifying event-generated leads.
Maintain accurate records of follow-up actions and outcomes


Fee-earner engagement


Attend meetings to capture and drive BD-related actions
Communicate growth priorities and required tasks to the wider marketing team


Sales enablement


Create and maintain materials for the sales enablement hub (eg, pitch decks, case studies, product sheets)
Ensure resources are up to date and accessible for fee earners


Client & product support


Respond to product-related enquiries promptly and professionally
Liaise with BD Manager to escalate complex queries or opportunities


Coordination & reporting


Support BD Manager with pipeline tracking and reporting
Assist in preparing summaries of BD activity for monthly reports


Requirements

Essential


Experience creating and maintaining BD collateral such as pitch decks, case studies, credentials, and product sheets
Understanding of basic BD processes such as lead follow‑up, tracking opportunities, and supporting pipeline activity
Confidence using CRM systems (InterAction preferable) to update records, log actions, and extract information for reports
Ability to capture actions from meetings accurately and ensure they are followed through to completion.
Comfortable working with data — able to review simple reports, spot inconsistencies, and pull-out key points for BD Manager updates


Desirable


Previous experience in a similar role within a professional services background or law firm


Personal Attributes


High attention to detail, with strong proofreading ability to maintain brand consistency and deliver high-quality content
Strong organisation skills, taking full ownership of tasks and ensuring delivery within agreed deadlines
Commercially aware and results-oriented, with a natural ability to recognise opportunities, think strategically, and identify ways to drive business growth and strengthen client relationships
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to convey ideas clearly
Ability to work collaboratively, consistently demonstrating alignment with the firm's core values
Detail-oriented with strong problem-solving skills
