Job description

 

About us

Betknowmore UK is a leading provider of gambling support and training services. Our mission is to deliver services that prevent and address personal and societal harms related to gambling, and to challenge the inequalities that shape people’s experiences, opportunities and outcomes. Lived experience sits at our core, from our Founder to our support team to our Management Board. Our prevention and support services are commissioned by NHS England, OHID and Public Health Scotland.

The role

As Triage and Assessments Coordinator, you will be the first point of contact for people coming into our support services. You will deliver high-quality, trauma-informed and person-centred assessments, making sure every service user is onboarded safely and quickly. Reporting to the Triage Team Manager, you will work closely with the Triage team, our wider support services and external referral partners.

Key information

Job title: Triage and Assessments Coordinator 
Location: Remote (UK-wide, with occasional travel) 
Salary: £33,000 – £35,000 FTE (depending on experience) 
Hours: Full time, 35 hours per week 
Contract: Permanent 
Closing date: Thursday 29 October 2026

What you’ll do

Carry out initial contact and comprehensive assessments, online and by phone
Develop safety plans and risk assessments in line with our model of care
Manage referrals on our database and waiting list
Provide Brief and Extended Brief Interventions where appropriate
Signpost and refer service users to further support when needed
Report and log safeguarding concerns, and bring wellbeing concerns to our weekly MDT
Keep accurate, up-to-date records and support reporting to funders and partners
Build strong relationships with referral partners and help improve how triage works

What we’re looking for

Experience delivering person-centred assessments
Experience conducting fluid risk assessments and handling complex safeguarding incidents
Experience working with diverse groups, including vulnerable or at-risk individuals
A strong understanding of safeguarding, and of recovery-based or strengths-based approaches
Strong organisational and time management skills
Good working knowledge of Microsoft Word, PowerPoint and SharePoint, and Airtable or a similar CRM
A commitment to equality, diversity, inclusion and trauma-informed practice
Willingness to travel across the UK, including occasional overnight stays

Knowledge of gambling harm, or experience in health, social care, addiction or the voluntary sector, would be an advantage.
