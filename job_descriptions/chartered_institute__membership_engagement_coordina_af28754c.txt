Help support and strengthen an active professional community

The Chartered Institute for Archaeologists (CIfA) is the leading professional body representing archaeologists working across the UK and internationally. Through our Groups, Networks and volunteers, we provide opportunities for members to connect, share expertise and contribute to the development of the profession.

We are seeking an organised and proactive Membership Engagement Coordinator to support volunteers with the delivery of member-focused activities and events and help strengthen engagement across our professional community.

This is an exciting opportunity for someone who enjoys working collaboratively, building relationships, and supporting volunteers to achieve shared goals.

About the role

Working closely with CIfA's Groups and Networks, you will support our volunteers to plan and deliver online meetings and events that provide member engagement, professional networking and continuing professional development (CPD) and contribute to CIfA's strategic objectives.

CIfA has 20 Area and Special Interest Groups and three professional networks.

The role offers considerable variety and would suit someone who enjoys managing multiple priorities while working with a wide range of stakeholders.

Key responsibilities

Support the planning, coordination and delivery of Group and Network activities.
Act as a key point of contact for volunteer committees and representatives.
Coordinate communications between Groups, Networks, CIfA staff and the wider membership and archaeological sector.
Assist with the promotion of Group and Network activities and opportunities through appropriate channels.
Support volunteer recruitment, induction and engagement.
Promote the benefits of professional networking, volunteering and CPD.

For more information please see the Job description and person specification.

Why join us?

At CIfA, you'll be joining a small team committed to strengthening professional practice and supporting the archaeological profession.
