Operations Support Administrator

22.5 hours per week

£17,021.93 per annum plus great benefits including 8% non-contributory pension and enhanced paid family leave.

Location – Remote /Centre based/Hybrid

About Us

At Fire Fighters Charity, we are here for the UK's fire services community, providing lifelong support that helps people live healthier and happier lives.

It is important we effectively utilise our capacity and our resources so that we can deliver more services to those who require our support and this role is key to us achieving that.

About the Role

This role sits within a small team and reports to the Operations Support Manager. You will play a key role in monitoring and managing service capacity, maintaining accurate oversight of occupancy levels, and coordinating all in-person and virtual appointment bookings and cancellations. Ensuring the seamless coordination of placements, maintaining real-time visibility of capacity, and supporting efficient operational processes that enable timely access to care are central to the success of this position.

Working closely with internal teams, you will build strong relationships to maximise available capacity and ensure resources are utilised effectively. You will be responsible for maintaining accurate, up-to-date data, providing real-time information that supports operational planning, identifies emerging trends, and enables informed decision-making. Through proactive coordination and attention to detail, you will help ensure a responsive, efficient, and high-quality service for the individuals we support.

About You

To be successful in this role you will have:

Proven administrative experience within a healthcare, social care, or similar service environment.
Experience using booking, scheduling, or appointment management systems. Previous experience of electronic bed management or capacity management systems would be advantageous but is not essential.
Experience maintaining accurate, confidential records and handling sensitive information in line with data protection requirements.
Strong data entry skills, with a keen eye for detail and a willingness to develop data analysis and reporting capabilities.
Experience using Customer Relationship Management (CRM) and/or Case Management systems to record, monitor, and manage service activity.
Excellent organisational skills, with the ability to prioritise workloads, manage multiple tasks, and respond effectively to changing service demands.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build positive working relationships and communicate professionally and compassionately with a range of stakeholders.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Outlook, Excel, Word, and Teams, together with confidence in using CRM and Case Management systems.

The post is subject to a disclosure check with the Disclosure and Barring Service.
