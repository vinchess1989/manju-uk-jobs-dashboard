HR Administrator

Stevenage - onsite full time for full month then hybrid working after.

12 month contract , extension possible.

21.5 Hours Per Week - (ideally across 3-4 days)

12-Month Contract

£18.00 per hour Inside of IR35




We are seeking a highly organised and proactive HR Administrator to join a busy and collaborative HR function supporting a large, complex organisation. This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced administrator who enjoys working with systems, data, and people, while delivering outstanding customer service and ensuring the smooth running of key HR processes.

Working closely with HR Business Partners and wider HR colleagues, you will play a pivotal role in supporting employee lifecycle activities, maintaining accurate HR records, managing queries, and helping to drive process improvements across the HR function.




As HR Administrator, your responsibilities will include:

Acting as a key point of contact for employee and manager HR queries.
Supporting and maintaining HR systems, including processing employee lifecycle changes.
Managing employee documentation and electronic personnel records.
Ensuring HR data is accurate, compliant, and maintained in line with data protection requirements.
Investigating and resolving data discrepancies and administrative queries.
Producing, maintaining, and analysing HR reports and data.
Supporting HR meetings through note-taking and administrative coordination.
Working collaboratively to improve processes, standardise ways of working, and share best practice.
Assisting with HR projects and continuous improvement initiatives.
Managing a varied workload while maintaining exceptional attention to detail.




What We're Looking For

Strong administrative experience within a fast-paced environment.
Experience working with HR systems, ideally Workday, although other HR platforms will be considered.
Strong Microsoft Office skills, particularly advanced Excel capabilities including data analysis and reporting.
Confidence working with large volumes of data while maintaining accuracy and confidentiality.
Excellent customer service and communication skills with the ability to support stakeholders at all levels.
Experience improving processes and identifying efficiencies.
A proactive and self-motivated approach with the ability to manage competing priorities.
CIPD qualification or previous HR administration experience would be advantageous but is not essential.
