Supplier Due Diligence, Compliance & Risk Management Analyst

Make sure to read the full description below, and please apply immediately if you are confident you meet all the requirements.


Stevenage (Hybrid Working 2-3 days a week onsite)
£30.00 per hour Umbrella
12-Month Contract (Extension Potential)


Our client, a leading organisation operating within a highly regulated and complex supply chain environment, is seeking a Supplier Due Diligence, Compliance & Risk Management Analyst to join their Procurement and Supply Chain function.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone with experience in supplier governance, compliance, procurement support, risk management, or due diligence processes who is looking to further develop their career within a fast-paced and collaborative team.

The Opportunity

As a Supplier Due Diligence Analyst, you will play a key role in supplier onboarding, compliance monitoring and risk management activities across the organisation's supplier network. Working closely with procurement teams and business stakeholders, you'll help ensure suppliers meet governance, regulatory and ethical standards while supporting critical supplier approval processes.

This position offers exposure to supplier risk assessment, compliance controls, governance frameworks and procurement systems within a highly professional environment.

Key Responsibilities

Conduct initial and ongoing supplier due diligence assessments

Review and analyse supplier financial, ethical and compliance information

Assess supplier questionnaires and supporting documentation

Request and validate supplier certifications and accreditations

Manage supplier declarations and electronic compliance campaigns

Support supplier onboarding activities and governance processes

Coordinate supplier approval committees and track actions, decisions and mitigation plans

About You

We're looking for candidates who can demonstrate:

Experience within Supplier Due Diligence, Supplier Governance, Procurement Support, Compliance, Risk Management or Supply Chain Administration

Strong analytical skills with the ability to assess risk and supplier information

Experience reviewing supplier documentation, questionnaires or compliance records
