TRAINING EVENTS LEAD – HFL1604

Contract: Permanent

Hours:_ full time at 37 hours per week, 52_ weeks per year

Salary: – FTE £30,000 - £32,000 p.a.

Location: Hybrid working consisting of remote working, flexibility required to work across event venues in Hertfordshire and neighbouring counties.This includes an opportunity to work up to 2 days per week in our Head Office in Stevenage, Hertfordshire if desired.

We're looking for an organised, proactive and commercially minded Training Events Lead to join our busy Events Team.

This is an opportunity to take ownership of a large and varied portfolio of professional learning events, from training programmes to webinars and online learning sessions. Working with colleagues across the organisation, you'll be responsible for ensuring every event is delivered professionally, efficiently and with an outstanding customer experience at its heart.

You'll work with a wide range of internal stakeholders, customers and suppliers, helping to shape learning experiences that ultimately support better outcomes for children and young people. The post holder will coordinate event delivery, project manage promotion of events and ensure value for money for both the business and the customer.

You'll join a supportive and collaborative team that values innovation, continuous improvement and sharing expertise. The role offers significant variety, genuine responsibility and the chance to develop your events management career within a well-established and respected organisation.

We are looking for someone who:

· Has experience delivering events, conferences, training programmes or similar projects.

· Thrives in a fast-paced environment and enjoys managing multiple priorities simultaneously.

· Can confidently balance competing deadlines and make effective decisions.

· Builds positive relationships with a wide range of stakeholders.

· Takes ownership, uses initiative and focuses on solutions.

· Enjoys working as part of a team and supporting colleagues to achieve shared goals.

· Has excellent organisational, communication and customer service skills.

· Is comfortable using digital systems and event management technology.

You may be an Events Graduate ready to take the next step, an Events Co-ordinator seeking greater ownership, or an experienced Events professional looking for a role that offers flexibility, variety and purpose.

Our ‘working from anywhere’ approach provides colleagues the opportunity to work flexibly in line with their role requirements. This consists of remote working, working in event venues and up to 2 days per week in our Head Office in Stevenage, Hertfordshire. The ability to reach different locations by car will be essential for this role. If you drive, then a full driving licence and appropriate car insurance will be required. As there is a permanent home working element to this role, you will need to ensure you are equipped for home working.

Benefits: a generous employer pension contribution, 28 days annual leave plus bank holidays (rising to 31 days after five-years) or the equivalent paid for Term Time employees, family-friendly policies, hybrid working, paid mileage, a cycle to work scheme, onsite charging points for electric vehicles, access to mental health and wellbeing support, company performance bonus, professional development opportunities, benefit schemes for discounts and cashback offers, and more.

Would you like to know more information about this opportunity? If so, further information can be found in the job outline on our website.

You can also contact Gabi Gutteridge who would be happy to chat to you on 01438 544406 or email hfl.recruitment@hfleducation.org

To apply, email hfl.recruitment@hfleducation.org with a detailed CV along with a cover letter outlining your suitability for the role, and including responses to the following questions:

Describe your experience of planning, coordinating and delivering events, training programmes or webinars. What was your level of responsibility and what were the outcomes?
Describe a time when plans changed unexpectedly or competing priorities arose. What actions did you take and what was the outcome?
An example of how you have built effective working relationships to achieve a successful outcome.
What drew you to apply for this role, and how your skills and experience make you well suited to managing a programme of professional learning events and webinars?

As part of our move towards eradicating unconscious bias within the recruitment process, we will be anonymising your forms.

For recruitment queries, our Central Recruitment Team can also be contacted on the hfl.recruitment email address above.

To submit your application or to discuss any recruitment queries please email our Central Recruitment Team on hfl.recruitment@hfleducation.org or 01438 544439.
