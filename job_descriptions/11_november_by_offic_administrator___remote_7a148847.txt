Job Title: Administrator - Fully remote
Contract Type: Temporary Ongoing

Working hours: 14-16 hours a week - 4-5 hours each day (Very flexible)

Salary: £17.58 an hour

Working days: Monday to Friday

MUST have experience using excel


Are you ready to make a meaningful impact in the education sector?
Our client, a dynamic and innovative organisation in the education industry, is seeking an enthusiastic Administrator to support their operations. This temporary position offers an excellent opportunity to showcase administrative skills while contributing to a rewarding environment focused on learning and growth.


Key Responsibilities:
As an Administrator, the successful candidate will play a pivotal role in ensuring the smooth running of daily operations. Responsibilities will include:




Managing and organising documentation, ensuring accuracy and compliance.
Assisting in the coordination of meetings, events, and workshops.
Providing general administrative support to various departments.
Responding to inquiries and communicating effectively with staff, students, and stakeholders.
Maintaining records and databases to ensure efficient information management.
Collaborating with team members to enhance operational processes.

What Makes This Role Exciting?




Opportunity to work in a vibrant educational environment that fosters creativity and growth.
Engage with a diverse range of people, making a positive impact on students' educational journeys.
Develop your skills in a supportive and collaborative team atmosphere.
Contribute to the organisation's mission of providing exceptional educational experiences.

Who is the Ideal Candidate?
Our client is looking for individuals who demonstrate:




Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
Excellent communication abilities, both written and verbal.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite and familiarity with administrative software.
A proactive attitude and the ability to multitask in a fast-paced environment.
Previous experience in an administrative role, preferably within the education sector, will be advantageous.

Why Choose This Opportunity?
This temporary role offers competitive pay and the chance to gain valuable experience in the education sector. It is ideal for those looking to enhance their administrative skills while being part of a mission-driven organisation.
