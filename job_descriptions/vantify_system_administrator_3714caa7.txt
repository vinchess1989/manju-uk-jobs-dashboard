About the role

Our System Administrator plays a key role in supporting the effective operation of Vantify's systems and services. Responsible for maintaining accurate client, property and user information, the role ensures business systems remain up to date and users receive the support they need to work effectively.
 
Acting as a central point of contact for system administration, user support and onboarding activities, our System Administrator works closely with clients, suppliers and internal stakeholders to resolve queries, maintain data integrity and support service delivery. Success in this role requires strong organisational skills, attention to detail, excellent communication and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

What you’ll be getting up to


Manage and maintain internal systems, including user accounts, client records, property information and system relationships. 
Ensure client, supplier and property data is accurate, complete and regularly updated. 
Respond to user and client queries within agreed service levels, providing effective support and issue resolution. 
Act as a first point of contact for new users, providing onboarding support, guidance and system training. 
Liaise with clients and suppliers to gather information and maintain accurate system records. 
Investigate and resolve system and data-related issues, escalating where appropriate. 
Support the mobilisation and onboarding of new clients, users and suppliers. 
Produce client reports and support reporting requirements as required. 
Monitor data quality and carry out regular validation and maintenance activities. 
Provide administrative support across operational activities and contribute to the smooth running of the wider team.




What we're looking for

Qualifications

GCSE Maths and English at Grade C/4 or above (or equivalent).
Business Administration, IT, Customer Service or related Level 3 qualification (desirable).
Microsoft Office certifications or equivalent IT qualifications (desirable).


 
Experience

Working in a system administration, data administration or business support role.
Managing and maintaining databases, system records and user information.
Providing client, user or administrative support in a fast-paced environment.
Working with CRM systems, property systems or business management platforms.
Supporting onboarding, training or user adoption activities.
Managing multiple priorities while maintaining high levels of accuracy and attention to detail.
Troubleshooting system, user or data issues and identifying practical solutions.


 
Role Details 

Location: St Ives office 
Reporting to: Office Manager
Notice Period: 1 month
