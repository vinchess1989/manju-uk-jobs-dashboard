We are recruiting a Research Support Assistant who has excellent planning, organisational and attention to detail skills. You will need to be highly competent in word processing, excel and able to learn bespoke databases for data entry and data integrity checking to succeed in this role.

About NIHR BioResource

Our mission is to facilitate human health research and its transformation into medical practice.

NIHR BioResource is a unique and powerful resource for studying disease mechanisms and for investigating the links between genes, the environment, health and disease, enabling scientific discoveries as well as facilitating translational medicine for the benefit of patients.

Further details on the NIHR BioResource, including some of our COVID-19 research studies, can be found on our website https://bioresource.nihr.ac.uk.

About The Job

The Research Support Assistant supports the NIHR BioResource Clinical Team with the day-to-day running of the Clinical Research Unit under the supervision of the Research Delivery Manager in order to ensure that the unit is fully operational, up-to-date and meets all requirements of a CUH clinical area.

The team (Research Nurses) provides clinical support to the NIHR BioResource in the recruitment and recall of volunteers.

The role holder provides administrative and organisational support to the Clinical Team in order to ensure successful volunteer recruitment and recall for a variety of research projects. This role is key to the preparation of research appointments, including the paperwork required, as well as taking responsibility for welcoming volunteers to the unit; all of which will influence the volunteer's research experience.

About You

Ideally you will have:

Education to A Level standard with GCSE Mathematics and English or have equivalent qualifications or equivalent experience

Excellent interpersonal skills to be able to greet volunteers and answer questions from research volunteers via phone call and email as required

Experience in document control and of inputting data accurately

What We Offer

The University offers a wide range of benefits including flexible working, family friendly policies, an attractive pension scheme (employer's contribution is 12.6%). and 36 days' annual holiday (inclusive of public holidays). We encourage career development by providing extensive training & development opportunities.

This appointment is part-time, 25 hours per week, Monday to Friday. Work ¿ life balance is important, and we are open to discussion on flexible working.

The post is available on a fixed-term basis until 31 March 2028 in the first instance. NIHR BioResource is funded by the National Institute for Health & Care Research (NIHR) on a 4-year funding cycle.

Our offices are on the Cambridge Biomedical Campus CB2 0QQ (approximately 2 miles south of the city centre).

This appointment requires a Research Passport application.
