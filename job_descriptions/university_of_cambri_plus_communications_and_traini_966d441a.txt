Plus (plus.maths.org), the award-winning mathematics communication platform based at the University of Cambridge, is looking for a part-time Communications and Training Network Co-ordinator with excellent communication skills, organisational ability and initiative to coordinate online publication, digital media, and social media channels, as well as oversee day-to-day administration of our networking and training activities.

Plus is part of the MMP (maths.org), one of the University's flagship knowledge exchange, public engagement and outreach projects, based in the Faculty of Mathematics. The MMP consists of a small, dedicated and friendly team working on different programmes; in addition to this role, the MMP team overall currently consists of 11 staff.

Plus focuses on communication of and public engagement with mathematics, communicating complex mathematical concepts and mathematical research to a wide audience - including educators, older school students, policy makers and the interested general public - through online content including articles, interviews and podcasts.

Plus also develops and delivers researcher development training in mathematical communication, and has established and continues to lead the national Mathsci-comm network of professionals involved in communicating complex mathematics and data science.

This post is an interesting, highly varied role, working closely with the Plus Editors and the MMP Head of Operations. Responsibilities include publishing and publicising content, including articles and podcasts, across multiple web and social media platforms, as well as managing the national Mathsci-comm network member liaison and communications, organising online events, and maintaining Plus and Mathsci-comm site content and project workflows. The role also involves writing additional supporting content and editing audio and video content as required. In addition, the role holder collates analytics and evaluation data, manages and maintains network and project records, and ensures digital accessibility standards and GDPR compliance.

The successful applicant will have excellent written and verbal communication skills as well as excellent organisational and time management skills. They will be flexible and adaptable, with strong problem-solving skills, and able to work effectively both independently and with other members of the team. The ability to manage parallel workflows, prioritise tasks, and meet tight deadlines is essential. A high level of accuracy and attention to detail are also essential.

The postholder will have considerable scope to develop many areas of their work and should enjoy the opportunity to work on their own initiative.

Please refer to the Further Particulars document for details of the role's key responsibilities, full selection criteria and additional information.

This post is part-time at 0.4 FTE. We welcome conversations about flexible working arrangements.

Fixed-term: Funding is available until 31 July 2028, with the possibility of extension subject to further funding.
