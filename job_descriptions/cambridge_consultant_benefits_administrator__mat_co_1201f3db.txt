Introduction

We're looking for a Benefits Administrator to join our Finance team on a 12-month maternity cover contract, supporting the delivery of accurate benefits administration across our UK business.

The role

You'll join our global Finance team, consisting of 23 colleagues, who are responsible for managing the entire accounting function across the company. As our Benefits Administrator, you'll play a key role in delivering accurate, compliant and timely administration of a wide range of employee benefits.

Working closely with colleagues across Finance, HR and the wider business, you'll support benefits administration and employee queries, as well as assisting with a range of finance activities when required.

Responsibilities will include:


Assist with collating New Starters information for payroll set-up
Collating information such as changes in personal details for update to payroll
Payroll/Benefits Inbox admin
Holiday Record Adjustments and Reconciliations
Contacting employees to check and agree holiday bookings
Administer Holiday Buyback Scheme
Dealing with Relocation Records & Claims
Private Medical Health – keeping records updated online and reconciling annually for policy renewal
Travel Insurance – keeping records updated and reconciling annually for policy renewal
Cycle Scheme account administration
Set up Pension Inductions invites and collation of new starter info for pension set-up
Assist with Pension Administration and Upload
Assist with benefits queries
Assist with other ad-hoc activities within the accounts team (ie Travel Bookings via credit card, help with monthly credit card etc)
P11D's – pulling info together and reporting
PSA – pulling info together and reporting
Preparing Payroll Journal & General Ledger Reconciliations
Assist with Share Option Scheme Administration


Skills required


Good Communication & Analytical Skills
Accuracy and attention to detail
Good Excel experience
Knowledge of Pension & Benefit administration
Relocation knowledge desirable
