Role: Community Host

Salary £30K

Location: Cambridge

Hours: Shift pattern, Monday to Saturday, 40 hours per week

Join a Groundbreaking Rental Revolution as a Community Host!

Step into an exciting opportunity to be part of the single-family rental community, redefining what it means to rent. They are creating vibrant, sustainable neighborhoods designed for living, working, and thriving - all with a focus on long-term community and well-being.

Your Role:
As the Community Host for their flagship development, you'll be the heartbeat of the community, delivering exceptional customer service, fostering connections, and creating unforgettable experiences. You'll lead community initiatives, manage day-to-day operations, and ensure residents feel truly at home.

What You'll Do:

Welcome residents and visitors with warmth, managing the front desk with professionalism.
Organize and oversee community events, bringing people together to build connections.
Support leasing strategies, ensuring high occupancy and resident satisfaction.
Manage social media accounts, showcasing the best of the community.
Ensure communal areas are pristine, safe, and inviting through regular inspections.
Handle administrative tasks, resident queries, and tenancy documentation.
Collaborate with the team to drive innovation and enhance resident experiences.

Who You Are:

A people-person with a passion for hospitality and community building.
Organized, detail-oriented, and proactive with strong communication skills.
Experienced in front-of-house or customer-facing roles (1-2 years).
Tech-savvy, with proficiency in MS Office and social media management.
Flexible and adaptable, ready to work varied shifts, including weekends and evenings.

Why Join Them?
Be part of a fast-growing, forward-thinking organization that values creativity, innovation, and making a difference. You'll help shape a community where renting isn't just a temporary option - it's a lifestyle.

Ready to redefine renting and create something extraordinary? Apply now and make it happen!
