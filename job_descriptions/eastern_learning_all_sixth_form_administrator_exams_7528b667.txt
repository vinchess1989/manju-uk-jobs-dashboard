This is an exciting opportunity for an experienced administrator to join our small sixth form at Impington International College.

This role will report directly to the Assistant Principals, Heads of Sixth form and will involve all aspects of our sixth form administration and exams management. The role is front facing; you will manage the reception including meeting and supporting visitors, parents and students and managing phone and email communications.

We are looking for a colleague with the following skills:


An excellent organised administrator with a high attention to detail
Work well under pressure and take multi-tasking in your stride
Have the ability to work confidently and collaboratively
A confident communicator with a positive can-do attitude
Ideally with experience of administration in education, but not essential
