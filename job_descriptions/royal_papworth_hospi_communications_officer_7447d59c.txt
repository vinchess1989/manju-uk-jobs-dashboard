Royal Papworth Hospital NHS Foundation Trust
Communications Officer

The closing date is 05 November 2026

Apply for this job
Job summary

We are looking for an enthusiastic and creative individual with strong copywriting skills and meticulous attention to detail to join our communications team.

Your main responsibility will be to co-ordinate the process for creation and review of our patient information leaflets. You will work with clinical teams to edit and publish leaflets and consent forms to ensure that accurate and understandable medical information is communicated clearly to help patients be fully informed and reassured on the procedures and treatments they need to have.

You'll also get involved in the full range of communication channels and responsibilities:

-Writing and designing content for all-staff briefings and intranet

-Writing stories for our weekly internal all-staff email

-Interviewing patients and staff for feature stories

-Assisting with staff and public engagement events

Your work will directly and positively impact excellent patient care and help staff to feel valued, empowered and proud to work here.

You will be line managed by our communications co-ordinator and supported by the whole team, who meet each week to plan messages, campaigns and troubleshoot issues together. The team has a flexible working arrangement, typically two or three days per week in the office.

Interested in using your skills to make a positive difference? Apply today.

Interviews will be held on the 12th and 13th November 2026.

The role profile is subject to job evaluation consistency panel.

Main duties of the job

To co-ordinate the process of creation and review of patient information leaflets, working with subject matter experts to produce patient information flyers, booklets and consent forms which have been approved by the relevant stakeholders, and managing the supply of digital and printed materials to those who require it.

To represent the communications team trust-wide and provide communications advice and ensure brand guidelines are followed.

Gather content, write up and produce content for our weekly staff e-newsletter as well as write and upload news stories and patient/staff case studies for the intranet and website.

To contribute to the generation, design and production of content for internal and external communications channels.

To support event coordination, primarily for staff engagement activities such as our annual staff awards and long service awards, but also for our annual members meeting and other events that fall under the communication team's remit.

To support the daily running of the communications team with a variety of administrative duties.

About us

Royal Papworth Hospital NHS Foundation Trust is the UK's leading heart and lung hospital, delivering care to more than 50,000 patients a year in its new state-of-the-art hospital in Cambridge. We perform the most heart and/or lung transplants in the UK and carried out the UK's first successful heart transplant in 1979. It also offers emergency heart attack treatment, sleep centre care and is one of just five centres nationally for those in severe respiratory failure.

Based on the Cambridge Biomedical Campus the largest centre of medical research and health science in Europe the Trust is at the heart of treatments of the future. Royal Papworth became the first hospital trust in the country to be rated outstanding in all five areas assessed by the Care Quality Commission - a rating it still holds today. The Trust holds its values of compassion, excellence & collaboration at its core, and all colleagues joining the team are expected to uphold these in their day-to-day roles & interactions. Royal Papworth is proud of its diverse workforce and encourages people to embrace their individuality.

The Trust values difference and welcomes all applications irrespective of age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy & maternity, race, religion & belief, sex and sexual orientation. Providing they meet the minimum post criteria, applicants with disabilities will be offered an interview.
