Royal Papworth Hospital Charity supports the world-leading care delivered at Royal Papworth Hospital by funding projects that go above and beyond NHS provision. Through the generosity of our supporters, we invest in pioneering research, innovation, patient experience, staff wellbeing and exceptional hospital environments. As our fundraising income continues to grow, we're looking for a highly organised and detail-focused Charity Finance Administrator to join our team.

About The Role

This is a pivotal role at the heart of our charity's operations. You will be responsible for ensuring that all charitable income is processed accurately, financial records are maintained to the highest standard, and compliance requirements are met.

Working closely with fundraising, finance and charitable funds colleagues, you'll help ensure every donation is managed responsibly and has the greatest possible impact for patients, families and staff.
