Summary

Exciting opportunity for a People & Payroll Coordinator to join a growing and values-led organisation based in South Cambridgeshire.
Supporting a newly formed People & Culture team, this role offers broad exposure across HR operations, payroll, people systems, data and employee lifecycle activities.
Ideal for an HR or payroll professional who enjoys working with data, improving processes and contributing to a high-performing people function.




Our Client

Our client is a well-established international business with a strong reputation for innovation, quality and sustainability within its sector. With a collaborative culture and ambitious growth plans, they are committed to creating an engaging environment where employees can develop and thrive. As part of an evolving People & Culture function, they are seeking a People & Payroll Coordinator to play a key role in delivering operational excellence and supporting the employee experience.




The Role

Reporting to the Head of People & Culture, this position combines payroll administration, HR coordination, people data management and systems administration. Working closely with both the Head of People & Culture and HR Manager, you will help ensure the smooth delivery of core people processes while driving efficiencies, supporting automation initiatives and providing valuable people insights.




This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys balancing attention to detail with continuous improvement and wants to broaden their HR experience within a supportive and collaborative team environment.




Main Duties

In this role, you will support the delivery of first-class HR and payroll services across the organisation, including:

Coordinating monthly payroll activities and liaising with outsourced payroll providers.
Managing employee lifecycle administration, including onboarding, contracts and offboarding.
Maintaining accurate people data across HR systems and ensuring GDPR compliance.
Producing people metrics, reports and dashboards to support decision-making.
Supporting employee engagement, benefits administration and wider People & Culture initiatives.




The Successful Candidate

The ideal candidate will have experience within HR, payroll or people administration and enjoy working in a fast-paced environment where accuracy and continuous improvement are highly valued.

Experience supporting payroll administration, ideally within an outsourced payroll environment.
Strong Excel and data analysis skills with excellent attention to detail.
Comfortable working with HR systems and technology to improve processes and efficiency.
Strong communication skills with the ability to build relationships across the business.
Proactive, organised and keen to develop a broader career within HR and People Operations.




What's on Offer?

Competitive salary of approximately £30,000 - £40,000 depending on experience.
Hybrid working arrangement with flexibility to support business needs.
Opportunity to join a newly established and growing People & Culture team.
Exposure across HR operations, payroll, people systems, reporting and employee relations.
Ongoing learning, development and mentoring from experienced HR leaders.
Collaborative and values-driven culture focused on performance, accountability, trust and teamwork.
The chance to play a visible role in shaping processes and improving the employee experience across the organisation.
