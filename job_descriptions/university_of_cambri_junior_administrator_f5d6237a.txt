Join the Victor Phillip Dahdaleh Heart & Lung Research Institute (VPD-HLRI), part of the University of Cambridge, as our Junior Administrator and play a key role in supporting the Institute's senior leadership and day-to-day operations.

The VPD-HLRI brings together researchers, clinicians and professional services staff from across the University, NHS and partner organisations, creating a collaborative environment focused on improving cardiovascular and lung health. As Junior Administrator, you will provide professional, proactive and confidential administrative and secretarial support to the Directors of both the Institute and the Clinical Research Facility (HLRI-CRF), helping to ensure the smooth and effective operation of their offices.

This is a varied and fast-paced role that sits at the centre of Institute activity. You will manage complex and frequently changing diaries, coordinate meetings and events, organise domestic and international travel, and provide high-quality administrative support across a wide range of activities. Working closely with senior academic leaders, you will help ensure priorities are managed effectively and that information, correspondence and documentation are handled professionally and confidentially.

The role involves regular contact with colleagues from across the University, Royal Papworth Hospital and a range of external organisations. You will act as a key point of contact for the Institute, responding to enquiries, coordinating activities and building effective working relationships with a diverse range of stakeholders. Strong communication skills, sound judgement and a professional approach are therefore essential.

Alongside supporting the Directors, you will contribute to a broad range of Institute operations. This includes organising seminars, meetings and visits, supporting governance and committee activity, preparing agendas and papers, taking minutes, tracking actions and maintaining records. You will also assist with recruitment administration, finance processes such as purchase orders, invoices and expenses, and the maintenance of accurate administrative records and databases.

We are looking for someone who is highly organised, proactive and able to manage a varied workload while maintaining excellent attention to detail. You will be comfortable handling confidential information, working to competing deadlines and using your initiative to resolve issues and keep activities running smoothly. Previous administrative experience, including diary management and meeting coordination, would be advantageous.

You should be confident using Microsoft Office applications including Outlook, Word and Excel, and be comfortable learning new systems and processes. Most importantly, you will enjoy providing excellent administrative support, building positive working relationships and contributing to a collaborative and supportive team environment.

You will be joining a welcoming professional services team within a world-leading research institute, with opportunities to develop your skills and contribute to an environment that supports internationally recognised research and innovation.

Interviews will be held on 6th November 2026
