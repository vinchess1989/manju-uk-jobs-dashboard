About This School

This outstanding school located in Milton is committed to building an environment where each student is given the opportunity to thrive for excellence. Offering an engaging curriculum to help students achieve valuable knowledge to take with them into their futures.

We are seeking a detail:oriented and skilled HR Administrator to join the school to support the departments success. This is a full time role and you will be expected to work 35 hours per week, Monday to Friday.

Your Role Will Include


Managing employee records and HR databases
Assisting with the recruitment process, including posting job adverts, scheduling interviews, and processing applications
Coordinating onboarding processes for new staff members
Administering payroll and benefits programmes
Handling HR:related inquiries and providing support to staff
Ensuring compliance with employment laws and school policies
Assisting in organising HR events and training sessions
Supporting the HR team with various administrative tasks and projects


Who Were Looking For


Strong knowledge of employment laws and regulations
Excellent organisational and communication skills
Ability to work independently and as part of a team
High level of discretion and confidentiality
Proficiency in HR software and Microsoft Office
Experience in a similar role or within an educational setting
Eligibility to work in the UK is essential
Enhanced DBS on update service or willingness to complete a new DBS
Relevant professional experience with 2 recent professional references


You will be entitled to these benefits


Flexibility Day to day, long term, temp to perm and permanent opportunities
Developing skill set / career progression
Your own dedicated and experienced consultant
Excellent working environment with supportive team members
Earn GBP300 in shopping vouchers by referring a friend and is subject to terms and conditions


About Us

Randstad Education is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people, all employees employed within schools are required to undergo an enhanced DBS check.
