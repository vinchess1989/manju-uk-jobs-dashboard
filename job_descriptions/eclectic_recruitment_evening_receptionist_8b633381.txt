Full job description

We are currently recruiting for a Temporary Receptionist to provide professional front-of-house support on an ad hoc basis. This role would suit someone with previous reception, customer service or administrative experience who is available to work evenings and weekends.

Main Responsibilities

Welcoming visitors and providing a professional first point of contact
Answering and directing telephone calls and enquiries
Dealing with general queries in a helpful and professional manner
Supporting with basic administrative and reception duties
Maintaining a welcoming, organised and professional reception area

Ideal Candidate

Previous experience in reception, administration or customer-facing work
Confident and professional communication skills
Friendly and approachable manner
Reliable and flexible approach to ad hoc evening and weekend shifts
Comfortable working independently and dealing with a range of visitors and enquiries

This is an excellent opportunity for someone looking for flexible temporary work in Northstowe, with shifts offered on an ad hoc basis. If you are available for evening and weekend work and have relevant reception or customer service experience, we would be keen to hear from you.

Please apply via the advert or contact Calum at Eclectic Recruitment for more information.

We endeavour to reply to every candidate, every time, but if you haven't heard back within 10 days, please understand that you have unfortunately been unsuccessful for this position, or the position has been filled. Please call the office or send an email to discuss other potential opportunities.
