Communications and Events Partner

Location: Remote Role (The Candidate can live anywhere in England) but must be prepared to travel nationally with access to a full licence and vehicle.

Salary: £35,000 per annum plus £3,715 car allowance

Vacancy Type: Permanent, Full Time (37.5 hours per week)

We’re looking for a Communications & Events Partner to take the lead on planning and delivering some of Stonewater’s key corporate events and colleague engagement activities.

From our annual Company Day and One Connect sessions to leadership activities and wider corporate events, you’ll create engaging experiences that bring colleagues together, strengthen connections and help bring our organisational priorities to life.

This is a varied, hands-on role where you’ll take an event from the initial idea right through to delivery and evaluation. One day you could be shaping an agenda and working with senior leaders on content, and the next you might be coordinating suppliers, managing event technology or making sure everything is ready for the big day.

What you’ll be doing

You’ll:

Lead the planning and delivery of corporate events, taking ownership from concept through to evaluation.
Develop creative event ideas, formats and experiences that engage colleagues and support our priorities.
Coordinate venues, suppliers, logistics, audiovisual production, digital platforms and event materials.
Create engaging communications, presentations, agendas, scripts and speaker briefings.
Work closely with senior leaders, including our Chief Executive and Chief Officer Group, helping shape content and event experiences.
Manage event plans, schedules, expenditure and delivery timelines.
Use digital and audience engagement tools to make our events accessible, professional and engaging.
Gather feedback and evaluate our events to continually improve the experience and demonstrate value.

What we’re looking for

You’ll have experience of event management, with the confidence to manage events of varying sizes and complexity and keep multiple priorities moving at once. You’ll be a strong project manager and excellent organiser, but just as importantly, you’ll be creative, proactive and comfortable building relationships with people at all levels.

You’ll also bring:

Experience of planning and delivering events.
Excellent project management and organisational skills.
Strong written communication skills, with experience drafting and editing content.
The confidence to work with and influence senior stakeholders.
A good understanding of communications channels and digital tools.
Experience working with external suppliers, venues or production partners.
Strong attention to detail and the ability to remain calm and solutions-focused when plans change.
Confidence using Microsoft 365, particularly PowerPoint and Teams.

You’ll need to be comfortable working independently and taking ownership, while knowing when to involve others.

Please note: Travel is an important part of the role, with events taking place across Stonewater’s national geography. There will be travel and attendance at key events, venue visits and site inspections. You’ll therefore need a UK driving licence with business insurance and be happy to travel regularly as part of the role.

If you’re an organised, creative events professional who enjoys bringing people together and wants the opportunity to shape engaging experiences across a national organisation, we’d love to hear from you.

This role may require specific qualifications, skills, knowledge, and behaviours to meet the Competence and Conduct Standard for Social Housing. If not already qualified, there may be an expectation to study towards a professional qualification.

Discover Stonewater:

Stonewater is one of the UK’s largest social housing providers, driven by our purpose that everyone has a place to call home. With around 40,000 homes, we proudly serve more than 93,000 customers, providing safe, affordable homes and the services and support that help people and communities thrive.

Our mission is to make a difference to our customers and communities through quality homes and great services that reflect our social ethos. Guided by our vision to be the most trusted, inspiring and sustainable provider of affordable homes, we offer homes for rent, shared ownership and purchase, alongside specialist housing and support services, including retirement living, supported housing, domestic abuse refuges, LGBTQ+ safe spaces and young people's foyers.

Everything we do is centred on our customers. We build trusted relationships, create lasting social value and invest in homes, services and communities that enable people to live independently, realise their potential and thrive. Through innovation, collaboration and a long-term commitment to sustainability, we are helping shape stronger communities for the future.

We are a Disability Confident Employer and working towards accreditation for becoming a Disability Confident Leader, which allows us to record and report on disability, mental health and wellbeing in the workplace to better support our colleagues. We have also achieved accreditation for being an Advanced Employer for Investing in Ethnicity Maturity Matrix.
