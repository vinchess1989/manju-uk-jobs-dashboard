Where Performance Meets Purpose

Iconic golf brands. Commitment to excellence. Colleagues who care. Working at Acushnet Europe means you bring your background and experience to a company with a rich history of creating the best products for dedicated golfers around the world. Your performance has purpose at Acushnet Europe.

Based in St Ives, Cambridgeshire, our golf products are recognized globally for their unmatched quality, made possible by the dedication and pride we put into everything we do.

Our Cresting Department offers embroidery and heat print services on a range of FootJoy and Titleist products striving always for quality excellence. In fact, we abide by the credo “Autograph your work with excellence”.

The team now have an opportunity for an enthusiastic and experienced administrator to join them on a full-time, fixed term contract for 18 months.

As Crested Goods Administrator, you'll oversee inventory and manage the order process through its journey from reports and processing, ensuring precise stock records, coordinate with suppliers for timely deliveries, process orders with precision, handle customer enquiries with exceptional service, collaborate across departments for operational optimization, and conduct data analysis to drive improvements.

Now on to the more interesting part: YOU…

What does it take for you to be successful in this role?

We are looking for someone who has a calm disposition and is comfortable working on their own initiative, but also within the team itself. With excellent organisational, customer, IT and administrative skills, you will also have experience of working within strict lead times. Some experience within the embroidery industry would be advantageous, although full training will be given to the successful candidate.

Sounds great right? If this role sounds great to you, what are you waiting for… Submit your application directly on our careers site. Don't forget to include a cover letter telling us why you're the perfect fit for the job.
