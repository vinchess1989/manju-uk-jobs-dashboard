Job Description

Help shape a thriving digital community that changes lives

We're looking for a passionate and engaging ClubhouseConnect Coordinator to lead the day-to-day delivery of our innovative digital Clubhouse across the Confident Futures and Youth Jobs Guarantee programmes. You'll create a welcoming online community where people can connect, develop skills, improve their wellbeing and move closer to employment, education and training opportunities.

This is a unique opportunity to combine community building, wellbeing support and employability practice in a role that genuinely changes lives. You'll work alongside Members, programme teams and specialist facilitators to create meaningful activities, encourage participation, and develop a digital space that members value and return to.

If you're passionate about helping people achieve their potential, thrive in a people-focused environment and enjoy building supportive communities, we'd love to hear from you.


Salary: £30,000
Location: Remote, with applicants required to be based within Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, Cheshire, Cumbria, Hampshire, Herefordshire, Kent, Lancashire, Oxfordshire, Staffordshire, Surrey, Sussex, Warwickshire or Worcestershire. Occasional travel within the service area will be required.
Hours: Full-time (37.5hrs) 9am to 5pm
Travel: Occasional travel for meetings, events and face-to-face activities is required.


What You'll Be Doing

As our Clubhouse Connect Coordinator, you'll:


Lead the day-to-day delivery and development of Clubhouse Connect, creating an engaging, inclusive and supportive digital community.
Coordinate and maintain a varied programme of activities, wellbeing sessions, employability support and member-led initiatives.
Facilitate a digital work-ordered day, enabling Members to build skills, contribute meaningfully and take ownership of the community.
Support Members to improve confidence, wellbeing, digital skills and progress towards employment, education, volunteering or training opportunities.
Provide practical employability support, including CV development, job searching, applications and interview preparation.
Build a strong peer-support culture where Members can connect, encourage one another and sustain positive progress.
Work proactively with internal teams to generate referrals and encourage ongoing participation.
Develop effective relationships with Job Brokers, Navigators, Employment Facilitators and wider programme colleagues.
Monitor and moderate online community spaces, promoting safe participation and responding appropriately to safeguarding concerns.
Maintain accurate records, engagement data and reporting to support programme outcomes and continuous improvement.


What We're Looking For

You'll be someone who enjoys working with people, building relationships and helping others achieve their goals. To be successful, you'll have:


A commitment to our values of Kindness, Respect, Integrity and Innovation.
Experience supporting young people or adults with mental health conditions, health challenges, social barriers or other complex support needs.
Strong digital skills and confidence using virtual platforms, online engagement tools and case management systems.
Experience facilitating groups, workshops, communities or support programmes in either digital or face-to-face settings.
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to engage and motivate people from diverse backgrounds.
Experience helping individuals build confidence, achieve personal goals and progress towards positive outcomes.
Excellent organisational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
Experience maintaining accurate records and using data to monitor performance and outcomes.
Knowledge of employability support and local labour market opportunities.
A willingness to travel when required and support occasional activity outside standard working hours.


It would be great if you also have


Experience working within community-based, recovery-focused or wellbeing services.
Experience supporting people into employment, education, volunteering or training opportunities.
Knowledge of the Clubhouse model and work-ordered day approach.
Experience developing partnerships with employers, community organisations or support services.
Mental Health First Aid, coaching, counselling or related qualifications.


What We Offer

We’re a mission-led organisation with social value at our core. We’ve been trusted for nearly a century to help people improve their economic wellbeing. We’re a Disability Confident Leader and proud to reflect the communities we serve.


You’ll make a real impact: Every success story you help write will be felt in homes, workplaces, and communities across the county.
You’ll grow: With access to training, reflective practice, and a national network of likeminded colleagues.
You’ll be supported: We believe in team working, wellbeing check-ins, and manageable caseloads.
You’ll be part of something bigger: From mental health awareness to inclusive recruitment and sustainable employment, your work contributes to broader change.

About Fedcap UK

In the UK, Fedcap is a not-for-profit organisation committed to improving people’s economic mobility.

We support people to find work and access better health, education and learning opportunities.

Working across our four integrated practice areas – education, workforce development, health, and economic development – we deliver research-based solutions that remove barriers and open doors to economic mobility and wellbeing for individuals and communities.

We’re part of The Fedcap Group which serves more than 332,000 people each year through a network of not-for-profit affiliates in the US, UK, Canada and Australia.

Our Values

Respect: Always be respectful to each other, our customers, partners, stakeholders and funders – we are dedicated to exceptional service.
Integrity: Act with integrity– always be open and honest in our dealings with all people and stakeholders.
Innovation: Be innovative – always try to do things better, to look for new solutions that improve our services to customers.

About Us

Mission: To create opportunities for people by designing and delivering innovative, sustainable solutions and creating measurable changes for those who are socially disadvantaged.

Fedcap Employment and Fedcap Employment Scotland delivers employability solutions across the UK to build communities through a blend of local frontline professional teams and like-minded supply chain partners, using innovative and tested ideas developed both locally and internationally.

Our service is designed to give each individual the best opportunity according to his/her abilities and needs. Our programs provide targeted employment supports to a range of underserved groups, including single parents, people who were previously incarcerated and individuals with health, mental health and/or substance use conditions.
