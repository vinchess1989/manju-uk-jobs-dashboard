Legal PA - Private Client

Location: Cambridge
Salary: £29,500
Contract: Permanent
Hybrid: 3 days in the office, 2 from home

The Opportunity

An exciting opportunity has arisen for a Legal PA to join a growing professional services firm in Cambridge. Supporting a team of lawyers within the Private department, you'll provide high-quality administrative and organisational support in a friendly, collaborative environment.

The wider Cambridge office has around 40 employees and continues to grow, making this a great opportunity to join an expanding team.

Key Responsibilities

Providing day-to-day PA support to lawyers, including diary management, meeting coordination and handling correspondence.

Managing client and matter administration, including opening files, compliance checks and document management.

Assisting with billing, financial administration, expenses and outstanding payments.

Arranging business travel and preparing itineraries.

Supporting business development activities, including organising client meetings and maintaining CRM records.

Liaising with internal teams to ensure work is completed efficiently and deadlines are met.

About You

Previous experience in a Legal PA or administrative support role, ideally within a professional services environment.

Private client legal experience would be advantageous, although candidates with experience in other legal practice areas will also be considered.

Strong organisational skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and work to deadlines.

Excellent communication skills and a professional, client-focused approach.

High attention to detail, discretion and confidence handling confidential information.

A proactive team player who is comfortable working in a busy, growing environment.

If you're an organised and motivated Legal PA looking for your next opportunity in Cambridge, we'd love to hear from you.
