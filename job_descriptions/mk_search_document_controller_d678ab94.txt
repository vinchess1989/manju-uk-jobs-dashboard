Document Controller Opportunity - Leading M&E Contractor - Cambridge

MK Search are working with a well-respected and growing M&E contractor is looking for a Document Controller to join its team on a commercial project in Cambridge.

With a strong pipeline of upcoming projects across the region, the business is experiencing significant growth. This is an excellent opportunity to secure long-term work with a contractor offering above-market day rates and opportunities to work on future projects.

The role

You will manage project documentation and help ensure the site team, subcontractors and client have access to accurate, up-to-date information. Your responsibilities will include:

Maintaining the document control system and keeping project records organised.

Managing the issue, receipt and tracking of drawings, technical submissions and other project documents.

Controlling revisions and ensuring teams work from the latest approved information.

Liaising with the project team, subcontractors and consultants to track outstanding documentation.

Supporting project reporting, site administration and the collation of handover documents.

About you

Previous document control experience within construction.

Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.

Experience using electronic document management systems.

Confident communication skills and a proactive approach.

Experience working with an M&E contractor would be advantageous.

What’s on offer

Above-market day rates, dependent on experience.

Long-term work on a commercial project in Cambridge.

A strong regional pipeline with potential for follow-on work.

The opportunity to join a well-respected contractor during a period of substantial growth.

Apply now with your CV to be considered for this Document Controller opportunity in Cambridge.
