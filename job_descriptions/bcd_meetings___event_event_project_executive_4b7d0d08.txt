About The Role

We are looking for an Event Project Executive to join our Meetings Management Life Sciences team working remotely. You will support the planning of successful meetings, incentive and events for a range of clients by managing delegate attendee management, event administration, delegate and client communication. As part of this role, you will also have the opportunity to travel onsite both in the UK and Internationally.

You will thrive on juggling multiple projects, delivering excellent customer service, great administration and enjoy all things data!

Sound like you? Read on!

As An Event Project Executive, You Will


Build and maintain registration websites, electronic invitations and confirmations for each assigned event following client guidelines and within client / event driven timelines.
Support the project team in the project plan of the life cycle of the event which will include dealing external meeting attendees which will include Healthcare Professionals.
Oversee all aspects of the website design, understanding system capabilities and reporting parameters to ensure data is captured for reporting
Primary contact for client registration and reporting questions and primary Healthcare Professional (HCP) liaison and contact (pre, onsite and post event)
Work within the applicable registration website technology to manage thresholds for arrival / departure parameters, room blocks, attendee changes, activities, data capture and reporting
Prepare all relevant onsite print work, signage and documents including reports
Provide project planning support within the wider Project Team
Manage day to day pharma compliance reporting requirements


You’re Good At


Delegate / attendee management experience including but limited to using registration tools
Excellent attention to detail and experience in proofing copy
Analytical thinking: ability to independently and through collaboration troubleshoot to achieve efficient and effective registration processes
Proficient in Microsoft office applications
Ability to react, think on your feet, offer solutions, work on multiple projects / tasks simultaneously
Ability and want to travel onsite to events in the UK and Overseas


You Might Also Have


Cvent experience in registration and reporting
Pharmaceutical event delivery knowledge
Pharmaceutical compliance knowledge
Event Management degree or work experience in the Event Industry


You Should Know


This is a full time role UK remote based working Monday – Friday
Our standard working hours are 9:00am - 5:30pm with an hour lunch break
You must have the ability to travel within the UK and Overseas which may include weekends (lieu time given)
You must have the right to work in the UK
