Full job description

Our fantastic client is looking to recruit a Sales Administrator on a fulltime permanent basis. Working as part of an established busy sales team you will support the team in terms of producing sales quotes as directed by the sales managers. Dealing with customers to make sure they are happy with the product design and range before finalising the product into production (this can be in the form of several conversations and emails). You will update the internal CRM system and do all associated administration to support the department.

In a nutshell if you are organised, can talk to customers and are looking for a busy role this is the ideal role for you.

The hours of work are 8.30am to 5pm with a slightly early finish on a Friday (37 hours a week)

The company offer a slaalry upto £29000 and 25 days holiday plus excellent benefits.
