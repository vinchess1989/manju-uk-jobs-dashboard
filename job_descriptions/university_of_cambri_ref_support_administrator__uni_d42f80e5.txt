Would you like to spend your time working alongside academics, supporting them with a national exercise that comes round only once every seven years?

The Research Excellence Framework decides how research funding is distributed across UK universities and how their research is judged nationally. Its results shape funding and reputation for years afterwards. The next exercise, REF2029, is submitted in November 2028.

The School of Technology is looking for a REF Support Administrator to support the submission for the Unit of Assessment (UoA) 12 in Engineering, the University's second largest Unit of Assessment. This UoA covers three departments in the University: Engineering; Chemical Engineering & Biotechnology; Materials Science & Metallurgy. It comprises around 260 full time equivalent research staff and expects to submit in the region of 650 research outputs, together with a portfolio of impact case studies and a Strategy, People and Research Environment (SPRE) statement.

At its heart this is a role about supporting people. REF requirements are detailed, and they land on academics who are already busy. You will work in the departments, helping researchers keep their records up to date, following up missing information, answering routine questions and taking as much of the administrative load off academic colleagues as you can.

Reporting to the School REF Project Manager, you will provide administrative support across the submission, including research outputs, impact case studies and the SPRE statement. You will support committees and working groups with agendas, papers, minutes and action logs, keep records and trackers accurate and up to date, help colleagues use Symplectic Elements, produce routine reports and support communications to staff across the three departments.

You will not be doing this on your own. You will work as part of a small REF team, with the support of the School REF Project Manager, and alongside colleagues in the central University REF, Research Information and Open Research teams. It is a good opportunity to learn how a major University submission comes together and to build a network across the University.

The work is varied and the post is a visible one. At different points you might be helping a researcher with their publication records, taking the minutes at a working group, chasing evidence for an impact case study or preparing an update for colleagues. Few people get the chance to see a REF through from preparation to submission, and it is experience that is valued across the sector long afterwards.

You do not need to have worked on the REF before. What matters most is that you are organised, careful with detail and good at working with people: approachable, patient, able to explain routine processes clearly, and trusted to handle confidential information with discretion. You will need confident IT skills, experience of keeping accurate records, and the ability to manage a varied workload, follow procedures and know when to ask for advice.

You might be starting or building a career in university professional services, or you might be an experienced administrator from another sector looking for work with a clear purpose and a visible result at the end of it. Both are welcome, and we are happy to talk the role through informally before you decide whether to apply.

This is a fixed term position until 30 November 2028 as the funding is linked to the REF exercise. The post calls for a visible presence in the departments across Central and West Cambridge, so it is not suited to a predominantly remote working pattern.

Applications are welcome from internal candidates who would like to apply for the role on the basis of a secondment from their current role in the University.
