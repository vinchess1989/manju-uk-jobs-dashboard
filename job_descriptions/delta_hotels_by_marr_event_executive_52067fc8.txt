Sales and Events Executive

Are you confident, upbeat, and great with people? Do you love sales and turning opportunities into bookings?

We’re looking for a dynamic and enthusiastic Events Executive to join our Sales & Events team at Delta Hotels by Marriott, Huntingdon.

You’ll Be Responsible For


Managing incoming sales enquiries by phone and email
Converting enquiries into confirmed bookings and events.
Develop and maintain client relationships and ensure seamless transition through the sales cycle to drive new and repeat business.
Upselling to maximise revenue and deliver exceptional guest experiences
Working with the wider team to create successful events.


We’re Looking For Someone Who Is


Confident, positive, and naturally personable.
Motivated by sales targets and achieving results.
Proactive, energetic, and solution focused.
A great communicator and team player.
Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment.


What’s in it for you?


Competitive Salary + discretionary KPI bonus.
28 days' holiday including bank holidays
Meals on duty and complimentary parking.
Training and career progression opportunities.
Wellbeing and employee assistance programmes.
Free Leisure Club membership.
Worldwide Marriott hotel room discounts and food & beverage discounts.


If you’re confident, energetic, and ready to turn enquiries into great events, we’d love to hear from you!
