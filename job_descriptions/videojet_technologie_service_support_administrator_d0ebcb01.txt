The Service Support Administrator is responsible for supporting the operational effectiveness of the Service function through coordination of Environment, Health & Safety (EHS) activities and service inventory management.

This role ensures compliance with EHS standards, accuracy of inventory processes, and effective administrative support to enable Field Service Engineers (FSEs) and Service Managers to deliver high-quality, safe, and efficient customer service.

Key Responsibilities


EHS Coordination
Act as a central coordinator for EHS-related activities across the Service organisation
Maintain and track EHS documentation (e.g. RAMS, risk assessments, training records)
Support compliance with internal policies and external regulations
Coordinate EHS audits, inspections, and follow-up actions
Track and report EHS metrics, incidents, and corrective actions
Liaise with EHS teams and external bodies where required
Support rollout of EHS initiatives, training, and communications
Inventory Management (Service Parts & FSE Stock)
Maintain accurate inventory data across systems (ERP / CRM / Salesforce)
Support Min/Max stock level management and monitor stock availability
Coordinate parts ordering, replenishment, and stock transfers
Track usage trends and support reporting on consumption and stock accuracy
Work closely with FSEs to ensure correct stock levels aligned to install base and service demand
Support stock audits and continuous improvement of inventory processes
Service Operations Support
Provide administrative support to Service teams, including job tracking, documentation, and reporting
Act as a point of contact for service-related queries and internal coordination
Ensure service records, customer documentation, and system data are accurate and up to date
Support invoicing, job closure, and service process compliance
Work closely with Service Managers and engineers to ensure smooth workflow progression



Key Skills & Experience


Proven experience in an administrative or service support role
Working knowledge of EHS requirements and compliance frameworks
Experience with inventory or stock management processes
Strong attention to detail and organisational skills
Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
Proficient in ERP/CRM systems and Microsoft Excel
Strong communication and coordination skills



Key Behaviours


Accuracy & Accountability – Maintains high-quality data and documentation
Collaboration – Works effectively with Service, EHS, and operational teams
Proactive Approach – Identifies issues and drives resolution
Compliance Mindset – Ensures adherence to safety and operational standards
Continuous Improvement – Seeks opportunities to improve processes
