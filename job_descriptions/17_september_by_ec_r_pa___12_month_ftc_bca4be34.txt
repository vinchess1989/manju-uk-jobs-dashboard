Full job description

Our client, based in Cambridge, is looking for a Personal Assistant to join their team on a 12 month FTC. You will be working within a team; providing one on one assistance, and supporting the group. You will be required to manage your own workload and work proactively. You will also be required to work with other team members on certain tasks, such as large client meetings, and providing cover when colleagues are on leave. The role is full time, Monday to Friday and is primarily office based (90%), although there may be some flexibility for part hybrid working (10%) if required.

Key responsibilities will include:

Diary management
Organising and booking complex national and international travel for team members and clients
Organising internal and client meetings, and minute-taking of group meetings
Putting together expense claim forms
Organisation and logistics for tradeshows and events – working with our client’s Events Team
Diary planning for annual review meetings
Preparation of regular financial and operational project reports
Taking general calls and enquires for the team including responsibility for relaying client messages and passing them to the relevant person.




For this role, it is essential that candidates have the following experience and skills:

Significant and relevant experience within a similar role
Proven ability to take minutes
Working knowledge of Microsoft Office (Excel, Word and PowerPoint)
Competency in the use of IT: including email, internet and databases
High attention to detail
Excellent organisational and communication skills
A track record of being proactive, multitasking and able to prioritise and co-ordinate own work.
