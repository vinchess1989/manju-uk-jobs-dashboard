We have an exciting opportunity for a Paralegal to join our Public Law and Human Rights team in our Cambridge office.

The Public Law and Human Rights team undertakes a range of work in the areas of judicial review, public inquires, civil liberties (including inquests and human rights act claims) social care, healthcare and medical treatment, mental capacity and Court of Protection welfare disputes, and trafficking cases. Our lawyers are experts in their field.

At Irwin Mitchell, we believe that everyone has the right to be treated fairly. Our Public Law & Human Rights team in Cambridge is dedicated to helping clients challenge organisations that have infringed on their rights. We have extensive experience across all fields of public law, ensuring our clients' rights are respected.

Our public law and human rights solicitors are regarded as some of the best in the country by Legal 500 and Chambers UK. We've taken cases to the Supreme Court, European Court of Justice, and the European Court of Human Rights. While we aim to resolve cases through negotiation and mediation, we are prepared to take clients' fights as far as necessary to achieve the results they deserve

Your Role And What You'll Be Doing

This is an excellent opportunity to gain hands-on experience within a specialist Public Law and Human Rights team.

Your responsibilities will include:


Taking instructions from clients and assisting solicitors in providing legal advice.
Supporting the progression of cases, including preparing documents, undertaking legal research and maintaining accurate case records.
Managing administrative and case-related tasks to ensure matters progress efficiently.
Achieving time recording and fee income targets to support the effective running of the team.
Building and maintaining professional relationships with clients, experts, public bodies and other external organisations.
Attending and contributing to team and practice area meetings.
Keeping up to date with developments in relevant law, policy and practice.
Participating in training and development activities to enhance your legal knowledge and skills.
Working collaboratively with colleagues to deliver an excellent client experience.


Essential Criteria


Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to communicate professionally with clients and external organisations.
Excellent organisational skills and the ability to manage competing priorities effectively.
Experience handling sensitive or confidential information with discretion and professionalism.
Empathy and professionalism when supporting clients through challenging or complex personal circumstances.


Desirable criteria


Previous experience working in a paralegal, legal assistant, or similar legal support role.
Experience working within the relevant area of law.
Experience supporting fee earners with case management and administrative tasks.
25 days' holiday as standard, rising to 28 days depending on role level, plus bank holidays and the option to purchase additional leave.
