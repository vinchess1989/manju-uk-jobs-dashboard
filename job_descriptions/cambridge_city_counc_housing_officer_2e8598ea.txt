Job Description

As a Housing Officer, you will manage tenancies across a defined Cambridge neighbourhood. You will help residents sustain their tenancies, resolve problems and make their neighbourhoods safer and better places to live.

You will:


Take ownership of a varied caseload, including tenancy disputes, complaints, tenancy audits and anti-social behaviour. You will work with residents to resolve issues and take enforcement or legal action where appropriate.
Identify safeguarding concerns and work with colleagues and partner agencies to support residents and manage risk.
Build relationships with residents and Residents’ Groups, and help deliver projects that improve neighbourhoods.
Manage and develop an Assistant Housing Officer, providing day-to-day support, supervision and clear direction.


You will have experience of delivering customer-focused services and achieving results in tenancy management or a similar enforcement or community development role. You can manage a busy workload, take ownership of complex cases and make sound decisions.

You will communicate clearly and sensitively, build trust with residents and colleagues, and work confidently with partner organisations. You will also have experience of first-line management or coaching others, and be able to support someone’s development and address performance concerns.

You will need a good standard of education and confidence using common IT applications, including Microsoft 365. A relevant housing qualification and experience working within housing is desirable.

Additional Information

The contractual work base is Mandela House. The role involves visits and meetings at sites across Cambridge, so you must be able to travel between them.

Cambridge City Council is committed to safeguarding children, young people and adults, and expects all staff to share this commitment.

An Enhanced DBS will be required as part of the role.

Hours: This is a full-time role, for 37 hours per week.

Although the role is advertised as full time, if you believe you have the skills and experience to join us and would like to discuss different working patterns, please do get in touch.

Contract: Permanent X2

Salary: £43,150 - £48,738 per annum (Band 6) pro rata for part time.

Location: This is flexible – working in Cambridge on site or at one of our offices or working from home. You will be able to balance your work location accordingly.
