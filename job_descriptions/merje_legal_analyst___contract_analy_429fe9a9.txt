Legal Analyst / Contract Analyst - Fully Remote (UK Based) - Up to £30,000 + Benefits




I am looking to hire multiple Legal Analysts to join a growing legal operations and contract management team supporting a range of corporate and financial services clients across the UK.




This is an excellent opportunity for someone with 6-24 months' experience reviewing contracts, legal agreements or commercial documentation who is looking to build a long-term career within legal operations, contract management and legal technology.




The Opportunity

You'll be joining a fast-paced team responsible for reviewing and managing a high volume of commercial agreements using established playbooks, processes and legal technology tools.

The role offers exposure to a variety of industries and agreement types, making it an ideal move for someone looking to broaden their legal experience outside of traditional legal practice.

Responsibilities

Reviewing commercial contracts and legal agreements.
Assessing contractual terms against established guidance and playbooks.
Identifying risks, issues and deviations for escalation.
Supporting high-volume contract review projects.
Working closely with legal, operational and commercial stakeholders.
Using legal technology and AI-enabled tools to improve efficiency and consistency.
Supporting wider contract management and legal operations activities.




About You

I am particularly interested in candidates with 6-24 months' relevant experience gained within:

Legal Operations
Contract Management
Commercial Law Firms
In-House Legal Teams
Alternative Legal Service Providers (ALSPs)
Legal Process Outsourcing (LPO) environments
Financial Services Legal Teams




Experience reviewing any of the following would be advantageous:

NDAs
MSAs
Statements of Work
Supplier Agreements
Commercial Contracts
Financial Agreements
Corporate Documentation




What's On Offer?

Fully remote working (UK based).
Salary up to £30,000.
Exposure to contract management and legal operations.
Opportunity to work with leading corporate and financial services clients.
Structured career progression.
Exposure to legal technology and AI-enabled review tools.
Supportive and collaborative team environment.




This opportunity would suit ambitious legal professionals looking to develop their careers within contract management, legal operations and legal technology while gaining exposure to a broad range of commercial agreements and clients.
