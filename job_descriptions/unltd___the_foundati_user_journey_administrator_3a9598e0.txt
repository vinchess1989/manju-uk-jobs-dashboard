Job description

You will be responsible for general administration duties supporting UnLtd’s Social Entrepreneur Award programmes, particularly the administration of our newly funded National Lottery Climate Action Programme acting as the first point of call for social entrepreneurs applying for an Award. You will provide internal administrative support throughout the application process and post-application journey. This role would suit someone who enjoys balancing people-focused support with strong organisation and attention to detail.
