Logistics Assistant

Location: Huntingdon, PE29 (On-site)
Contract: Temporary
Hours: Monday to Friday, 10:00am to 3:00pm
Pay Rate: £12.71 per hour

Join a Busy Logistics Team

We are looking for a reliable and organised Logistics Assistant to support the day-to-day operations of a busy warehouse and distribution environment in Huntingdon.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone with strong administrative skills, attention to detail, and an interest in logistics, supply chain, or warehouse operations. Whether you already have logistics experience or are looking to develop your career within the sector, we'd love to hear from you.

The Role

As a Logistics Assistant, you'll play an important role in ensuring goods move efficiently through the operation and that essential administrative tasks are completed accurately and on time.

You'll start each day by reviewing delivery schedules, checking incoming and outgoing shipments, and updating internal systems with transport and stock information. Working closely with warehouse, logistics, and customer service teams, you'll help ensure orders are processed smoothly and efficiently.

Throughout the day, you'll support with transport documentation, data entry, shipment tracking, inventory updates, and responding to internal enquiries. You'll be responsible for maintaining accurate records and ensuring that logistics information is kept up to date.

No two days are the same, and you'll be someone who enjoys a fast-paced environment where organisation and attention to detail are key.

Key Responsibilities

Support the day-to-day administration of logistics and warehouse operations.
Process and maintain shipment and delivery records.
Update stock and inventory information on internal systems.
Assist with booking deliveries and coordinating transport schedules.
Monitor incoming and outgoing orders and resolve any discrepancies.
Prepare and manage transport and shipping documentation.
Liaise with warehouse, customer service, and operational teams.
Track deliveries and provide updates as required.
Ensure all records are accurate and maintained in line with company procedures.
Provide general administrative support to the logistics team.

About You

We're looking for someone who is organised, proactive, and enjoys working as part of a team.

Essential Skills & Experience

Previous administration experience in an office, warehouse, logistics, or customer service environment.
Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
Good communication skills, both written and verbal.
Confident using Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel and Outlook.
Strong organisational and time-management skills.
Ability to prioritise workload and meet deadlines.
Positive and professional approach to work.

Desirable

Previous logistics, transport, warehouse, or supply chain experience.
Experience using ERP, stock control, or warehouse management systems.
Understanding of shipping and transport documentation.

What You'll Bring

A positive attitude and willingness to learn.
Excellent attention to detail.
Strong problem-solving abilities.
The ability to work independently and as part of a team.
Reliability and a flexible approach to supporting operational requirements.

What's on Offer?

Temporary contract.
Monday to Friday working pattern.
Part-time hours, 10:00am to 3:00pm.
£12.71 per hour.
Friendly and supportive working environment.
Opportunity to gain valuable experience within a logistics and supply chain function.

Apply Now

If you're an organised and motivated individual looking for a role within logistics and administration, we'd love to hear from you. Apply today to join a busy and supportive team in Huntingdon.
