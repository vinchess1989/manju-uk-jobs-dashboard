We are working with a biotech in Cambridge to hire an HR Coordinator on a 6-12 months fixed term contract.




Joining a small HR team, you will become the operational backbone of the people function. In this fast-paced, high-impact role, you will manage the administrative engine of the entire employee lifecycle, ensuring seamless day-to-day operations and a positive employee experience.




Your Impact

HRIS & Data Integrity: Maintain absolute accuracy within our HR Information Systems, tracking data changes, updates, annual leave, tracing sickness absence and compliance records.
Onboarding & Offboarding: Own the new starter journey by managing documentation, background checks, and HR coordination, while ensuring respectful offboarding processes.
Employee Relations Support: Act as the first point of contact for day-to-day policy queries.




What You Bring

HR Foundation: Proven experience in an HR administrative or coordinator role, ideally backed by a CIPD Level 3 or 5 qualification or equivalent practical experience.
Tech-Savvy Mindset: Proficiency with modern HRIS platforms and an appetite for leveraging automation tools to streamline manual processes.
Unwavering Discretion: Exceptional attention to detail combined with the emotional intelligence required to handle sensitive, confidential data.
