Mortgage Administrator / Trainee Mortgage & Protection Adviser

Location: Cambridgeshire – applicants must be local to the office at PE15 6RP

Hours: Part-time, 4–5 hours per day, with one day per week in the office

Employment Type: Employed

Basic Salary: To be confirmed at interview

Looking to start a career in mortgages? This opportunity offers a route into the industry, starting in administration with the aim of progressing into a Mortgage & Protection Adviser role after 6–12 months.

Our business partner within Mortgage Advice Bureau (MAB) is looking for an organised, motivated individual who is keen to learn. No previous mortgage experience or CeMAP qualification is needed to start.

About The Role

You’ll begin by supporting the team with day-to-day mortgage administration, learning how the mortgage process works and building your knowledge through practical experience.

Your Responsibilities Will Include


Supporting advisers with mortgage administration and case progression.
Maintaining accurate client records and documentation.
Communicating with clients, lenders and other relevant parties.
Helping the team deliver a smooth and professional service.


As your knowledge and confidence develop, the aim is for you to transition into a mortgage adviser role after 6–12 months, subject to your progress and the required qualifications.

What You Can Expect


A route into mortgage advice – start in administration and build towards an adviser role.
Training and support – learn alongside experienced mortgage professionals.
Part-time hours – working 4–5 hours per day.
One office day per week – applicants must live locally.
A recognised brand – develop your career with a business partner of Mortgage Advice Bureau.


What We’re Looking For


A confident communicator who enjoys working with people.
Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
A proactive attitude and willingness to learn.
An interest in mortgages, finance or property.
The ambition to work towards the qualifications needed to become an adviser.
GCSEs or equivalent.


Interested?

If you’re looking for a part-time role with the opportunity to build a career in mortgage advice, we’d love to hear from you. Apply directly to find out more.
