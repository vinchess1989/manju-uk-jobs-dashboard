Marketing Assistant – Fully Remote




🏠 Location: Fully Remote – UK Based

💷 Salary: £26,000–£33,000, depending on experience

🗓️ Hours: 9.00am–5.00pm, Monday–Friday




The Opportunity

FindCore are thrilled to be assisting a business with the recruitment of a Marketing Assistant.

Supporting the marketing team with day-to-day activities and helping deliver engaging content and campaigns.




Key Responsibilities

Assist with creating and scheduling social media content
Maintain website content and update marketing materials
Support email marketing campaigns and newsletters
Monitor campaign performance and prepare basic reports
Assist with market research and general marketing administration




Skills & Experience

Strong written communication skills and attention to detail
Confident using social media platforms and learning new systems
Strong organisational skills and a creative approach
Able to prioritise tasks and meet deadlines
Comfortable working independently and communicating with a remote team




What’s on Offer

Salary of £26,000–£33,000, depending on experience
Fully remote working
Consistent Monday–Friday hours, 9.00am–5.00pm
Experience across a range of day-to-day marketing activities




The closing date for applications is 15/11/2026. Applications will only be reviewed once the closing date has passed.
