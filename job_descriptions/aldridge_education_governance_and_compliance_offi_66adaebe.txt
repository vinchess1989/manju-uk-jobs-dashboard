About Aldridge Education

Aldridge Education is a national multi-academy trust supporting ten schools and colleges, as well as an early learning provision, which spans Brighton, London and the Northwest. Our story began in 2008 with the opening of Darwen Aldridge Community Academy by the Aldridge Foundation, led by our patron Sir Rod Aldridge. That first academy set the blueprint for a family of entrepreneurial, community-focused settings. As the family grew, Aldridge Education was established in 2016 to lead and support the group.

Across every Aldridge setting, we are guided by shared values of engagement, inclusion and opportunity. We believe education should be stimulating, enriching and enjoyable, delivered in environments where learners feel safe, valued and able to access high-quality learning that prepares them for life beyond their Aldridge years. Our focus is on widening opportunity, fostering a strong sense of belonging and developing the skills, curiosity and resilience needed to thrive in a complex, changing world. Through the Aldridge Attributes, our Connect programme and our commitment to Aldridge Choice, we aim to ensure every learner – especially those facing the greatest barriers- can look to the future with confidence, purpose and ambition.

About the Role:

The postholder is responsible for supporting the development of Local and Trust Governance processes and arrangements and for the implementation and administration of these. The postholder will also work with the Head of Local Governance and Compliance to implement and administer trust compliance programmes covering policies, websites, complaints and Data Protection. . As clerk to the Trust Board and its sub committees the postholder will provide administrative support to facilitate effective and efficient meetings and ensure Trust governance information is managed effectively and in accordance with legal requirements, observing confidentiality where necessary.

Sub-committees include Finance & Operations Committee (FOC), Education Standards Committee (ESC), Remuneration Committee (Remco) and Nominations Committee.
