About the Role

We are looking for an organised, proactive and commercially aware Assistant Product Development Manager to join our Product Development team, supporting the successful delivery of new product development (NPD) projects for Bensons for Beds’ Owned Brands.

Based at our Huntingdon manufacturing facility, you will play a key role in coordinating NPD projects from initial brief through to manufacturing readiness and product launch.

Working closely with the Product Development Manager, you will take ownership of day-to-day project management and progression: coordinating activities, monitoring key milestones, keeping stakeholders aligned and identifying and managing potential risks, with significant issues and key decisions escalated to the Product Development Manager.

Alongside your project management responsibilities, you will provide practical support across product development activities, including coordinating product photography, assembly instructions, care guides and supporting product documentation.

This is an opportunity for someone with experience in project coordination, buying, product development or a related discipline to develop their career within a furniture manufacturing and retail environment.




Key Responsibilities

1. New Product Development Project Management

Take ownership of the day-to-day coordination and delivery of multiple NPD projects, from initial brief through to product launch.
Develop, maintain and communicate project critical paths, ensuring key milestones, responsibilities and deadlines are clearly defined.
Proactively monitor project progress, following up on outstanding actions and driving activities forward to maintain agreed timelines.
Organise and support with coordinating cross-functional project meetings, preparing agendas, documenting key decisions and ensuring actions are completed.
Maintain accurate project trackers, providing regular updates on project status, milestones, risks and dependencies.
Identify potential delays, risks or challenges, escalating significant issues and key decisions to the Product Development Manager.
Support the continuous improvement of NPD processes, project management tools and ways of working.

2. Cross-Functional Collaboration & Manufacturing Coordination

Support the Product Development Manager in the coordination between Buying, Manufacturing, QC, Purchasing and other relevant stakeholders.
Build strong working relationships across departments to support effective communication, collaboration and accountability.
Attend product development and manufacturing meetings, ensuring key actions, decisions and next steps are clearly documented and communicated.
Support prototype and product sample reviews, coordinating feedback, amendments and subsequent actions with relevant teams.
Work closely with manufacturing stakeholders to monitor development progress and support the transition of new products into production.
Ensure project stakeholders have access to accurate and up-to-date information throughout the development process.

3. Product Development & Launch Support

Support the preparation and coordination of product photography briefs, ensuring key product features, configurations and requirements are accurately captured.
Coordinate product samples, photography schedules and associated requirements with internal teams and external partners where appropriate.
Support the drafting, creation and maintenance of NPD documentation, furniture assembly instructions and product care guides.
Liaise with QC, Manufacturing and other relevant teams to coordinate the review and approval of product documentation, ensuring technical accuracy is validated by the appropriate specialists.
Track the completion of product documentation and supporting materials to ensure readiness ahead of product launches.
Provide additional coordination and administrative support across product development activities as required.




Skills & Experience

Essential

Previous experience in project coordination, product development, retail buying or a similar role.
Excellent organisational and time management skills, with the ability to coordinate multiple projects and competing priorities.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with confidence working across different departments and levels of seniority.
A proactive and solutions-focused approach, with the ability to follow up on actions, challenge delays constructively and drive progress.
Strong attention to detail and the ability to maintain accurate project information and documentation.
Good working knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook.
Ability to work independently on day-to-day responsibilities, while recognising when issues require escalation.

Desirable

Experience within furniture, homewares, retail or manufacturing.
An understanding of new product development processes, from initial concept through to launch.
Experience developing or maintaining project plans, critical paths or milestone trackers.
Experience coordinating product samples, photography or technical documentation.
Familiarity with project management tools or methodologies.

Personal Attributes

Highly organised, methodical and detail-oriented.
Proactive, self-motivated and comfortable taking ownership of responsibilities.
Confident and approachable, with the ability to build positive working relationships.
Comfortable following up with stakeholders, tracking agreed actions and escalating delays when required.
Adaptable and able to respond positively to changing priorities.
Curious and enthusiastic about products, manufacturing and product development.
Keen to develop project management skills and take on increasing responsibility.




Measures of Success

Success in this role will be demonstrated through:

Effective day-to-day coordination and progression of NPD projects across Bensons for Beds’ Owned Brands portfolio.
Accurate, up-to-date project plans and critical paths, with clear visibility of milestones and responsibilities.
Proactive management of project actions, risks and dependencies, with timely escalation where required.
Strong cross-functional communication and collaboration throughout the development process.
Timely coordination of product photography requirements, assembly instructions, care guides and supporting documentation, with completion tracked and risks to launch readiness escalated promptly.
Consistent application of NPD project management processes and contribution to continuous improvement.
