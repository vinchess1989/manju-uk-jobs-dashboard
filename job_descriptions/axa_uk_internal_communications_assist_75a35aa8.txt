About AXA

AXA is a global leader in insurance and financial services, dedicated to helping customers protect what matters most to them. As the sixth-largest insurance company in the world, we provide a wide range of services, including health, car, home, and business insurance. We support millions of customers worldwide, helping them navigate life's uncertainties with confidence.

AXA UK Support Functions look after our three customer-facing business units, providing the infrastructure and expertise to make sure we can be there for our customers.

Job Overview

You’ll play a key role in helping deliver engaging, well-organised and high-quality internal communications across AXA UK. Working as part of the Content and Channels team, you’ll provide essential coordination and operational support across content creation, publishing, channel management, engagement campaigns and large events and meetings. You’ll help keep the Internal Communications function running smoothly including owning the forward plan, maintaining communication calendars and distribution lists, keeping channels up to date, supporting major events and leadership activity, and ensuring reporting is accurate and on time. This is a fast paced and varied role, ideal for someone highly organised, collaborative and enthusiastic about content creation, storytelling, content creation, digital channels and tools and helping to make AXA UK a great place to work through communications.

Key Responsibilities


Upload, format and publish content across internal communication channels, ensuring it’s accurate, accessible and aligned to editorial and brand guidelines.
Manage and maintain internal distribution lists, the overall communication forward plan and content and publishing schedules, ensuring communication reaches the right audiences at the right time.
Coordinate logistics and operational support for campaigns, meetings and events, site visits and other engagement activity.
Maintain organised libraries of communication assets, presentations, imagery and approved content.
Support reporting and insight activity, including monthly and quarterly communication reporting and ongoing campaign and channel metrics.
Provide administrative and coordination support across the Internal Communications team.
Work closely with the Internal Communications Managers and Content & Channels Specialists to support delivery of communication priorities.
Help ensure communication activity is accurate, well organised and delivered to a high standard.


Work Arrangements

At AXA we work smart, empowering our people to balance their time between home and the office in a way that works best for them, their team and our customers. You'll work at least two days a week (40%) away from home, moving to three days a week (60%) from January 2027. Away from home means attending the office, visiting clients or attending industry events. You’ll travel to our London office on a regular basis. We’re also happy to consider flexible working arrangements, which you can discuss with Talent Acquisition.

Your Skills & Experience


Excellent written English, grammar and proofreading skills, with strong attention to detail and accuracy is essential.
Digital and administrative skills, including Microsoft 365 and collaboration tools.
Highly organised and self-motivated, with confidence to manage tasks independently and work with minimal supervision.
Calm under pressure, comfortable working in a fast paced environment with multiple priorities, changing demands and short deadlines sometimes out of standard office hours.
Confident working with stakeholders at all levels, with a professional, collaborative approach.
Able to handle confidential and sensitive information with a high level of discretion, professionalism and good judgement.
Comfortable working with digital and visual communication materials, including presentations, imagery, video and branded content.
Experience supporting communication activity such as content creation, publishing, digital channel management and large meetings and events would be beneficial.
Proactive, collaborative and eager to learn.


As a precondition of employment for this role, you must be eligible and authorised to work in the United Kingdom.
