Full job description

Location: Saffron Walden (CB10)

Type: Permanent

Hours: 9am - 5.30pm (Monday to Friday)

Salary: circa £40,000 depending on experience

Job Reference: 36366




Our client, an innovative technology and engineering business, is seeking a highly organised and proactive Project Management Assistant to support the successful delivery of a varied portfolio of projects.




Responsibilities:

• Provide administrative and coordination support across multiple projects

• Maintain project plans, schedules, resource records and documentation

• Track project budgets, expenditure and forecasts

• Monitor project actions, risks and issues, following up with relevant stakeholders

• Prepare progress reports, dashboards, presentations and management information

• Arrange project meetings, prepare agendas, record minutes and monitor agreed actions

• Support project start-up, closure and lessons-learned activities

• Identify opportunities to improve processes, standardise working practices and automate routine tasks

• Assist with the delivery of smaller projects as experience develops




Requirements:

• Strong organisational skills and excellent attention to detail

• Ability to manage competing priorities and coordinate multiple activities effectively

• Confident communicator, comfortable engaging with stakeholders at different levels

• Strong written communication skills, including report writing and meeting documentation

• Good working knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook

• A proactive, analytical approach with an interest in process improvement and automation

• Ability to work independently while contributing positively to a team




Desirable experience:

• Background in project administration, project support or project coordination

• Understanding of project management principles and methodologies

• Experience using project management or business systems such as Microsoft Project, Jira or ERP platforms

• Familiarity with resource planning, budget monitoring or workflow automation




Please contact us as soon as possible for more details or apply below!
