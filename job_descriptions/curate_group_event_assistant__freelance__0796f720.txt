About Lumos Experiences

Lumos Experiences, is one of the most distinctive live entertainment concepts, creating atmospheric candlelit musical concerts that transform unexpected spaces into something genuinely special.

We're growing fast, running multiple shows per week across new locations, and are looking for friendly, reliable and guest-focused event assistants, ideally based near Cambridge or Norwich, to support the delivery of shows across East Anglia.

About The Role

Job type: Freelance

Shift pattern: Fridays and Saturdays; shifts are typically 8 - 10 hours

Onboarding: Full training provided, including a paid training shift

Work location: In person, on site

Day rate: £14 per hour

This freelance role is perfect for anyone who thrives in live event environments and is excited to gain experience with a creative, expanding business.

As an Event Assistant, you’ll be a key part of delivering the Lumos guest experience. The role combines setting up the stage and venue with looking after guests, with a focus on professionalism, presentation and supporting the Event Manager so that every concert runs smoothly. You’ll represent the Lumos brand at all times, from the moment guests arrive to the moment they leave.

It’s a great fit for someone who has a good eye for detail, is confident interacting with the public, takes pride in how they present themselves and enjoys being part of a live music atmosphere.

As you grow in confidence and show reliability, leadership and strong event management skills, there will be opportunities for promotion to Lumos Event Manager.

What You'll Do

Guest welcome and front-of-house


Greet guests warmly and professionally on arrival
Assist with ticket checking and guest list management
Direct guests to their seats
Answer general questions about the evening
Support audience flow before, during and after the performance


On-site support


Assist the Event Manager with the non-technical event set-up
Help manage seating adjustments and late arrivals
Monitor audience areas during the performance
Assist with an orderly guest exit at the end of the event
Ensure a team member is always present in the room as a point of contact


Conduct and professionalism


Remain calm, polite and solutions-focused at all times
Stay aware of your surroundings throughout the shift
Speak clearly and confidently to guests
Escalate issues promptly to the Event Manager


Team support


Follow direction from the Event Manager
Work collaboratively with other staff members
Step in proactively during busy periods
Help keep the front-of-house area clean and organised


What We’re Looking For


Confident interacting with the public
Warm, professional communication skills
Collaborative and respectful to fellow team mates
Takes pride in appearance and presentation
Stays professional under pressure
Enjoys live music and atmospheric venues
Understands that small details create exceptional experiences
Holds a full UK driving licence and has their own car or happy to car share
Ideally based in or around Cambridge or Norwich
Comfortable with manual handling and lifting tasks


Why you’ll love working with us


You’ll join a small, friendly team that transforms beautiful venues into unforgettable live music experiences
You’ll be part of a creative, growing company, with the opportunity for more hours
It’s a flexible, freelance role


If you’re excited by the idea of working at candlelit concerts in some of the most beautiful churches and venues in the region, we’d love to hear from you!
