The Faculty of Law is a world leading institution for legal research and teaching. We are seeking to appoint a Tripos Course Coordinator to support the Director of Tripos to ensure the smooth running of the teaching and learning activities relating to the Law Tripos, as well as supporting the Director of International Strategy & Partnerships to ensure the smooth running of the undergraduate exchange programme.

The Faculty of Law currently has over 100 University, Faculty and College teaching officers. Almost every area of legal interest is represented. Within its professional services team, the Faculty has 25 administrative staff and an IT team of 6.

Key responsibilities

¿ Provide course coordination and administration for the Law Tripos.

¿ Coordinate the Tripos seminar applications, liaise with Seminar Course Convenors, collect and collate dissertations for marking.

¿ Deal with enquiries from prospective, current and former undergraduate students regarding all aspects of the course, including admissions procedures, funding opportunities, course requirements and progression.

¿ Provide degree verification and confirmation of awards for professional bodies.

¿ Coordinate the Faculty's undergraduate Student Exchange Scheme and support the Director of International Strategy & Partnerships.

¿ Support the Director of Tripos with the administration of the Legal Skills and Methodology pre-arrival course.

¿ Act as Secretary to the Directors of Studies Committee and the Staff Student Consultative Committee.

The ideal candidate will have previous administrative experience and be highly competent in standard software packages (in particular Word, Excel and Outlook). A confident IT user with excellent organisational skills, the candidate should also possess good time management, communication and interpersonal skills. We are looking for someone who is service-oriented and pro-active, able to use their own initiative as well as adhering to the collaborative environment that our department fosters. Candidates must be educated to at least A-level standard or equivalent.

The role holder will be based in central Cambridge. This is a full-time, permanent position.

Further details can be found in the Further Particulars document.

The Faculty of Law successfully renewed its Athena Swan Bronze Award in January 2026. This recognition highlights our ongoing efforts to advance gender equality and build an inclusive culture across the institution.

Benefits Of Working At Cambridge Include

¿ Competitive rates of pay with automatic service-related pay progression and annual cost of living increases;

¿ Generous annual leave allowance of 36 days paid leave (pro rata including bank holidays);

¿ Flexible and hybrid working opportunities;

¿ Generous maternity, adoption and shared parental leave entitlement and other family friendly schemes (e.g. workplace nurseries and salary exchange schemes for childcare);

¿ An auto-enrolment pension scheme, with a generous employer contribution;

¿ Travel benefits and retail discounts at over 2,000 local and national stores;

Schemes to support with relocation / the provision of accommodation

The closing date for the role is 10 January 2027. Interviews will take place on 22 January 2027 at the Faculty of Law.
