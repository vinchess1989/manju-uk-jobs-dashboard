Marketing Coordinator

Salary: £28,000 to £30,000 + approximately £3,000 annual bonus

Location: Cambridge

Working arrangement: Full time | Hybrid working available following successful completion of office based training and probation




About the Opportunity

Insignia Search is delighted to be supporting an established international technology business in Cambridge with the appointment of a Marketing Assistant.




This is a fantastic opportunity for a graduate or someone at the beginning of their career who is looking to develop their skills within a professional and supportive marketing environment.




Our client is looking for someone who is organised, enthusiastic, has excellent communication skills and enjoys working with people, information and processes.




You don't necessarily need a marketing degree or previous marketing experience. We're equally interested in candidates from business administration, events, customer service or commercial support backgrounds who demonstrate the right attitude and willingness to learn.




The Role

Working alongside an experienced marketing team, you will support a variety of marketing and commercial activities across the UK and Ireland.




Your responsibilities will include:

Supporting the coordination of marketing campaigns, webinars, conferences and exhibitions
Processing incoming enquiries and marketing leads, ensuring accurate information is recorded within the CRM system
Researching companies, contacts and potential business opportunities
Assisting with written content for email campaigns, LinkedIn and other marketing communications
Maintaining marketing databases, spreadsheets and campaign records
Supporting event planning, administration and coordination
Helping monitor campaign activity and prepare basic reports
Working closely with colleagues across marketing and sales to ensure activities run smoothly
Ensuring all communications and marketing materials are accurate and professionally presented




About You

We're looking for someone who:

Has excellent written and verbal communication skills
Is highly organised with strong attention to detail
Enjoys working with information, spreadsheets and administrative processes
Is confident using Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, Word and PowerPoint
Can manage multiple tasks and prioritise effectively
Has a positive attitude and enjoys working as part of a team
Is curious, proactive and genuinely interested in developing a career in marketing
Is comfortable working in a structured environment where accuracy and consistency are important




A degree in marketing, business or a related subject would be welcome but is not essential.




Previous experience in marketing, events, administration or commercial support would also be advantageous.




A driving licence is desirable but not essential.




What's on Offer?

Basic salary of £28,000 to £30,000 per annum
Additional bonus of approximately £3,000
Hybrid working following successful completion of initial office based training and probation
Structured training and ongoing professional development
Supportive and collaborative working environment
Exposure to marketing activities within an international business
Flexible core working hours
Generous pension scheme
Office parking
Company lunches and team activities
Genuine opportunities to develop your skills and progress your career




Why Apply?

This is an excellent opportunity to gain practical marketing experience within an established international business.




You'll receive structured training, work alongside experienced colleagues and develop a broad understanding of marketing operations, communications, events and commercial activities.




We are particularly interested in hearing from individuals who are reliable, detail oriented and enthusiastic about learning.




Interested?




If you're looking to begin or develop your career in marketing and would welcome the opportunity to join a supportive and professional team in Cambridge, we'd love to hear from you.
