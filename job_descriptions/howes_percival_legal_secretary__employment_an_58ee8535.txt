About The Role

We have an exciting opportunity for someone with a secretarial background to join our Employment and Corporate / Commercial / Banking Team in Cambridge as a Legal Secretary.

This role would be perfect for someone who is currently working as a Secretary, PA or any similar position.

Some of your duties will include:


Support with reformatting documents, numbering and inserting docs.
Willing to adapt to new ways of working.
Diary management for the team.
Managing expenses for the team.
Assist with billing clients including the preparation of WIP summaries and billing schedules.
Assisting with file opening and closing and file administration.
Preparing hard copy and e-bundles for employment tribunals and distribution of the same.
Liaising with clients and Counsel Clerks re case management and billing.
Preparing hard copy documents for CCB.
Some typing (letters and attendance notes).
General PA duties.


A little about you:


Proficient with Microsoft Office.
Be results focused and driven to deliver.
A good eye for detail.
Good organisation and time management abilities.
Excellent team player.
Motivated with a strong commitment to learning.
Prior experience within a law firm would be advantageous.
