As a Contracts Administrator (FTC, 1 year), you will play an important role in supporting the smooth delivery of customer projects. Working closely with Project Coordinators and the wider Operations team, you will provide administrative support throughout the project lifecycle, respond to customer queries and ensure that project documentation, systems and reporting are kept accurate and up to date. You’ll also act as a key point of contact for customers following order placement, so strong communication, attention to detail and the ability to manage competing priorities will be important.

Responsibilities

Provide administrative support to Project Coordinators and the wider Operations team.
Prepare and issue project documentation and maintain accurate records.
Respond to customer queries by phone and email.
Support order and project administration from order through to completion.
Raise purchase orders, service tickets and other project-related requests.
Support reporting and highlight any actions or issues requiring attention.

Qualifications

Previous experience in an administrative, contracts, project support or customer service role.
Experience communicating with customers, suppliers or business partners by telephone and email.
Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities.
Good attention to detail and a methodical approach to administration.
Confident written and verbal communication skills.
The ability to work effectively in a busy, deadline-driven environment.
Good IT skills and confidence using Microsoft Office and business systems.
GCSEs, or equivalent, including Maths and English.

About Us

About DOVISTA

DOVISTA is a leading European provider of vertical windows and exterior doors. With 13 distinctive brands and approximately 7,000 employees across 10 markets, the Group is locally strong and internationally connected, driven by a shared purpose to bring daylight and fresh air into people’s everyday lives.

 

Each brand has its own story, expertise, and strengths, yet all work to improve everyday living through quality solutions that meet our customers’ needs and shape homes and buildings that connect people with the world outside.

 

DOVISTA is built on a heritage rooted in Danish design, an entrepreneurial mindset and industry knowledge dating back to 1941. The company is part of the VKR Group and is wholly owned by VKR Holding A/S, a family- and foundation-owned company that includes a grant-giving Employee Foundation. Together, the VKR Group employs approximately 17,600 people in 38 countries.
