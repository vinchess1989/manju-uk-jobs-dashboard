What are we looking for?

We are currently looking for a Finance Administrator to join our Finance team based at our Head Office in Cambridge.

The Finance Administrator is required to work closely with the wider Finance Team to help manage and process a high volume of financial transactions. The Finance Administrator responsibilities include maintaining financial records and updating Finance systems with regards to daily transactions.




Key Responsibilities

Responsible for the daily banking and reconciliation of the business unit bank accounts.
To assist in student Pre-Arrival statement production.
Processing student refunds, following procedures in line with agreed SLA’s.
To liaise directly with fee payers & sponsors regarding student account queries.
To build effective working relationships with Centre Staff, Sales and Admissions Teams, and the Central Finance Team in order to quickly and courteously resolve queries.
To seek out opportunities for continuous improvement across the business in respect of existing finance processes.
Identify and post unallocated cash receipts in line with existing KPI’s
Process Domestic/International payments via online banking.
Reconcile finance systems to bank statements.
Maintain accurate financial spreadsheets.
Monitor shared mailboxes.
To undertake other duties not specifically stated above, which from time to time are necessary for the effective performance of the business without altering the nature or level of responsibility involved.

Please note that interviews for shortlisted candidates and any potential job offers may be in place before the application end date. We recommend that, if interested in any of the roles, you apply as soon as possible.




Person Specification

Skills

Strong focus on customer service.
Excellent communication skills by phone and email.
Knowledge of Microsoft Office applications.
Problem-solving abilities.
Accuracy and attention to detail.
Can prioritise conflicting demands.
Remains balanced, rational and courteous in dealing with others at all times.
Good team player.




What does Cambridge Education Group do?

Cambridge Education Group is an international education provider that offers a range of services such as Pathway and Pre University Programs, Pre University Education, Preparatory education through foundations and master’s programmes, English Language courses, University application guidance along with creating Internship opportunities.
