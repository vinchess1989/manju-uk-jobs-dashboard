Team Assistant - Central London
Salary: £35,000-£38,000 Full‑time | Permanent
Hybrid working conditions

The Firm A long‑established, growing law firm ranked within the top 200 in England. Known for its modern, forward‑thinking approach, the firm is recognised as a leader in its specialist practice areas. Their ethos centres on delivering exceptional client service through expertise, strong relationships and professionalism.
  
The Role A fantastic opportunity to join a high‑performing legal team in a busy, client‑facing support role. The Team Assistant will act as a workflow manager, coordinating document production, file management and administrative support across the department. You’ll develop a strong understanding of client needs and play a key part in ensuring seamless support for fee‑earners.
  
Key Responsibilities
Administrative Support
Manage electronic diaries and ensure solicitors are prepared for daily meetings
Arrange meetings, conference rooms, refreshments and liaise with attendees
Coordinate travel bookings
Handle photocopying, printing, couriers and outgoing correspondence
Oversee file opening/closing and general record‑keeping
Maintain systems to ensure tasks are completed within required timeframes
Support marketing activities, including submissions, pitches and presentations
Communication
Manage internal and external calls, ensuring timely message handling
Monitor emails/post and redirect client correspondence appropriately
Liaise with business support teams on behalf of solicitors
Coordinate planned absences and arrange cover where needed
Client Relationship Management
Become familiar with key clients and contacts
Maintain and update client information on internal systems
Handle basic client queries and general administration
Arrange client meetings
Document Production
Check and quality‑assure documents produced by the support team
Type urgent or confidential correspondence
Produce and maintain Excel spreadsheets
Financial Administration
Support solicitors with daily time recording
Assist with billing, credit control and financial reporting
Request cheques, bank transfers and manage incoming payments
Support expense management and ensure correct client billing
  Team Responsibilities
Provide support to colleagues when capacity allows
Pick up calls for team members when they are away from their desk
Work collaboratively with other legal and support departments
Cover fee‑earners when their usual assistant is absent
Undertake any additional duties as required
  Person Specification
Typing speed of at least 60 wpm
Strong MS Office skills (Word, Outlook, Excel, PowerPoint)
Excellent telephone manner and communication skills
Highly organised with strong attention to detail
Calm under pressure and able to meet tight deadlines
Proactive, dependable and solution‑focused
Approachable, enthusiastic and able to build trust quickly

Competitive salary (£35-£38K)

Annual discretionary bonus
Reduced gym membership
Discount vouchers
Season Ticket Loan
