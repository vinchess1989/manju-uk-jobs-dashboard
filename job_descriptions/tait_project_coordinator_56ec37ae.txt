TAIT partners with artists, brands, IP holders and place makers to bring culture-defining, never-before-seen experiences to life. With a legacy of innovation spanning over 45 years, TAIT has grown from pioneering in rock ‘n’ roll concert staging to setting the global standard for extraordinary live events and experiences through cutting-edge technology, precision engineering, and creative design. TAIT’s 20 global offices have developed iconic productions and experiences in over 30 countries, all seven continents, and even outer space for renowned performers, theme parks, exhibits, and venues across the globe, including partnerships with Taylor Swift, Cirque Du Soleil, Royal Opera House, Nike, NASA, Bloomberg, Google, Beyoncé, and The Olympics

Position Purpose

As a member of the Project Management Team, this position provides daily support to the project lead and project team members for projects as assigned. Will develop an understanding of the overall operations of the company. Areas of responsibility and interaction include the production process, the complexity of internal team/client/vendor relationships, meeting and event set up and coordination including meeting note process (taking, retaining, communicating), and various administrative tasks and duties. The individual will gain hands-on experience in an ever-growing and changing industry while being exposed to a variety of corporate clients. Works with internal groups to plan and execute programs; serves as a communication conduit – verbal and written.

Responsibilities


Maintain calendars and production schedules, coordinating internal and external resources and attendees for project-related meetings
Maintain project related files in Google Drive, Box, Microsoft SharePoint, and Slack
Coordinate the distribution of production and creative documentation as it relates to assigned projects.
File and archive documents as needed.
Organize expense reports, purchase requisitions, and submit proper documentation as directed.
Coordinate with internal resources (Designers, Tech, and Production) to ensure that all work is completed efficiently and on schedule.
Ensure prep, packaging, delivery, and distribution of project-related deliverables, documents, information, and materials as directed.
Conduct project-related research.
Participate in the overall office operation, including providing relief at the front desk, setting up/coordinating internal events for all employees, etc.


Position Requirements


High School Diploma or G.E.D. equivalency
General office experience
Production experience in a similar industry
Demonstrates ability to take accurate and complete written meeting notes.
Strong computer skills with the ability and desire to learn new technologies: Google Suite, Microsoft Suite, and/ or Keynote
Proven communication and presentation skills written, verbal, and numerical. Includes knowing when to ask for help or direction and initiating that communication.
Ability to multitask in a creative open office environment.
Detail-oriented, adaptable, and able to meet stringent deadlines with a high level of initiative, follow through, and self-motivation
Ability to prioritize work assignments and focus on most critical tasks or know when to ask what to prioritize.
Ability to work independently and as a team player.
Ability to use independent judgement and sound decision-making skills.
Must possess the ability to effectively perform research from a wide variety of outside sources with only minimal guidance.
