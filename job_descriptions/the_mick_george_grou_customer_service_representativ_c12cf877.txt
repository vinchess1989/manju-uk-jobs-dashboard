Here at the Mick George Group, we are currently recruiting a Customer Service Representative to join the team.

Location: Mick George Head Office - Huntingdon

Working Hours: Monday - Friday between 7am – 6pm on a shift pattern, Saturdays as and when required.

Pay: To be discussed

Benefits:

NEST Pension operated via salary sacrifice
Employee Assistance Programme
Multi-Discount platform

Recognition scheme
Long-service rewards

The role will heavily rely on a positive, customer service focused individual who thrives in fast paced environment. Making sure every customer has the ultimate customer experience from start to finish. Multi-tasking is key to being able to manage answering the phone, complete administrative, prioritise tasks all with high attention to detail. Previous Customer Service experience is essential to thrive in this position

Main Responsibilities

Be the first point of contact for all Mick George Customers building rapport with new and existing customers ensuring a positive customer experience from start to finish. Answer all incoming calls and emails from customers, have adaptable approach to each customer’s needs and requirements keeping them updated throughout the process. Be highly organised completing all administrative tasks in a timely manner with high attention to detail. Undertake all relevant training as and when necessary, keeping up to date on all Mick George Products and Services.

Skills/Qualifications

Ability to work in a high volume, fast paced office environment
Ability to work under pressure
Highly organised with a polite and confident telephone manner
Computer literate, with Microsoft Office knowledge
Driven and pro-active individual
Flexibility to get tasks completed as needed with excellent attention to detail
