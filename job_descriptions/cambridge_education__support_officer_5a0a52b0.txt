Who are we looking for?




We are currently looking for a new Support Officer to join our Retention team at Cambridge Education Group.




The role is on a full-time, permanent basis with the location in Cambridge, United Kingdom. We operate on a hybrid work pattern where 3 days per week can be worked from home.




CEG Online are passionate about learner engagement and care deeply about student progression and graduation success. Our Support Officers are dedicated to supporting and enhancing student experience. Working alongside our Student Experience Advisors to form a Student Support team, the Support Officers ensure that students receive a high level of response to their enquiries and that appropriate outreach is provided when they are in need.




Frequently the first point of call for most student enquiries, the Support Officers primary responsibility is to provide pastural support, identifying students’ needs and resolving them with guidance or connecting them with staff at our partner institutions, including academic teams and student support services (e.g. Wellbeing, Accessibility and Employability). Responding to enquiries via email and CRM, within target SLA’s and resolving issues either directly or through escalation within targeted resolution time periods is core to the role.




Key responsibilities as a Support Officer:




Daily resolution of inbound student enquiries, within targeted response SLA’s.
Monitoring of student performance and reporting via data entry into class rosters.
Outreach to students via phone, text, WhatsApp and Email to discuss and resolve class performance issues.
Collaborate with academic and partner teams, to resolve complex cases and agree strategies for reducing future occurrences.
Provide core guidance to students on subjects such as:
Navigating the online university systems
Provision of fundamental study skills including time management and developing a work/life balance
Programme structure and learning methodology
University policies and procedures
Study planning and course scheduling
Guidance on services available for Wellness, Mental Health, Employability and Accessibility
Concerns with finance funding.
Participate in online events with students (one-to-many and one-to-one), to provide study support guidance.
Record all interactions within our CRM systems (HubSpot), ensuring a high degree of accuracy and giving clear visibility of contact rates and student issues.




Key aspects we will be looking for:




Experience of providing excellent customer service via telephone and email
Experience of complying with established policies, procedures and timescales
Experience of working in a student-facing support role in FE or HE, particularly in a pastoral care context (Desirable)
Excellent interpersonal skills and ability to communicate effectively and confidentially, verbally and in writing
Excellent organisational skills
Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Office, in particular Word, Excel and Outlook, and experience of working with a wide variety of computer systems and packages
Ability to work under pressure, maintain attention to detail and prioritise workload in a busy office in order to meet deadlines
Ability to build rapport with a diverse range of colleagues and customers
