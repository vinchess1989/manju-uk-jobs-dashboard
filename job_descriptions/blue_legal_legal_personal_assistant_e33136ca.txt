We are recruiting for a Legal PA to join a successful and collaborative Private Department, providing high-quality administrative and organisational support to a team of lawyers.




This is a fantastic opportunity to join a forward-thinking firm that values its people, encourages professional development and offers a supportive working environment.




🔹 Key Responsibilities:




Providing comprehensive PA and administrative support to fee earners, ensuring the smooth running of their day-to-day activities.
Managing complex diaries, coordinating meetings and organising travel arrangements.
Supporting the full client and matter lifecycle, including file openings, client onboarding, compliance checks and file closures.
Assisting with legal billing, invoicing, credit control and financial administration.
Acting as a key point of contact for clients, fee earners and internal departments, handling correspondence and enquiries professionally.
Supporting business development activities, including coordinating client events, meetings and maintaining CRM records.
Liaising with document production teams and other internal departments to ensure efficient delivery of legal support services.




🔹 About You:




Previous experience gained in an administrative role supporting a team in a similar professional services environment (ideally legal).
Strong time management skills: can juggle several tasks at any given time, plan ahead, and respond positively and willingly to re-prioritise and adapt to changes in requirements.
