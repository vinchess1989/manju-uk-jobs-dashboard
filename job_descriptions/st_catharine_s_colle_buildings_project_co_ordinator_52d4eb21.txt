This is an exciting new role within the Buildings and Maintenance Team as we continue our strategic journey to enhance our student accommodation, preserve our historic buildings and implement our road map to net zero.

It is a proactive, instrumental role working with the Head of Buildings and Maintenance to help deliver both small and capital expenditure projects on schedule and within budget while meeting all regulatory and quality requirements.

This role would suit someone who is highly organised, able to multitask, computer literate, comfortable working to deadlines, and interested in supporting construction projects.




Primary Responsibilities:

Document control
Scheduling
Stakeholder communication
Financial tracking and processing
Compliance and regulation




The above is not an exhaustive list of duties.




This is a two-year fixed term appointment (subject to a 3-month probation period) with the potential to becoming permanent thereafter.

Competitive salary range of £33,000 to £38,000 pro rata, depending on skills and experience.

This role is 20 hours per week, over 5 days a week. The role is based on-site and there is flexibility on specific hours of work.
