The role

As part of our Executive Assistant team, you’ll provide support across our Business Units, working with people at all levels.

This is a varied role that includes full diary management for seniors and arranging travel for our busy project teams – no two days look the same. You’ll be given the autonomy to manage your own workload whilst working collaboratively with fellow EAs on a range of tasks and providing cover when needed.

This is an office-based role with some flexibility to work from home.




Key Responsibilities

Provide strategic calendar management and time planning support for seniors. Schedule complex meetings across multiple time zones.
Coordinate a variety of meetings which may include minute and action taking, supported by AI tools such as Copilot.
Attend senior leadership meetings and work alongside the team to progress actions.
Organise and attend leadership team offsites, ensuring materials and logistics are in place.
Facilitate, record and share internal meetings.
Manage international travel arrangements in line with our travel policy.
Organise conference attendance and associated travel arrangements.
Liaise directly with clients when arranging meetings, building a strong working relationship.
Arrange tours of our facilities and be on hand to support visits to our site.
Perform general administrative tasks such as submitting expense reports, pulling together presentations and general correspondence.
Process statements of work and obtain signatures for contracts.
Manage the induction and onboarding process for new employees.
Work with your line manager to ensure smooth running of the business, including coordinating cover during periods of leave.




Requirements

Relevant experience within a similar role.
Experience supporting senior stakeholders in a fast-paced environment.
Proficiency in using MS365 and Microsoft Teams.
Comfortable using, or willing to learn, AI tools such as Copilot to support administrative tasks.
Experience coordinating complex international travel.
Must be able to prioritise work effectively and communicate with key stakeholders regarding deadlines.
Strong attention to detail and accuracy
Complete reliability handling confidential, sometimes sensitive information.
A proactive team player with a strong desire to muck in where required.
