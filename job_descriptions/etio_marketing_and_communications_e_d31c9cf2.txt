About The Role

Job Description

Job Title: Marketing & Communications Executive

Department: Etio – National Centre for Arts Education

Hours: 37.5 hours per week (Flexible)

Contract Type: Permanent

Location: UK Remote (Must have the right to work in the UK without restrictions)

Salary: £29,750 - £35,000 GBP pa

Travel Requirements: Occasional travel within the UK may be requested

Overview Of The Role

The Marketing & Communications Executive plays an important role in supporting the delivery of marketing, communications, and engagement activities across the National Centre for Arts Education (NCAE) programme.

Reporting directly to the Marketing & Communications Manager, you will help bring the NCAE's work to life through engaging content, effective digital communications, campaign delivery, and stakeholder engagement activities. Working closely with colleagues, partners, educators, Music Hubs, and cultural organisations, you will support the promotion of programmes, events, resources, and sector initiatives that champion arts education across England.

This is an exciting opportunity for a creative, organised, and digitally-savvy marketing professional looking to develop their career while contributing to a nationally significant programme that supports excellence, access, and innovation in arts education.

This is perfect for someone who is passionate to help us fulfil our mission to "Elevate Education, Everywhere."

Key Responsibilities

Your core responsibilities include, but are not limited to:


Support the delivery of the NCAE marketing and communications plan, helping to promote programme activities, resources, events, and impact.
Create engaging content for a range of channels, including websites, newsletters, social media platforms, blogs, case studies, and marketing materials.
Assist in the planning and delivery of digital marketing campaigns to increase awareness, engagement, and participation across NCAE programmes.
Maintain and update website content, ensuring information remains accurate, accessible, and engaging for users.
Coordinate and schedule social media activities, monitoring engagement and helping to grow online audiences.
Support the production of marketing collateral, presentations, reports, event materials, and stakeholder communications.
Assist with the promotion and delivery of events, webinars, workshops, and sector engagement activities.
Monitor marketing performance and engagement metrics, contributing to reporting and continuous improvement activities.
Work collaboratively with programme teams, partners, and stakeholders to gather content, success stories, and impact evidence.
Ensure all communications and marketing activities align with NCAE and Etio brand guidelines and organisational values.


Expected Behaviours And Working Style


Make every day count – Take pride in delivering high-quality work that contributes to meaningful outcomes for the arts education sector.
Lead the way – Bring creativity, enthusiasm, and fresh ideas to communications and marketing activities, continually looking for ways to improve engagement.
Deliver with empathy – Create inclusive, accessible, and audience-focused communications that build positive relationships and reflect diverse perspectives.


Role Deliverables


Deliver engaging and high-quality marketing and communications content that supports NCAE objectives.
Maintain an active and professional digital presence across key communications channels.
Support successful marketing campaigns, events, and stakeholder engagement activities.
Contribute to increased awareness, participation, and engagement across NCAE programmes and initiatives.
Provide accurate performance reporting and insights that support continuous improvement and informed decision-making


Essential

Skills, Experience, and Qualifications:


Experience creating visual content using tools such as Canva, Adobe Creative Suite, or similar design software.
Understanding of analytics tools and experience monitoring marketing performance.
Experience in a marketing, communications, digital content, or related role.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with strong attention to detail.
Experience creating content for digital platforms, including websites, social media, newsletters, and marketing materials.
Knowledge of content management systems (CMS), email marketing platforms, and social media management tools.
Good understanding of digital marketing and social media best practice.
Experience using Microsoft 365 applications, including Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Outlook, and Teams.
Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.
Creative thinking and the ability to present information in engaging and accessible ways.
Experience working collaboratively with internal and external stakeholders.
A proactive approach with a willingness to learn, develop, and contribute to team success.


Desirable


Experience working within education, arts and culture, public sector, charity, or professional services environments.
Knowledge of arts education, Music Hubs, cultural learning programmes, or the wider education sector.
Degree-level qualification or equivalent professional experience in Marketing, Communications, Public Relations, Digital Media, Arts Management, or a related discipline.
