The Opportunity

As a Personal Travel Expert, you will be the owner of the client relationship within an Audley Travel retail store, delivering a seamless end-to-end experience from first interaction through to post-trip engagement and future travel planning.




You will combine proactive sales, relationship management, and travel expertise to convert retail footfall into long-term client relationships, while working collaboratively with Country Specialists to deliver a premium, personalised travel experience.




Key responsibilities include:




Sales & Performance

Proactively engage retail customers and convert footfall into high-quality opportunities
Generate demand and build a strong forward-looking pipeline
Design and sell holidays, taking ownership of pace, follow-up and booking confidence




Client Experience

Own the end-to-end client relationship from first interaction through to post-trip engagement
Deliver a seamless client journey aligned to the Audley Way
Design and sell fully transactable holidays independently within defined product areas (e.g. cruise, beach and selected short-haul journeys), taking ownership of pace, confidence and booking completion
Collaborate on tailor-made Audley holidays that require Country Specialist expertise, co-creating more complex itineraries while retaining full ownership of the client relationship
Use sound judgement to determine when to lead independently versus when to involve specialist expertise, ensuring the right approach for each client
Build long-term client relationships to drive repeat business and future travel planning
Resolve client issues and ensure consistently high standards of service at all stages




Collaboration

Work in partnership with Country Specialists to co-create itineraries where required
Retain ownership of the client relationship while collaborating to deliver destination expertise
Contribute to a one team environment, supporting colleagues to maintain continuity for clients




Retail Experience & Brand

Create engaging, personalised interactions with all store visitors
Inspire travel through effective storytelling and questioning
Act as a brand ambassador within the retail environment
Support in-store events and client engagement activity




We offer both full-time and part-time opportunities with flexible shift patterns across the week.




Full-Time: 37 hours per week, worked across 5 days out of 7, with a base salary of £29,000 plus commission.
Part-Time: Minimum 22 hours per week, worked across 3 days out of 7, with a pro-rata salary based on contracted hours.
Both roles include evening and weekend shifts as part of a rota, and colleagues will typically enjoy one full weekend off each month.




Store opening hours: Monday to Saturday: 9:00am–6:00pm | Sunday: 10:30am–5:00pm




What You’ll Bring to the Team

You’ll bring experience in a sales-driven, client-facing role, specifically within travel or a premium service environment - alongside strong consultative selling skills, you’ll have the ability to convert opportunities and build meaningful relationships with clients.




Additional experience required:

Ability to manage end-to-end client relationships and deliver high levels of service
Commercial awareness with a focus on both immediate results and long-term client value
Confident working in a fast-paced retail environment and engaging customers proactively
Strong communication and collaboration skills
Passion for delivering exceptional, personalised client experiences
Good understanding of Office 365 including MS Word, Excel, SharePoint and Outlook
Embodies the Audley values of being positive, being the difference, being one team and being passionate about travel
