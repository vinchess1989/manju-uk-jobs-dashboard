Part-time Virtual Events Coordinator

Leading Global Research & Advisory Organisation

Fully Remote

£14.60 + holiday pay
12-month temporary contract
1 January 2027 -31 December 2027
20 hours per week (4 hours per day, Monday–Friday)



The Opportunity

We are looking for an organised and proactive Part-Time Virtual Events Coordinator to join a leading global research and advisory organisation.

This is a great opportunity for someone with experience in event coordination, webinars or virtual conferences who enjoys working in a fast-paced environment and managing the practical details that bring events together.

You will support the planning and delivery of virtual events from registration through to live execution, working closely with internal teams and programme partners to ensure everything runs smoothly and attendees receive a positive experience.

Key Responsibilities


Coordinate the planning and delivery of virtual events, webinars and conferences.
Set up and manage event registrations and attendee information.
Liaise with internal stakeholders and programme partners to coordinate event requirements and timelines.
Support partners with virtual event platforms, registration processes and available resources.
Assist with live event delivery, ensuring everything runs smoothly.
Identify ways to improve attendee engagement and the overall event experience.
Gather feedback and suggest improvements to event processes and workflows.
Work with wider teams to support consistent and efficient event delivery.
About You


2–4 years' experience in event coordination, conference administration or virtual event delivery.
Previous experience supporting or coordinating webinars, virtual events or conferences.
Strong organisational skills and excellent attention to detail.
Experience coordinating registrations, stakeholders and event logistics.
Comfortable using virtual event platforms and registration systems.
Confident managing multiple priorities and working independently.
A proactive, hands-on approach with good communication skills.
