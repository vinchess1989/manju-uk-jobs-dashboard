Full job description

Our well established client is looking for an organised and reliable Print Administrator to join their busy team. The successful candidate will provide administrative support, ensuring that customer orders, production paperwork and schedules are processed accurately and efficiently.




Key Responsibilities

Process and manage customer print orders.
Prepare and maintain production paperwork and job sheets.
Update internal systems with order and production information.
Liaise with customers, suppliers and internal departments.
Monitor job progress and ensure deadlines are met.
Maintain accurate records of stock, materials and completed jobs.
Assist with scheduling print work and coordinating production activities.
Respond to telephone and email enquiries.
Provide general administrative support to the print department.




This is a busy role and would suit someone who has an attention to detail who can look over print and ensure that it is correct before printing.

The company offer a base salary of upto £27,500 and excellent benefits. The role is Monday to Friday.
