About the Role


We're looking for an organised, enthusiastic and detail-oriented individual to join a busy finance team based in Ely as an Accounts Administrator.

This is a great opportunity for someone looking to start their career in finance, particularly recent graduates or individuals who are keen to develop their skills and gain their first experience within an accounts environment. No previous finance or accounts experience is required, as full training and support will be provided.

We're looking for someone with a positive attitude, excellent communication and organisational skills, and a genuine willingness to learn. You'll play an important role in supporting the day-to-day finance function, helping to maintain accurate financial records, process information and build relationships with internal teams and external stakeholders.

Key Responsibilities






Assist with reconciling account statements and investigating any discrepancies.




Support monthly reconciliations across a range of accounts, using both manual and automated processes.




Help identify, record and report reconciliation findings, ensuring financial information is accurate and up to date.




Assist with setting up payments through online banking systems.




Review financial transactions and reports, checking calculations and data entries for accuracy.




Follow established financial policies, procedures and relevant requirements.




Respond to queries and provide support to internal teams, customers and external stakeholders.




Build and maintain positive working relationships with colleagues and external contacts.




Support the wider finance team with general administrative duties and ongoing tasks.


About You






No previous finance or accounts experience required




Highly organised, with the ability to manage tasks and prioritise your workload.




Strong attention to detail and a methodical approach to work.




Excellent written and verbal communication skills.




Good numerical and analytical skills, with confidence working with figures and data.




Basic knowledge of Microsoft Office, particularly Excel, Word and PowerPoint.




A proactive attitude with a willingness to learn, develop and take on new responsibilities.




Confident asking questions and communicating with people at different levels.




Reliable, enthusiastic and able to work effectively as part of a team.


The Opportunity


This is an excellent opportunity for a recent graduate or someone at the beginning of their career who is looking to gain valuable experience within finance and accounts.

You'll receive hands on training and exposure to areas including account reconciliations, payment processing, financial administration and working with financial data. It's a great starting point for someone looking to build their knowledge, develop new skills and progress their career within finance.

If you're organised, have great communication skills, enjoy working with numbers and are keen to learn, we'd love to hear from you.
