About the opportunity

Via is partnering with a growing UK business looking for an Operations Assistant to support its daily administrative and operational activities.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone organised, reliable and detail-oriented. No previous operations experience is required, and candidates from administration, customer service or other transferable backgrounds are encouraged to apply.




What you'll be doing

Support daily business operations and administrative tasks
Maintain accurate records, spreadsheets and internal systems
Assist with data entry, reporting and document management
Coordinate tasks, deadlines and follow-ups across teams
Respond to internal and external enquiries
Identify issues and help improve operational processes




What we're looking for

Strong organisational skills and attention to detail
Good written and verbal communication skills
Basic Microsoft Office and computer skills
Ability to manage priorities and meet deadlines
A proactive, reliable and professional approach
Confidence working independently in a remote environment




Previous experience in administration, data entry, customer service or business support is beneficial but not essential.
