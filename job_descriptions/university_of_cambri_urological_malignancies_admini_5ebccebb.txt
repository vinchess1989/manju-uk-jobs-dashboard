The Urological Malignancies Virtual Institute is a dynamic, multidisciplinary programme with around 100 members based in Cambridge. We are looking for an Administration Assistant to support the delivery of our varied study portfolio in urological cancers.

This is a varied and busy role working on several activities at any given time and supporting a number of different people. There is contact with a wide range of internal and external people and the role requires an organised and methodical approach as well as tact and diplomacy.

The role also includes a range of other administrative tasks including maintenance of records and distribution lists, updating webpages, communications support, providing support for committees and dealing with expense claims and enquiries.

Informal enquiries may be made to Mrs Carley Batley, the Urological Malignancies Programme Manager in the first instance (cb2147@cam.ac.uk). Further information on our group can be found at our website: https://www.cambridge-urologicalmalignancies.org.uk.

Fixed-term: The funds for this post are available until 31 March 2028 in the first instance.

Click the 'Apply' button below to register an account with our recruitment system (if you have not already) and apply online.

Please ensure that you outline how you match the criteria for the post and why you are applying for this role on the online application form.

Please include details of your referees, including email address and phone number, one of which must be your most recent line manager.

Closing date for applications is 17 November 2026.

Interview dates to be confirmed.

Please quote reference RE51492 on your application and in any correspondence about this vacancy.
