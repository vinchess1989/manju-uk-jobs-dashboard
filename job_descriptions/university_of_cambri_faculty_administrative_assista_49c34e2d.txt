The Faculty of Classics invites applications for the position of Faculty Administrative Assistant. The Faculty of Classics is a leading teaching and research institution in the University of Cambridge, comprising more than 60 members of staff, around 280 undergraduate and around 80 postgraduate students.

We are seeking an Administrator to join our small, busy team to play a vital role in the administrative support for the teaching and research activities of the academic staff and students of the Faculty of Classics as well as providing general administrative support to the Faculty. The role is wide-ranging and involves a significant amount of contact with members of the Faculty and of the general public.

Applicants will have excellent interpersonal and organisational skills, very good IT skills, an excellent telephone manner, an eye for detail and the ability to work in a team environment. Applicants will be able to work independently to tight deadlines, be willing to learn new skills and will be able to use their initiative and judgement. Applicants must be able to prioritise and work well under pressure. Applicants will be able to deal sympathetically and effectively with a wide range of people at all levels with tact and diplomacy, observing the need for confidentiality where appropriate.

Applicants should be educated to at least A Level, grades A-C or equivalent, or equivalent level of practical experience. They must have good knowledge of MS Office, including Word, Excel and Outlook.

Experience of working in a busy office, within Higher Education and knowledge of the Classical World is desirable.

The closing date for applications is midnight (GMT) on Monday 4 January 2027. Interviews are planned to take place on Thursday 14 January 2027, subject to change.

Click the 'Apply' button below to register an account with our recruitment system (if you have not already) and apply online.

Informal enquiries about the role are welcomed and should be directed to Lina Undicini at pu1000@cam.ac.uk

For questions on the application process, please contact the School's HR Team on sahhr@admin.cam.ac.uk

Please quote reference GE51675 on your application and in any correspondence about this vacancy.

The University actively supports equality, diversity and inclusion and encourages applications from all sections of society.

The University has a responsibility to ensure that all employees are eligible to live and work in the UK.
