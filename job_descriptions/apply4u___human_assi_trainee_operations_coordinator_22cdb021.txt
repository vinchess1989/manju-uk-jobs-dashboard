Trainee Operations Coordinator – Remote UK | Training Provided

Salary: £27,000 – £32,000 per year

Location: Remote, UK

Job Type: Full-time | Permanent

Training: Full training provided

About the Role

Apply4U is recruiting on behalf of our client for a Trainee Operations Coordinator to join a growing team on a fully remote basis.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone looking to build a career in business operations, coordination and administration. You do not need extensive previous operations experience, as training and ongoing support will be provided.

As a Trainee Operations Coordinator, you will help coordinate day-to-day business activities, maintain accurate information, monitor tasks and support different teams to ensure operations run efficiently.

Key Responsibilities

Support the coordination of daily business operations.
Assist with planning and organising operational activities.
Maintain accurate records, reports and business information.
Update internal systems and databases.
Monitor tasks, deadlines and outstanding actions.
Coordinate information between different teams.
Assist with preparing operational reports and documentation.
Respond to routine queries and provide administrative support.
Identify and escalate issues that require additional support.
Help ensure processes are followed consistently.
Support improvements to administrative and operational procedures.

About You

The ideal candidate will be:

Organised and able to manage multiple tasks.
Reliable with good attention to detail.
Confident using computers and online business systems.
A clear and professional communicator.
Comfortable working independently in a remote environment.
Interested in business operations and coordination.
Willing to learn and develop new skills.
Able to prioritise work and meet deadlines.

Previous experience in administration, customer service, business support or operations is beneficial but not essential.

What’s on Offer

Salary of £27,000 – £32,000 per year.
Fully remote UK working.
Full-time permanent position.
Full training and ongoing support.
Career development opportunities.
Opportunity to gain practical operations experience.
Supportive professional working environment.
