PROPERTY SALES PROGRESSOR - CUSTOMER SERVICE

Salary: £28,237.00 + Bonus (circa £32,440.00 OTE)

Location: St. Ives, Cambridgeshire

Ambitious and driven? Bags of personality? Want to work with a growing team with the support to get things done?

Experience of working within either the Property industry and/or within a Sales or Customer Service role?

Come and join in the fun at Move With Us!

Benefits

Competitive Salary of £28,237.00 + bonus (circa £32,440.00 OTE), a friendly and modern office in beautiful surroundings, 25 Days Holiday (Plus 8 Days Public Holiday), Option To Buy Or Sell Holiday, Company Pension, Life Assurance, Enhanced Maternity, Paternity & Adoption Pay, Free Conveyancing Legals, GP 24-hour service, Retail Discounts Plus Many More!

What You’ll Be Doing

You’ll be a joining us as a fundamental member of the Property Sales Progressor team with activities as follows:


Managing your own caseload of properties to a high standard
Liaising with, looking after, and constantly updating our clients, beneficiaries and business partners with the progress of each transaction
Chasing estate agents and mortgage offers, checking chains and encouraging buyers and clients to do what they need to do
Troubleshooting and customer care, ensuring transactions are as smooth as possible

Requirements

And now for the nitty gritty:

Although this post does not have any pre-requisite qualifications, we do still need candidates to have previous work experience in either the property industry, or within an office environment in sales or customer services, as a foundation to build on from.

You’ll need to have an engaging personality, be customer focused, keen to build relationships with your clients, and have lots of confidence and enthusiasm.

You will enjoy solving problems, thinking on your feet, and thrive in a busy environment where you will be afforded every opportunity to shine!

As such you will need to show commitment and have ambition to succeed, as once you are through the door and up to speed, the opportunities to progress and develop are almost limitless for those who want them. You will also need to be well versed and experienced with Microsoft Office, especially Excel, as this is used heavily within this department.

Whilst able to operate independently, you will also need to be a team player and willing to support your colleagues as needed to deliver results. As such your bonus will be influenced by the success of the team and not just individual performance.
