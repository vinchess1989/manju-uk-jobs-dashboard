Hours: Monday to Friday 40 hours a week, 8am-4pm, 52 weeks

Are you a well organised administrator who wants your work to make a real difference? Gretton School is looking for a Referrals Administrator to be the first point of contact for families and local authorities. You'll help autistic children and young people find a school where they can feel safe, supported and able to thrive.

Your new school

Gretton School welcomes autistic children and young people aged from 5-19 years old, as weekly boarders or as day learners.

Every learner at Gretton has a diagnosis of Autism, and the ability to learn at or above the level of their mainstream peers, but require a specialist setting in order to achieve their remarkable potential.

As autism specialists, we understand that consistent daily routines and staffing helps to keep anxiety at a minimum and ensures that our learners feel safe and confident in their surroundings.

We have a high staff to learner ratio and classes are small and personal - we know each of our learners really well and employ the best strategies to support them in their everyday school life.

A broad curriculum, an emphasis on holistic development, and the development of life and social skills, means that we are able to bring out the best in all our learners, and to provide them with opportunities to flourish.

At whatever stage a young person joins Gretton, we will ensure they are happy and prepared to make the most of the opportunities, responsibilities and experiences of life.

Your new role

As Referrals Administrator, you'll join the Referrals, Admissions and Transitions Team. Your main job will be triaging and assessing consultations from local authorities. This is a varied school admin role where you'll be managing the whole referral and admissions process, from the first enquiry to placement.
Your responsibilities will include:

Receiving and processing local authority referrals, and checking that all paperwork is complete and up to date
Reading EHCPs and supporting documents to make an initial assessment of suitability
Sending referrals and student papers to the Headteacher and relevant staff, and responding within 15 days on whether a placement is appropriate
Handling parent enquiries by email, phone and through the website with sensitivity and professionalism, and arranging visits
Organising and running parent visits and open afternoons, including student-led tours where appropriate
Keeping the Current Referrals log and Referrals List up to date, and projecting admissions to inform staffing, class groups and building plans
Scheduling admission assessments and issuing offer letters, contracts and placement information
Liaising with the Finance Department, solicitors and the Senior Leadership Team, including preparing tribunal packs and bundles when needed
Producing referral, admissions and marketing data for weekly updates and termly board and Governors' reports
Keeping student records and the Admissions Register accurate on SchoolPod / Arbor


What you'll need to succeed

Essential:

GCSE English and Maths (A*-C), Adult Literacy and Numeracy Level 2, NVQ Level 2 or an equivalent in a relevant area
Experience using administrative and clerical systems
Excellent listening, literacy and numeracy skills, and strong ICT skills including Microsoft Office
Confidence communicating with people at all levels, including parents, local authorities and senior leaders
The ability to follow instructions accurately while using sound judgement when needed
A team player who makes a positive contribution
A commitment to equal opportunities, inclusive education and the safeguarding and welfare of all students
The ability to keep appropriate professional relationships and boundaries
Evidence of continued professional development and a willingness to take on more responsibility

Desirable:

A-levels or further professional qualifications
Experience of working in a school
Experience in a referrals and admissions team

What you'll get in return

A salary of £26,450 - £27,200
A permanent, full-time role, Monday to Friday, 8am - 4pm (40 hours a week, 52 weeks a year)
Regular training, performance reviews and support with your professional development
A meaningful role at a specialist SEND school where you'll help shape every student's journey from the start
A warm, close-knit and supportive team where staff feel valued for their skills and ideas
A January 2027 start, or sooner if possible
