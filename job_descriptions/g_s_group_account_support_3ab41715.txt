Are you ready to start your career in a fast-paced, dynamic commercial sales environment? As an Account Support, you’ll be part of a motivated team, gaining hands-on experience in key account management, sales administration, and customer service. This role is perfect for someone who is organised, proactive, and eager to learn the ins and outs of commercial and sales operation within a large fresh produce business.

What You’ll Be Doing:


Complete administrative duties within the commercial team to support overall account strategy
Process and distribute sales orders accurately and on time, ensuring all updates and amendments are logged
Investigate and resolve EDI errors
Book haulage/transport and manage pricing in JDE
Communicate confirmed prices to customers where applicable
Set up and amend SKUs in internal and external systems
Liaise with customers and internal teams to maintain accurate forecasts
Support promotions, customer visits, product samples, and Brandbank listings
Maintain KPI trackers and assist with returns, rejections, shortages, and debit notes
Cover weekend order processing on a rota and provide holiday cover within the Account Support team
Work with the wider commercial team to improve processes and drive efficiencies
Assist with any additional tasks reasonably requested by your manager


What We’re Looking For:


Strong attention to detail and organisational skills
Proficient in the use of Microsoft packages particularly Excel
Good communication skills and a team-oriented mindset
Strong time management and problem-solving abilities
Be able to work using your own initiative.
Organisation and planning skills.
Have a “can-do” proactive approach to work.
Experience in sales or customer service is beneficial but not essential as we will offer training and development.


Why Join Us:

This is an exciting early careers opportunity where you’ll gain practical experience, learn from a talented team, and develop skills that will set you up for a successful career in commercial sales operations. You’ll be part of a supportive, energetic environment where your contributions really make a difference.
