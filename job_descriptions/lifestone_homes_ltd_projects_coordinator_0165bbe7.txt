About The Role

We are looking for an Investments Projects Coordinator to join our Asset Management team and play a key role in supporting the delivery of planned investment works across our homes and communities.

Working closely with Project Managers and Supervisors, you will provide essential administrative, scheduling and coordination support across a range of planned workstreams, including investment projects, insurance works and roofing programmes. You will act as a central point of contact for residents, contractors and internal teams, helping to ensure projects are delivered efficiently, safely and with a strong focus on customer satisfaction.

This is an excellent opportunity for an organised and proactive individual who enjoys working in a fast‑paced environment and wants to build a career within property services, asset management or planned maintenance.

Key Responsibilities


Provide administrative and logistical support across investment and planned works programmes
Support Project Managers and Supervisors with the coordination of works from planning through to completion
Organise and collate documentation, safety reports, specifications and project information
Maintain accurate records, job statuses and project information across company systems
Upload safety reports, scopes of work and Construction Phase Plans before works commence
Communicate with residents regarding upcoming, ongoing and outstanding works
Maintain accurate customer contact records and engagement logs
Update Asset Management records and project databases accurately
Process subcontractor quotations, purchase orders and invoices
Allocate works and issue relevant documentation to contractors and operatives
Coordinate planned works programmes and monitor project progress
Produce reports, meeting notes and project updates where required
Manage work in progress and ensure jobs are completed and closed within agreed timescales
Upload warranties, guarantees and completion records following project completion
Investigate and respond to customer enquiries, complaints and service issues
Support service improvements through customer and contractor feedback
Provide cover and support during periods of absence within the wider team


Our expectations


Planned works programmes organised and delivered efficiently
Accurate and timely project administration and record keeping
Effective management of work in progress and follow‑on works
High levels of customer satisfaction and communication
Accurate project information maintained across company systems
Purchase orders, invoices and contractor documentation processed correctly and on time
Strong support provided to Project Managers and Supervisors
Positive contribution to team performance and service delivery targets
Continuous improvement in administrative processes and project coordination activities
