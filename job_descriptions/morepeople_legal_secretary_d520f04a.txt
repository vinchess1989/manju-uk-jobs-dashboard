Legal Secretary

Location: Cambridgeshire

Salary: Negotiable

If you're organised, detail-focused and looking to build a career within a professional legal environment, this is an excellent opportunity.

You'll provide important secretarial and administrative support to Private Client fee earners, helping the team deliver an efficient, professional service to clients. You'll work closely with experienced colleagues and develop your knowledge of legal procedures and firm protocols along the way.

What's the role?

As Legal Secretary, you'll provide support with a varied range of administrative, secretarial and client-facing responsibilities.

The role would suit someone who is proactive, organised and keen to learn. You'll need excellent attention to detail, the ability to manage competing priorities and a calm, professional approach when working to deadlines.

You'll also have the opportunity to develop your technical knowledge through training and hands on experience, supported by senior colleagues and the wider team.

What will you be doing?

You'll be involved in a varied range of legal secretarial and administrative activities, including:

Providing typing, secretarial and administrative support to fee earners
Transcribing and preparing correspondence and legal documents using digital dictation, word processing and appropriate firm precedents.
Operating the firm's case management systems, including electronically storing and attaching documents to the relevant matters.
Maintaining client and matter files accurately and ensuring documents are filed promptly.
Supporting matters from inception through to closure and developing an understanding of the property transaction process.
Managing incoming and outgoing mail, including reviewing and clearing scanned mail.
Arranging appointments, meetings and travel, and maintaining fee earner diaries as required.
Providing professional and friendly support to clients both in person and over the telephone.
Assisting with compliance reports and other administrative requirements across the department.
Preparing and submitting enquiries to utility companies and local authorities.
Developing an understanding of the different forms and procedures involved in property transactions.
Completing general administrative duties including scanning, printing, photocopying and e-faxing.
Supporting colleagues across the wider team and assisting with other reasonable requests as required.

What are we looking for?

Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage competing priorities.
Excellent attention to detail, including spelling and punctuation.
Good computer literacy and working knowledge of Microsoft Office.
The ability to communicate effectively at all levels.
A calm and professional approach when working under pressure.
Good time management and the ability to plan work effectively to meet deadlines.
The ability to work collaboratively as part of a wider team.
A commitment to confidentiality and professional standards.

Previous secretarial experience and familiarity with digital dictation would be advantageous, but are not essential.

Why join?

This is an opportunity to join a team where attention to detail, collaboration, client service and a willingness to continually develop are valued.

You'll be supported in developing your skills and knowledge through hands on experience, internal training and professional development. Training may include legal secretary introductory courses, working within a law firm, relevant training and department specific sessions.
