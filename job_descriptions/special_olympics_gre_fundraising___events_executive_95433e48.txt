Job description

Bring people together through fundraising and events


Could you help supporters turn their enthusiasm for SOGB into fundraising, memorable events and lasting connections?


As Fundraising & Events Executive, you’ll develop our challenge and community fundraising activity and coordinate events and experiences with corporate partners. You’ll support people from their first enquiry through to their fundraising and event experience, while taking care of the details that make activity welcoming, inclusive and well run. The work is varied: one day you might be supporting a fundraiser or club, the next coordinating event logistics or working with colleagues on a partner activation.


We’re looking for someone who is warm, proactive and highly organised, with a real enjoyment of working with people. You’ll be comfortable juggling practical details and competing deadlines, communicating clearly with different audiences and pitching in wherever needed. You’ll care about making fundraising and events accessible, and take pride in helping supporters feel valued and well supported.
