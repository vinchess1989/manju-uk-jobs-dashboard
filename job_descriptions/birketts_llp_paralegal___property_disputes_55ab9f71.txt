About us




Birketts is a full service, UK Top 50 law firm. With a heritage spanning 160 years, we have more than 700 lawyers and legal professionals and over 600 staff based in Bristol, Cambridge, Chelmsford, Ipswich, London, Norwich and Sevenoaks. We advise businesses, government and public sector organisations and individuals in the UK and internationally across four principal practice groups: Real Estate, Corporate Services, Dispute Resolution and Private Client.




The team you will join




Our Property Disputes Team sits within our wider Dispute Resolution Division and acts for a wide range of clients including private individuals, partnerships, trusts, registered social landlords and corporate clients in both the public and private sectors.




The work you will be doing




Drafting and reviewing tenancy agreements and advising on occupational arrangements
Drafting, reviewing and serving notices
Advising on possession options and implications of relevant legislation to the private rented sector
Preparing and reviewing pleadings, witness statements, and settlement documents.
Preparing instructions to Counsel
Conducting legal research and preparing clear, concise advice notes for clients and senior team members
Keeping up to date with legislative changes in the sector
Attending court hearings, mediations, and negotiations as appropriate.
Manage a caseload of residential letting matters, working under supervision of experienced solicitors
Attending appropriate business development opportunities
Working to deadlines
Maintaining accurate time-costing procedures
Ensuring compliance with file management
Proactively support other members of the team and to provide cover as and when necessary




Please note that this job profile is not an exhaustive list of duties but merely an outline of the key components of the role. You may be required by your line manager to take on additional responsibilities when requested.




What we are looking for




Law degree or equivalent experience
Demonstrable interest in and/or knowledge of relevant law and legal procedure relating to residential lettings/private rented sector
Ability to undertake legal work requiring technical and procedural understanding
Competence in accurately recording time and understanding chargeable work
Strong legal research and investigative skills
Advanced written and verbal communication skills
Ability to analyse information and present findings clearly and precisely
Strong attention to detail to produce work documentation which is consistently of a high standard
Professional integrity and a commitment to providing excellent client service
To be competent in using Microsoft Word, Excel, time recording software, case management, Outlook and digital dictation




Hybrid Working




Due to the nature of work required, this role is fully office based.
