Job Snapshot

🎯 Job Functions: Administrative | General Business | Project Management

Overview

CV-BRIDGE LTD is looking for an organized, proactive, and detail-focused Operations Coordinator to support day-to-day business activities in a UK - based role.

This entry-level opportunity is ideal for someone with strong organizational, communication, and coordination skills who wants to build a career in business operations, administration, and project support.

Key Responsibilities

Support day-to-day business operations and administrative activities
Coordinate schedules, meetings, documents, and internal communications
Maintain accurate records, reports, spreadsheets, and operational data
Track tasks, deadlines, and follow-up actions across different teams
Assist with project coordination and general business support
Help improve internal processes and maintain efficient workflows
Communicate with internal teams and external contacts professionally
Provide administrative support to ensure smooth daily operations

Requirements

Strong written and verbal communication skills in English
Excellent organization and time-management abilities
Good attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities
Confident using email, spreadsheets, documents, and online business tools
Strong problem-solving and coordination skills
Professional, reliable, and proactive approach to work
Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team
Previous administration, operations, project support, or customer service experience is beneficial but not required

What We Offer

Competitive £30,000–£35,000 annual salary
Permanent, full-time position
Flexible hybrid working arrangement
Structured onboarding and role-specific training
Ongoing professional development and mentoring
Clear opportunities for career progression
Supportive and collaborative working environment
