Operations Assistant (Remote)

Overview

CV-BRIDGE LTD is looking for an organized, proactive and detail-oriented Operations Assistant to join our remote UK-based team.

This role is ideal for someone with strong organizational, communication and problem-solving skills who enjoys supporting day-to-day business activities and helping internal processes run smoothly.

Job Snapshot

📍 Location: United Kingdom

🏠 Workplace: Remote

💼 Job Type: Permanent

⏰ Employment: Full-Time

🎯 Experience: Entry Level

💷 Salary: £32,000–£34,000 per annum

Key Responsibilities

• Support day-to-day operational and administrative activities

• Maintain accurate records, documents and internal information

• Assist with data entry, reporting and process coordination

• Support teams with operational tasks, follow-ups and priorities

• Update spreadsheets, systems and internal documentation

• Monitor workflows, deadlines and routine business activities

• Respond to internal and external queries professionally

• Identify and escalate operational issues where appropriate

• Help improve efficiency across day-to-day processes

Requirements

• Strong organizational and time-management skills

• Excellent attention to detail and accuracy

• Good written and verbal communication skills

• Comfortable using Microsoft Office and online systems

• Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities effectively

• Professional, proactive and reliable approach to work

• Able to work independently in a remote environment

• Previous administration, operations, customer service or business support experience is beneficial

What We Offer

• £32,000–£34,000 annual salary

• Remote UK working arrangement

• Permanent, full-time position

• Opportunity to build practical operations and administration experience

• Exposure to digital workflows and modern business systems

• Opportunity to develop organization, communication and problem-solving skills

• Career development within a growing UK-focused business
