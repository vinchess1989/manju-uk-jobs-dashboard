Administrator (4-month temp) – Hybrid (2 days office / 3 days home)
Granta Park, Cambridge
£12.71 per hour

We are seeking an Administrator to join this busy charityon a temporary basis (approx. 4 months). This is a fantastic opportunity for an experienced administrator who enjoys managing a high-volume inbox, keeping processes on track, and providing first-class support to a friendly, ambitious team.

The role
Reporting into the Research & Partnerships Directorate, you will provide proactive administrative support to the Research Funding team, taking day-to-day responsibility for the Research inbox and a range of coordination and processing activities. You’ll be confident working independently, juggling priorities, and ensuring accurate records and timely communication.

Key responsibilities
- Manage the shared Research inbox: triage and prioritise emails, respond to routine queries, and escalate specialist questions to colleagues
- Support grant funding administration, including processing application information and coordinating review paperwork
- Act as an administrative point of contact for grant-holders: follow up actions, send reminders, provide updates, and escalate where needed
- Process invoices and maintain accurate records, checking information and following up missing details
- Support meetings and events: scheduling, attendee liaison, travel/accommodation, venues/catering and materials preparation
- Provide wider administrative support across the Research team as required

About you
- Proven experience supporting a busy team, ideally including managing a shared inbox or high-volume enquiries
- Highly organised with strong attention to detail and the ability to see tasks through to completion
- Confident with Microsoft Office, particularly Outlook and Excel (for record-keeping and basic financial admin)
- Strong written and verbal communication skills, with a helpful and professional approach
- Discreet and trustworthy when handling confidential and sensitive information
- Research or grant-funding exposure is helpful but not essential – training will be provided
