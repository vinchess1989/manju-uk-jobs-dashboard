We are looking for a Transaction Assistant to join our Commercial Property Real Estate team.

The role

We are looking for a transaction assistant to join our Real Estate team, with a particular focus on post-completion work. You will play an important role in ensuring that the steps following completion of our clients' transactions are dealt with accurately, efficiently and within the relevant deadlines, particularly Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) returns and HM Land Registry applications.

Working closely with lawyers and colleagues in the Real Estate team, you will manage an allocated post-completion caseload with appropriate supervision and guidance. This is a role for someone who enjoys detailed, process-driven work, takes pride in getting things right and follows matters through to registration and file closure. The majority of your time will be spent on post-completion work, with opportunities to assist with other real estate tasks as required.

Your Main Responsibilities Will Include


Preparing SDLT returns using information from transaction documents and the matter team, checking the accuracy of the information and referring any queries or points requiring tax advice to the supervising lawyer.
Obtaining the necessary approvals and arranging submission of SDLT returns and payment of SDLT in accordance with the firm's procedures, retaining submission acknowledgements and certificates on file.
Preparing and submitting HM Land Registry applications, including applications to register transfers, leases and charges, with supporting documents and the appropriate fees, following review where required.
Checking completed documents and post-completion requirements, identifying missing information, consents or certificates and liaising with the matter team and third parties to obtain them.
Maintaining accurate post-completion checklists, diaries and trackers, monitoring filing deadlines, priority periods and requisition response dates, and escalating any risk of delay promptly.
Monitoring the progress of Land Registry applications, dealing with routine correspondence and preparing responses to requisitions, with supervision on more complex issues.
Liaising with HM Land Registry, HMRC, clients, lenders, other solicitors and managing agents as appropriate to progress outstanding post-completion matters.
Preparing and serving post-completion notices, such as notices of transfer or charge, and obtaining acknowledgements and compliance certificates where required.
Checking completed registrations against the transaction documents and instructions, reporting discrepancies to the supervising lawyer and arranging delivery of updated title information to clients and lenders.


The person

Experience in commercial real estate, conveyancing or post-completion work would be helpful but is not essential, as training and supervision will be provided. This would include people with secretarial and administrative experience. More importantly, you will bring excellent attention to detail, strong organisational skills and an interest in developing specialist post-completion expertise.

Given the responsibilities of this role, your skills and experience will include:


Excellent attention to detail and a careful, accurate approach to preparing forms, checking documents and recording information.
Good numeracy and confidence working with transaction figures, fees and payment information.
A willingness to learn, respond to feedback and develop your knowledge of real estate and post-completion processes.
Previous experience preparing SDLT returns, submitting Land Registry applications or dealing with requisitions would be desirable, but is not essential.


Note you do not need to have a degree or equivalent qualification.
