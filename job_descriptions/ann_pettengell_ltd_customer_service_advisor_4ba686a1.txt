Full job description

Our client is looking for an enthusiastic and customer-focused Customer Service Advisor to join their small, friendly team in the heart of Cambridge. This is an exciting opportunity for someone with previous experience in the travel industry, ideally within the premium or luxury travel/accommodation market, who takes pride in delivering exceptional customer service.

You will be a confident communicator with a positive, can-do attitude and a genuine passion for helping customers. As part of a small team, you will need to be flexible and willing to provide support across the business wherever required.

This position will be a part-time position, working 20 hours a week (see below for working pattern). All working hours are office based.

Key Duties

Responding to customer enquiries by email and telephone
Amending bookings and managing or responding to special requests
Proactively handling customer requests and resolving any customer issues that may arise
Responding to customer reviews and addressing any issues raised
Providing additional support to the team to include Translating villa descriptions, website content and marketing materials
Undertaking other reasonable duties as required to support the proper performance of the role

Key Skills and Experience

Previous experience within the travel industry, preferably within luxury accommodation market
Excellent customer service and communication skills
Self-starter, motivated with a positive ‘can-do’ attitude
The ability to work independently, proactively resolve issues and manage customer requests effectively.
Well-organised with the ability to prioritise workload
Strong attention to detail
Good IT skills with competency in MS Office
Committed and hard-working with a flexible approach
Fluency in French and/or Dutch would be highly desirable

Working Hours

Monday: 09:00–17:00
Tuesday or Wednesday: 09:00–17:00
Thursday or Friday: 09:00–13:00

Please note: From April to October, the short weekday shift will be replaced by a Saturday shift every other week, 15:00–19:00.
