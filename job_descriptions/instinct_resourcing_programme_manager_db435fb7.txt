Job Title: Programme Manager

Salary: £35,000 - £38,000

Location: Hybrid – 2 days a week in Cambridgeshire

Permanent




The Role

This is an exciting opportunity to work within a rapidly growing educational institution, providing end-to-end project, event, and client management for their learning programs.




As a Programme Manager, you'll oversee the coordination of clients, faculty, venues, and logistics to ensure every programme is delivered to the highest standard.




The Company

You will work with a well established education organisation with an international reputation for delivering high-quality learning programmes. They work with global organisations to design and deliver impactful professional development experiences.




Skills & Experience

Detailed project and event management (booking accommodation, travel, catering, AV, budget tracking, risk registers).
Client, delegate, venue, and faculty relationship management and communication.
On-site delivery and execution (hosting dinners; technical support on Zoom/Teams).
Facilitating brief/debrief sessions, rehearsals, and lessons learned frameworks.
Flexible, adaptable, customer-centric attitude with strong relationship-building skills.
Excellent written and verbal communication.
Ability to thrive in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple priorities/deadlines.




This is a really exciting opportunity to be fully immersed in a rapidly growing organisation, and manage their programmes and events.




If you are interested, please apply with an updated CV and/or reach out to ben.forrester@instinct.co.uk
