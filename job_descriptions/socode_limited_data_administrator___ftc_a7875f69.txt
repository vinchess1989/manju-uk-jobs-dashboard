Data Administrator - FTC

Cambridge

Permanent
Easy Apply

SoCode is in an exciting period of growth and advancement. We’re committed to using the very best technology to improve the experience for our clients and candidates, and that starts with an exceptional data environment.

As we head into 2027, we’re looking for a Data Administrator to take full ownership of our data landscape. You’ll own our database and data flows and lead a focused data cleanse and enrichment project. It’s a hands-on role where your work will be felt across the business, and you’ll be trusted to run it start to finish.

What you’ll be doing
• Taking ownership of our data quality and data flows, making sure they’re accurate and well organised • Leading a deep data cleanse and enrichment project, bringing our records up to date and consolidating them • Keeping each record current, complete and consistent
• Following and improving clear processes so the data stays in good shape afterwards
• Tracking and reporting progress so the team can see the difference you’re making
• Flagging patterns, gaps and ideas for improvement as you go

What we’re looking for
• A sharp eye for detail and real pride in getting things right
• A commercial mindset: you understand why good data matters to a business
• Someone who works well independently and takes accountability for their own work
• A process-driven approach, with the discipline to work methodically through a large volume of work
• The ability to put two and two together and spot what doesn’t look right
• Good communication skills and a confident, friendly manner
• A willingness to work hard and see a project through No previous experience is needed. This is a great first role or next step, and we’ll give you the context and support to get going.
