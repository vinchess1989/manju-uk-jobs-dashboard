🚀 Interim Commercial Paralegal

📍 London/Cambridge | Full-time office based | Initial 3-month interim contract (day rate)




I’m working with a fast-growing organisation operating at the cutting edge of the defence and aerospace technology sector, looking for an experienced Commercial Paralegal to join its legal team on an initial 3-month interim basis.




You’ll be:

📑 Reviewing, drafting and negotiating customer/supplier contracts and NDAs
📝 Supporting the development of legal templates and playbooks
🤝 Working closely with internal stakeholders and external suppliers
🚀 Helping shape the legal function as the business scales




What we’re looking for:

Law degree
2–3+ years’ paralegal experience
Strong supplier/commercial contracts experience
Exposure to Company Secretarial matters
Confident working autonomously in a fast-paced environment
