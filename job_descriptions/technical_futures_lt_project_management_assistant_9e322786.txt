Full job description

Project Management Assistant - High-Tech Engineering - Cambridge / Essex borders - to C£40K + Hybrid + Good Benefits. A thriving Technology Company seeks a highly organised Project Management Assistant with good coordination skills, excellent written communication skills and ideally experience working within an engineering, technology or Consultancy environment.

Applications for this Project Management Assistant role are welcome from well-organized individuals with previous experience of working in a project administration, project coordinator or project support role. You’ll be rewarded with a competitive salary and an extensive benefits package.

The Project Management Assistant will play a key role in assisting Project Managers and Engineering Leads by improving the effectiveness and efficiency of the Project Management function, whilst also identifying opportunities to improve processes and ensuring the use of project management tools to company standards.

Key Skills and experience should include:

Proven experience of working in a similar role such as Project Admin, Coordinator or Project Support.
Highly organised with good attention to detail.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications and familiarity with project management methodologies.
The ability to work well with systems, data and business processes.
Experience working within an engineering or technology company is beneficial.

There is great scope for career development for the right candidate.
