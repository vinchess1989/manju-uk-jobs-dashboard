Auction Assistant – Cambridgeshire AuctionsJob Summary

We are seeking a dedicated, organised and reliable Auction Assistant to join our team at Cambridgeshire Auctions. This role is suited to someone with strong administrative and communication skills who enjoys working in a busy office environment.

Cambridgeshire Auctions specialises in the sale of a range of goods, including cars, vans, jewellery and diamonds. The successful candidate will play an important role in supporting the day-to-day operations of the business, assisting with auction administration, customer enquiries, accounts, payments and the smooth running of our auction sales.

Key Responsibilities

Managing incoming telephone calls and directing enquiries appropriately.
Handling customer enquiries by telephone, email and face to face.
Preparing and processing invoices and recording payments accurately.
Monitoring outstanding customer balances and proactively contacting customers to chase overdue payments.
Communicating firmly and professionally with customers regarding unpaid invoices, payment deadlines and outstanding account queries.
Maintaining accurate customer account records and updating payment information.
Organising and maintaining files, records and documentation in both physical and digital formats.
Accurately entering and updating customer, vehicle and auction information.
Updating and checking information across online auction platforms to support the smooth running of auction sales.
Coordinating administrative arrangements relating to successful auction bidders.
Assisting customers with collection arrangements and general auction enquiries.
Handling customer complaints and disputes professionally, escalating more complex matters to management when necessary.
Supporting colleagues with daily administrative duties and assisting the auctioneer during auction sales when required.
Managing competing priorities and working effectively under pressure to meet deadlines.
Preparing jewellery and diamond items for dispatch, including careful packing and ensuring items are securely packaged.
Organising and coordinating postage and dispatch arrangements for jewellery and diamond purchases.
Checking items and relevant documentation before dispatch to ensure orders are accurate and ready for shipment.
Keeping accurate records of dispatched items and tracking information.

Skills and Experience

Essential:

Strong organisational skills and the ability to prioritise a varied workload.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Good attention to detail and accuracy when handling information, invoices and payments.
A confident telephone manner and professional customer service skills.
The ability to communicate clearly, remain professional and handle challenging customer conversations.
Good computer skills and the ability to learn new systems and online platforms.
The ability to work independently and as part of a team.
A reliable, proactive and professional approach to work.
The ability to remain calm and organised in a busy working environment.

Desirable:

Previous administrative or office experience.
Experience in customer service, accounts, invoicing or chasing outstanding payments.
Knowledge of auction processes, online auction platforms or the motor trade.

Previous auction or administrative experience is preferred but not essential, as training will be provided. We are looking for someone who is enthusiastic, willing to learn, confident communicating with customers and able to adapt to a varied and busy working environment.
