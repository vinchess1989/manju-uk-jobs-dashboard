Are you an organised and proactive individual with experience in finance and office administration? 

Finance and Office Coordinator

Location: Near Newmarket, Suffolk
Salary: Competitive, depending on experience
Hours: Flexible, 20–30 hours per week
Contract Type: Permanent 
Sector: Administration & Finance

Our client based near Newmarket are seeking a highly organised Finance and Office Coordinator.

This is a varied and rewarding role combining financial administration, office coordination, team support, marketing and event planning. The successful candidate will play a key role in ensuring the smooth day-to-day running of the business, supporting colleagues and helping to maintain a positive and sociable working environment.

The ideal candidate will be professional, dependable and confident managing financial records and administrative systems, whilst also bringing energy, initiative and excellent interpersonal skills to the role.

As the Finance and Office Coordinator, duties will include:

Managing day-to-day financial administration

Coordinating office systems, supplies, contracts, records and relationships with external suppliers and support providers.

Providing administrative and document support 

Supporting recruitment

Organising team and client events

Maintaining and updating the company website

Supporting the company's LinkedIn presence

Providing administrative support for projects

Acting as a central point of contact within the office

An ideal candidate for the Finance and Office Coordinator will have:

Previous experience in finance administration, office administration or a varied coordination role.

Strong organisational skills

Confidence handling invoicing, expenses, timesheets and financial records.

Strong Microsoft 365 skills, particularly Excel and Word.

Excellent communication skills, verbal and written.

Interviews will take place in Newmarket, following a registration process by PureKat Consultancy Ltd. If we have not responded to your application within 3-5 days, unfortunately you have not been successful on this occasion, but please feel free to contact us for other opportunities. PureKat Consultancy Ltd is acting as an Employment Agency for this permanent vacancy.
