Are you great with customers and enjoy finding solutions? We're looking for a Retention Advisor to help keep our existing insurance customers by providing excellent service and support at renewal.

What You'll Do


Speak with customers about their insurance renewals
Understand customer needs and provide suitable solutions
Handle policy queries and changes
Build strong relationships and deliver outstanding service
Work towards retention and renewal targets


What We're Looking For


Strong communication and customer service skills
Confidence in handling conversations and negotiations
A positive and proactive approach
Insurance experience is desirable but not essential


What You'll Get


Competitive salary and bonus
Training and development opportunities
Supportive team environment
Career progression opportunitie
