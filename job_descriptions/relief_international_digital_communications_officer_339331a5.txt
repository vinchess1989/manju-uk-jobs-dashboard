We are currently recruiting a Digital Communications Officer to join our Resource Development team.

This is a full-time position, homebased in the United Kingdom, with a salary of £28,000 per annum.

At Relief International, we are committed to building a representative, inclusive, and authentic workplace. We strongly encourage applications from candidates from minoritised backgrounds and those who bring lived experience of the contexts in which we work.

This role is classified as requiring standard pre-employment checks.

About Relief International

Relief International is an international non-profit organisation that partners with communities impacted by conflict, climate change, and disaster to save lives, build greater resilience, and improve long-term health and wellbeing. We work in countries across Africa, Asia, and the Middle East, delivering Health and Nutrition, WASH (Water, Sanitation and Hygiene), Education and Livelihoods programming that creates the foundation for community resilience. We do this by working with local actors to develop safe and inclusive programming that is evidence-based, conflict-sensitive, climate-smart, and responsive to community needs.

About The Opportunity

This is an exciting early-career opportunity for someone looking to make an impact within a global humanitarian organisation. Reporting to the Head of Media & PR, the Digital Communications Officer will support brand and storytelling, digital engagement, and media outreach to showcase the impact of Relief International’s work around the world. As part of a small, fast-paced team, you will work closely with country office colleagues to develop compelling content for multiple platforms and audiences.

Your Responsibilities

This role includes the following responsibilities.


Act as a point of contact for brand and storytelling support across Relief International's global country programmes
Help to create a range of communications products including reports, website articles, email content, and social media posts
Monitor messages, comments, and inquiries, coordinating with colleagues to respond where relevant
Help to manage Relief International's editorial calendar, coordinating content across digital channels
Produce compelling video assets that support fundraising and brand awareness goals
Work with photographers and videographers to capture high-quality content from country programmes
Draft, translate, and issue media statements relating to Relief International's work and priority issues
Track metrics across external channels and help to compile reports that evaluate performance


About You

You will bring the following experience and skills.


Degree in communications, media, journalism or a related field
Excellent written and spoken English
Strong attention to detail
A creative and visually aware approach, with the ability to produce accurate, high-quality content across multiple channels
Passion for storytelling, digital communications, and emerging social media and content trends
Excellent organisational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines
A strong interest in humanitarian action, international development, and the work of NGOs
The following would be advantageous:
Experience editing and producing video content
Strong written and spoken French
