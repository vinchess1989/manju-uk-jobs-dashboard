HR Administrator (Fixed-Term Contract 9-12 Months)
South Cambs - Cambridgeshire
£26,000 - £27,000 + Benefits
Full Time – 37 hours
Office Based with 1 Day WFH

Our client is a successful, employee-focused business celebrating over 30 years of growth and success. With a strong reputation for developing and promoting talent internally, they are looking to appoint an HR Administrator to join their friendly and collaborative HR team on a fixed-term basis.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone looking to build a career in HR. Whether you already have HR administration experience or are a recent graduate with strong customer service skills and a genuine interest in Human Resources, this role offers fantastic exposure across the full employee lifecycle.

The Role
Working closely with an experienced HR team, you will support a broad range of HR activities, including:

Managing HR administration throughout the employee lifecycle
Maintaining employee records and updating the HR system
Supporting onboarding and new starter processes
Coordinating interviews and welcoming candidates on-site
Producing HR documentation and correspondence
Liaising with external payroll providers to ensure accurate employee data
Supporting the wider HR team with projects and day-to-day activities

About You
We're looking for someone who is:

Bright, proactive and keen to develop a career in HR
Organised with excellent attention to detail
Confident communicating with people at all levels
Customer-focused with strong relationship-building skills
Comfortable learning new systems and processes
Able to manage a busy and varied workload

Previous HR experience would be advantageous but is not essential.

Why Apply?

Join a welcoming and supportive culture
Work alongside an experienced HR team
Genuine career development opportunities
Social and collaborative working environment
Team social budget and regular company events
Opportunity to gain broad HR experience across the employee lifecycle

Additional Information

Immediate start preferred to allow for a thorough handover

If you're looking to take the next step in your HR career and gain exposure within a supportive and people-focused organisation, we'd love to hear from you.
