Job Summary




We are seeking a diligent Attendance and Administration Officer to Join Abbey College Cambridge. You will lead on student attendance and assist the HR, Exams and Operations functions, helping to provide proactive and efficient administrative support.




Overview




You will lead on student attendance, ensuring that missing students are located quickly and ensuring that attendance records are accurately maintained. You will also work with the wider administrative functions in the college, helping to provide proactive and efficient administrative support.




Your duties and responsibilities will include the following:




Keeping attendance registers up to date.
Coordinating the system of tracking of missing students with the relevant stakeholders during the day. Sending out absence notifications twice per day.
Contacting directly students missing from lessons.
Reporting to Vice Principal Pastoral any discrepancies between registers and attendance records of students.
Maintaining a range of school records and data relating to student attendance records, including supplying information required for the DFE.
Responding to requests for such data from a range of staff in the school and assisting in the interpretation of such data and information.
Working with Personal Tutors and Housemasters to supply accurate student attendance data.
Monitoring late attendees to challenge persistent offenders and flag trends to Housemasters.
Ensuring the student database remains accurate.
Provide Daily and Weekly attendance reports.
Assisting with the recruitment and onboarding of new staff.
During peak periods, support Examinations Officer with the administration of exams.
Supporting departments with filing, photocopying, scanning and Data entry
Occasional Reception cover may be required to assist the Operations Team for lunch cover and during staff absence.







Qualifications and Experience

5 GCSEs including English at level 4/5(C) or above or equivalent level qualifications.
Highly effective communication skills both verbally and written
Good attention to detail.
Ability to work on own initiative.
Excellent organisation and planning skills.
Friendly, enthusiastic, approachable.
Ability to work under pressure, identify priorities and meet tight deadlines
Flexible approach to workload.
Willing to undertake other duties within the college and work as part of a bigger team.
