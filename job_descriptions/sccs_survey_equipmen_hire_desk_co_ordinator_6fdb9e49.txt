SCCS Survey Equipment is currently looking for a Hire Desk Co-ordinator to come and join our team based at our office in St Neots. You will join us on a full time, permanent basis, and in return, you will receive a competitive salary of £30,000 per annum.

About the Company

SCCS Survey Equipment, part of Hexagon, is a leading company specialising in the rental and sale of high-quality surveying equipment. With a strong commitment to customer satisfaction and innovation, SCCS provides cutting-edge solutions to construction and surveying professionals across various industries.

At Hexagon, we do not just measure the world – we define its future.  As the global leader in measurement technologies, we provide the confidence that vital industries rely on to build, navigate, and innovate.

About the Hire Desk Co-ordinator role:

The purpose of this role is to be the main point of contact for customers seeking to hire our surveying equipment. The Hire Desk Co-ordinator will manage incoming calls and emails from customers in a timely, efficient and professional manner. Key activities include preparing quotations, liaising with internal departments to confirm equipment availability, processing orders, co-ordinating deliveries and collections, and managing the associated sales administration.

Key responsibilities as our Hire Desk Co-ordinator will include:

Responding to hire enquiries received by telephone and email.
Processing customer orders accurately using Syrinx software.
Arranging delivery and collection of orders in line with customer requirements, liaising as necessary with the warehouse, logistics and workshop teams.
Managing and resolving mid-hire events such as machine breakdowns and exchange requests.
Handling general enquiries from customers, prospective customers and sales colleagues.
Supporting the management team in leading and contributing to the regular review of procedures to improve customer care, performance, and productivity.

What we’re looking for in our Hire Desk Co-ordinator:

Essential:

Excellent communication, interpersonal and negotiation skills.
Proven experience in a busy customer service office environment.
Highly organised and thorough, with excellent attention to detail.
Self-motivated, conscientious and adaptable to change.
Ability to work collaboratively as a key member of the wider organisation.
Ability to build and maintain strong relationships with key customers.
Strong time-management skills, with the ability to prioritise workload and work to strict deadlines under pressure.

Desirables

Fluent working knowledge of Microsoft Office suite & Syrinx would be ideal, although training would be provided.
Knowledge of survey equipment, software, and workflows such as GNSS, laser scanners, total stations, machine control, and requirements of the users of such equipment
Previous experience of working in the Hire environment e.g. plant or rental management 
Prior account management experience.
