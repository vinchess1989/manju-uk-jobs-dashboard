Booking Administrator

Apply for this job
Job summary

We are looking for an enthusiastic, organised and motivated Booking Administrator to join our friendly and busy team at the Ely Community Diagnostic Centre, part of Cambridge University Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust.

As a Booking Administrator, you will play an important role in supporting the delivery of diagnostic services, helping patients access the tests and investigations they need in a timely and efficient manner.

Working as part of our administration team, you will be responsible for coordinating and booking patient appointments across a range of diagnostic services, including CT, MRI, Ultrasound, X-ray and other specialist investigations. You will also support the day-to-day running of the centre, responding to patient enquiries, managing referrals and providing reception cover when required.

Community Diagnostic Centres are an exciting and expanding part of the NHS's long-term plans to transform diagnostic services, bringing essential tests and investigations closer to patient's homes, improving access to care and helping to reduce waiting times. As a relatively new and developing service, Ely CDC offers an opportunity to be part of this transformation, contributing to the growth and development of services that make a real difference to our local communities and the NHS.

This is an excellent opportunity to join a growing service that is helping to bring essential NHS diagnostic investigations closer to patients' homes.

Main duties of the job

The successful candidate will be responsible for providing an efficient, accurate and patient-focused administrative service, supporting the day-to-day delivery of diagnostic services at Ely Community Diagnostic Centre.

Key responsibilities will include:

Booking, rescheduling and cancelling patient appointments across a range of diagnostic services, ensuring appointments are managed in line with departmental procedures and waiting time targets.Managing incoming referrals and waiting lists, ensuring patients are booked appropriately and within required timescales.Monitoring appointment availability and working proactively to minimise unused capacity, helping the department make the best use of its diagnostic resources.Responding to patient enquiries by telephone and in person, providing clear and helpful information regarding appointments and investigations.Providing reception cover, including welcoming patients, checking them in for appointments and supporting the smooth running of clinics.Maintaining accurate and up-to-date patient records using electronic patient administration systems, including Epic.Liaising with clinical teams, colleagues and other departments to resolve booking queries and support effective patient pathways.Identifying and escalating booking issues, capacity concerns and potential delays to patient care.Supporting the achievement of departmental performance standards and NHS waiting time targets.

About us

Our Trust

Cambridge University Hospitals (CUH) NHS Foundation Trust comprises Addenbrooke's Hospital and the Rosie Hospital in Cambridge. With over 13,000 staff and over 1100 beds the priorities of the Trust focus on a quality service which is all about people - patients, staff and partners. Recognised as providing 'outstanding' care to our patients and rated 'Good' overall by the Care Quality Commissioner, is testament to the skill and dedication of the people who work here. CUH's values - Together - Safe, Kind, Excellent - are at the heart of patient care, defining the way all staff work and behave. The Trust provides accessible high-quality healthcare for the local people of Cambridge, together with specialist services, dealing with rare or complex conditions for a regional, national and international population.

CUH is committed to promoting a diverse and inclusive community - a place where we can all be ourselves. We value our differences and fully advocate and support an inclusive working environment where every individual can fulfil their potential. We want to ensure our people are truly representative of all the communities that we serve. We welcome applications for all positions in the organisation irrespective of people's age, disability, ethnicity, race, nationality, gender identity, sex, sexual orientation, religion or belief, marriage and civil partnership status, or pregnancy and maternity status or social economic background.
