Operations Support

Location: Peterborough
Salary: £28,000 - £30,000 per annum
Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:00am - 5:00pm (1-hour lunch)

The Opportunity

Our client is a well-established and growing industrial business looking for an Operations Support Assistant to join their small, friendly and supportive team.

This is a varied, hands-on role combining administration, customer service, stock control and operational support. Full training will be provided on the company's systems, products and processes, with opportunities to gain industry-recognised qualifications and develop a long-term career.

Initially, you will focus on office support and learning the company's bespoke systems. As your knowledge develops, you'll become involved across the wider operation, including stock management, order fulfilment, customer service and stores.

If you enjoy variety, learning new things and getting involved wherever needed, this could be an excellent opportunity.

Key Responsibilities

Process customer orders, bookings and quotations
Produce delivery notes, certificates and customer documentation
Process purchase orders and supplier paperwork
Maintain accurate records on internal systems
Handle customer enquiries by telephone and email
Learn the company's products, stock codes and processes
Identify products and components for customer orders
Book stock in and out and assist with inventory control
Monitor stock levels, complete stock checks and support stock takes
Prepare, pick and pack orders for dispatch
Check incoming deliveries and ensure correct storage
Assist customers with trade counter enquiries
Support operations across both the office and stores
Work closely with colleagues to deliver excellent customer service
Assist management with general operational duties and projects

About You

We are looking for someone with:

Strong customer service and communication skills
Good IT skills, including Microsoft Office
Excellent attention to detail and accuracy
A proactive, flexible and hands-on approach
Confidence working across both office and stores/warehouse environments
Ability to manage a varied workload and priorities
Willingness to learn technical products and processes
Ability to work independently and within a small team

Previous experience within administration, operations, customer service, stores or warehousing would be advantageous.

Career Development

This role offers genuine long-term progression. Full training will be provided, with opportunities to complete industry qualifications, take on further responsibilities and progress within the business.

Benefits

Private healthcare after 12 months
20 days' annual leave plus bank holidays
Additional paid Christmas shutdown without using annual leave
Salary review after 3 months
Further pay review following completion of industry qualifications
Full training and ongoing development
Long-term career progression
