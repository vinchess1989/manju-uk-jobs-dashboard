Full job description

Berry Recruitment are looking for a Private Client/Secretary to join a successful company in March!

You will be responsible for providing accurate and confidential secretarial and administrative support to the companies Private Client team.

Key Responsibilities will be providing:

Secretarial and administrative support
Private client matter support
Financial and matter administration
Client service and teamwork
Compliance and professional standards

The ideal candidate would have:

Strong secretarial or administrative experience in a professional services environment.
Knowledge in word-processing with a high level of accuracy and attention to detail.
Knowledge in Microsoft Office.
Strong organisation, prioritisation and diary-management skills, with the ability to meet deadlines.




The hours of work are Monday to Friday, 9am until 5pm.

The starting salary is between £26,000 and £28,000 per annum.

This is a permanent position.




For further information, please contact Jade at Berry Recruitment, King's Lynn.

We are committed to fair and inclusive recruitment. All applicants will be considered on the basis of their skills, experience, qualifications and ability to perform the role, and no applicant will be treated less favourably because of a protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010.
