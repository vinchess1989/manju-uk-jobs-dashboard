Full job description

Ready to shape student success behind the scenes? Want to bring order to complex timetables and records? Looking for a role where your accuracy and initiative genuinely make a difference every day?

We are currently looking for an

Academic Services Coordinator

to join our busy team in Cambridge, supporting the academic life cycle and helping ensure compliance, accuracy and a smooth student experience.

Hours of Work: full time

In this role you’ll support the Manager of Academic Services in coordinating effective student academic support services, with a sharp focus on timetabling, student records and compliance. You’ll maintain the College’s timetabling system, work closely with the university partner and internal stakeholders, and ensure information meets College, partner university and external expectations—always following relevant policies and regulations.

You’ll thrive in a small cross-functional team, bringing initiative to inbox management, data coordination, communications, board administration and reporting, while upholding the highest standards of accuracy.

Academic Services Coordinator Requirements:

Strong administrative capability with exceptional attention to detail and accuracy
Confidence managing complex processes across academic records, timetabling and compliance
Proven initiative and the ability to work productively within a small, cross-functional team
Ability to handle sensitive information responsibly, maintaining confidentiality in line with GDPR
Experience supporting operational relationships with internal stakeholders and external partners (university partner desirable)

Academic Services Coordinator Benefits: Salary £30,250 to £34,350

Permanent role with long-term stability in Education and Training
Full-time hours and a clear office working pattern
Opportunity to contribute to meaningful student support across the academic life cycle
Career development within the Navitas Group, including UK and international opportunities
Supportive team environment where your accuracy and initiative are valued

Meet the Organisation: Who We Are and What We Do

Navitas is a diversified global education provider delivering an extensive range of educational services for students and professionals, including university programmes and language training. At ARU College, we help students transition successfully into university study—backed by a value-driven culture and equal opportunity employment. Please note: ARUC reserve the right to fill the position by invitation.

If you think you are suitable for this Academic Services Coordinator role, please apply now!

Bring your organisation skills, your commitment to accuracy, and your drive to make student journeys smoother—because your work here truly matters.

If you have eligibility to work in the UK on an ongoing basis, we’d love to hear from you.

As ARU College is a safeguarding employer, the successful applicant will be required to undergo a DBS check.
