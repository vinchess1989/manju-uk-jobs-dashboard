Admin Officer
Location: Cambridgeshire,CB1 1BA
Working Days / Hours: 37 hours Monday - Friday
Working Pattern: 9am - 5pm
Pay Rate: £12.86 per hour Day 1 Rate Increasing to £13.25 after 12 weeks

Are you at your best in a busy, service-led environment where attention to detail truly matters? Our Client is hiring in Cambridge to support the smooth running of Courts and Tribunals-keeping cases moving, information accurate, and customers well supported.

What you'll be doing:
* Prepare papers, files, documents and records for hearings, meetings and court/tribunal activity
* Update and input information onto an in-house computer system; support post opening and dispatch
* Clerking support for civil and family courts/tribunals-checking materials are up to date and ready
* Handle counter and enquiries via face-to-face, written and telephone communication
* Contact relevant parties, support scheduling and listing/rota activities, and serve court documents where required
* Contribute to Team Information Board (TIB) meetings and help keep them relevant, timely and productive
* Support processing of casework, including standard documentation and court orders, ensuring accuracy and interpretation against the court file
* Check and verify documents, records and returns against criteria, regulations and quality standards
* Carry out basic calculations and produce straightforward statistical analysis where needed
* Assist with assembling information for daily TIB preparation, bundles and reporting

What you'll bring:
* Strong administrative ability with excellent customer service focus-"right first time" matters
* Confidence working to guidelines and procedures, escalating complex issues to a team leader or supervisor when needed
* A clear, organised approach to managing your time and workload throughput and accuracy
* Competent computer skills to create/update records and support accurate data input
* A willingness to be flexible-some ad hoc working across local offices may be required

Additional information:
HMCTS embraces Lean principles and continuous improvement-there's space to use discretion, initiative and contribute to small projects.

If you're successful in your application a BASIC DBS will be required as part of the on-boarding checks with Brook Street.


If you're someone who enjoys keeping things running smoothly, supporting customers with warmth and professionalism, and taking pride in accuracy, we'd love to hear from you. Apply now for this Administrative Officer opportunity in Cambridge.
