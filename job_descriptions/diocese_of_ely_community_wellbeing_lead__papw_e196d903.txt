Papworth Team Ministry are looking for a Community and Wellbeing Lead to help people feel connected, supported and involved across the Papworth Team villages.

Based at The Venue in Papworth Everard, you’ll develop welcoming community activities, support warm spaces and establish a visiting and companionship scheme for people who would benefit from regular contact and friendship.

35 hours per week, initially on a 12-month contract.

The successful applicant will be expected to work five days a week, including flexibility for evenings and some Saturdays as required.

Salary £32,000 per annum.

Find out more and apply: https://www.papworth.team/about/vacancies

Deadline for applications: Sunday 29 November 2026
