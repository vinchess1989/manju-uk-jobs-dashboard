Kneesworth House are looking for an HR Administrator to join the team. This role is office-based on site. Monday - Friday, 9am - 5pm.

Priory Hospital Kneesworth House, Royston, Hertfordshire is a level 2 rehabilitation service providing a range of specialist recovery pathways for adults within a purpose-built facility. It is accessible by public transport, with Royston railway station approximately 2–3 miles from the service and limited local bus links. Due to the location of the service, access to a vehicle or the ability to drive is recommended.

What you'll be doing

Join our team as an HR Administrator, where you’ll play a key role in supporting the hospital’s HR and recruitment activities. Working closely with managers and our central HR teams, you’ll help deliver a smooth and effective employee journey, from attracting and recruiting new colleagues through to onboarding, compliance and ongoing development.

What you’ll be doing:
Supporting recruitment activity across the hospital, working with managers and the Central Talent Acquisition team to attract and recruit high-quality candidates.
Managing the recruitment process, including monitoring applications, coordinating interviews and assessment centres, and maintaining the Applicant Tracking System.
Coordinating the onboarding of new starters, ensuring all pre-employment checks, documentation and inductions are completed accurately and on time.
Monitoring employee compliance, including DBS renewals and mandatory training, ensuring colleagues remain up to date with requirements.
Arranging and coordinating training, working closely with managers and the Central Learning & Organisational Development team.
You can find additional information in the attached job description.

What you'll bring to the role

Role requirments
You should have previous experience in a Recruitment, HR or general administration role, ideally within healthcare, although this is not essential. You will be educated to A-Level standard or equivalent, with a Certificate in Personnel Practice (S/NVQ Level 3) or a willingness to work towards it. Excellent keyboard skills, confidence using standard office applications and strong organisational and communication skills are also essential.
