A great opportunity for someone organised, proactive, and looking to build experience in technical or food industry administration.

At G’s, we’re proud to be one of the UK’s largest fresh produce businesses - supplying over a billion packs of healthy produce each year. But behind every product is a strong technical foundation: food safety, customer compliance, quality assurance, and continuous improvement.

We’re now looking for a Technical Support Administrator to join our team supporting the delivery of excellent technical service across our customers and internal teams, based at our site near Ely, Cambs.

What You'll Be Doing:

You’ll play a vital role in the smooth operation of the technical function, with a focus on administration, communication, and data handling.

Key responsibilities include:


Coordinate and update technical KPIs, complaint trends, and shelf-life data, both internally and externally.
Manage and respond to customer returns and queries, while analysing trend data to support quality improvements.
Support the technical team in managing and updating product specs, artwork, and new line forms.
Help prepare and format internal/external presentations and crop reports.
Attend availability meetings and contribute to cross-functional collaboration.
Assist with specification reviews and version control in response to performance or seasonal changes.
Support technical projects, audits, and improvement activities.


What We're Looking For:


Previous experience in an administrative, coordination, or technical support role, in food, FMCG, retail, or supply chain would be advantageous.
Strong organisational skills and attention to detail — able to manage multiple systems and deadlines.
Confident communicator — comfortable working with data, documents, and people across different teams.
Proficient in Microsoft Office (especially Excel and PowerPoint).
A proactive mindset with the ability to build strong relationships both internally and externally.


Why Join G’s?

This is a great opportunity to gain hands-on experience in a technical fresh produce fast paced environment with exposure to customer compliance, product development, and supplier performance — all while supporting a business at the forefront of fresh, healthy, sustainable produce.

What we offer:


Monday to Friday 40 hours per week.
31 days holiday (including bank holidays)
Pension scheme, health cash plan & wellbeing support
Discounts on cinema, supermarket, and lifestyle brands
Regular social events and learning opportunities
