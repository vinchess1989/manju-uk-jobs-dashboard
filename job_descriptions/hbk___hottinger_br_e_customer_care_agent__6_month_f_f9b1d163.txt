The position

At HBK, we aim to empower the Innovators! Whether you are a colleague or client, our job is to make your achievements more attainable, innovative and sustainable.

To support our clients in being the best they can be, we rely on a team of first class Customer Care heroes who serve as their go-to contacts whenever support is needed. Whether it's assistance with quotes and orders or handling ad hoc inquiries, the Customer Care team is often the first point of contact—and the face of HBK.

By qualifying, registering, and assigning queries to the most relevant internal departments, our Customer Care professionals play a key role in ensuring our clients feel supported. Thanks to their organizational skills and customer focus, our clients trust that once they've reached out to us, they're in good hands.

We are currently looking for a Customer Care Agent reporting to the Customer Care Manager for LDS business on a 6 month fixed-term contract.

This position is based at our Royston site in the UK and requires fluency in English. The role follows a hybrid working model, however the successful candidate will be expected to be onsite for training during the first couple of months to ensure a successful onboarding experience.

You will be part of a dynamic team that is connected to a larger global organization. You'll interact with customers from various industries and regions, collaborate across borders, and work closely with our factories primarily in the UK.

Primary Responsibilities

As Customer Care Agent you will be responsible mainly for UK customers and some Export area:


Answering incoming calls and requests
Supplying customers with information
Informing customers of pricing
Processing orders
Following up on quotes
Pro-active phone calls to promote our services and increase customer satisfaction
Supporting and collaborating with other departments and worldwide locations to ensure deadlines are met
Performing general administrative tasks and supporting operational efficiency
Assisting with miscellaneous tasks as needed to support business objectives


Professional Qualifications And Personal Skills


Experience from a sales or service function where customer satisfaction has been paramount
Excellent communication skills in English
Additional language skills, particularly German and/or French, would be an advantage
Proficient in Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, Outlook); SalesForce CRM experience as well as Oracle or SAP is a plus
Strong team and interpersonal skills
Task-oriented, self-motivated and flexible
Driven by customer satisfaction
An energetic personality and the ability to multi-task and handle stressful situations


One Company – HBK

HBK (Hottinger, Brüel & Kjær) is a global leader in test and measurement, formed in 2019 through the merger of Brüel & Kjær and HBM. With over 3,000 employees across 80 countries, we deliver advanced solutions that help customers improve product performance and reliability. HBK operates within the Test and Measurement segment of Spectris. For further info please visit www.hbkworld.com.
