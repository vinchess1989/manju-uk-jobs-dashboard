Office Administrator / Secretary - Huntingdon - Maternity Cover

An Office Administrator / Secretary is required for a growing construction company based in Huntingdon. The company specialises in a variety of construction projects across the UK and has a very busy pipeline of future work.

The successful candidate will ideally have previous experience of working as an administrator / office manager / secretary in the construction industry but candidates without this specific experience may also be considered.
