Quotations Administrator - Pharmaceutical

We are seeking an organised and client-focused Quotations Administrator to support our pharmaceutical team. You will manage the quotation process from initial enquiry through to completion, working closely with internal teams to deliver accurate, timely and commercially effective responses. This role is central to providing a positive ALS experience and supporting business growth.

Hours Per Week: 40

Days Per Week: Monday to Friday (08:30 - 17:30)

Contract: Full Time and Fixed Term (until May 2028)

Annual Salary: £27,500 + £1000 GMP Allowance

Closing Date: Tuesday 24th November 2026

Hiring Manager: Glynn Harvey

The Day to Day


Manage the end-to-end quotation process promptly, accurately and consistently.
Coordinate with Technical, Quality, Transport, Accounts, Sales and Business Development teams to meet client requirements.
Set realistic deadlines, monitor progress and provide clients with clear, regular updates.
Support new client setup, including ALIMS configuration, credit checks and Technical Agreement processes.
Maintain accurate and up-to-date client ALIMS accounts, pricing and price lists, removing malformed suites where required.
Manage the sales inbox, ensuring enquiries are dealt with promptly and team KPIs are achieved.
Provide excellent customer service and support the Client Services team with day-to-day activities.
Identify cytotoxic drug products and materials at quotation stage, working with the COSHH team to manage associated health and safety risks.
Support Sales and Marketing activities to help drive business growth.
Promote a positive safety culture, reporting incidents, near misses and unsafe practices promptly.
Ensure adherence to GMP and BS EN ISO/IEC 17025:2017 principles and requirements.


The Essentials


Previous experience in an administration, quotations or client-focused role, ideally within a pharmaceutical or laboratory environment.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Strong organisational, analytical and numerical skills, with excellent attention to detail.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office and Outlook.
The ability to work under pressure, manage competing priorities and meet deadlines.
A proactive approach, with the confidence to use initiative and act on information provided.
A friendly, enthusiastic and client-focused attitude.
The ability to work effectively as part of a team in a dynamic environment.
Tenacity, determination and a commitment to delivering excellent customer service.
An understanding of GMP and BS EN ISO/IEC 17025:2017 requirements.
