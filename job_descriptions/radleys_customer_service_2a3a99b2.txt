About The Job

To work as part of a small, friendly Customer Service team, delivering a high standard of support to customers and internal colleagues.


Respond to customer enquiries via telephone, email and live chat
Process and acknowledge purchase orders using Salesforce and Oracle NetSuite
Prepare customer quotations using Salesforce and Microsoft Word
Support UK Technical Sales Specialists and International Territory Managers
Liaise with customers and distributors to arrange shipments
Monitor and review back orders daily, working with the Supply Chain team
Maintain accurate records and update customer data in Salesforce
Provide customers with delivery updates and communicate any delays
Record customer feedback and contribute to weekly reporting


Our company

Radleys was founded in 1966 by Bill Radley as a scientific glassblowing business.

Nearly 60 years on, we remain a proud British manufacturer, still focused on what we do best,high quality scientific equipment for chemistry.

We design and build precision laboratory systems in house, bringing together chemists, glassblowers and engineers with decades of practical experience. Our equipment is trusted by leading pharmaceutical, biopharma, chemical and academic organisations worldwide, a reputation earned through consistent quality, technical depth and long term thinking.

We intend to build on that foundation. To do so, we need capable, detail focused people who take pride in their work and want to contribute to a business that values quality, expertise and continuous improvement.

The Role

This role has an element of business support, which includes:


Provide practical support around the office when needed, including welcoming visitors and preparing refreshments for external meetings.
Help organise and support internal meetings and events, including arranging catering, setting up rooms and clearing away afterwards.
Provide administrative support to other departments when needed.


Experience And Capabilities

The ideal candidate will have the following skills and experience:


Previous customer service experience (essential)
Confident using Microsoft Office, particularly Word, Excel and Outlook
Experience with order processing systems (e.g. Pegasus Opera, Oracle NetSuite, SAP)
Experience using CRM or database systems (e.g. Salesforce, Access, ACT, Maximiser)


And Have The Following Qualities


Strong communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to build positive working relationships with customers and colleagues
A positive, proactive approach with a ‘can do’ attitude
Good organisation and attention to detail, with a high level of accuracy
The ability to prioritise and manage a varied workload
A willingness to suggest improvements to processes and systems
A reliable and diligent approach, seeing tasks through to completion
The ability to work both independently and as part of a team
A practical approach to problem-solving


The type of person who will succeed here

The successful candidate will also be reliable and diligent, capable of working both independently and as part of a team, with a practical approach to problem-solving and a willingness to suggest improvements to processes and systems.
