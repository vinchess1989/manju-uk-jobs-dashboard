Job Description

Royal Voluntary Service has an opportunity available for a Deputy Manager to join our team at Addenbrookes Hospital, Hills Road, Cambridge, CB2 0QQ. You will join us on a full-time, permanent basis, and in return, you will receive a competitive salary of £13.49 per hour (FTE 24,552 per annum).

About The Deputy Manager Role

Royal Voluntary Service hospital cafes, shops and trolleys are part of the NHS and a real asset in their own right. Staffed by employees and volunteers, we provide an outstanding range of beverages and healthy food choices. Our customers love the fact that the money they spend with us pays for volunteer action in hospitals and the community.

Working alongside a friendly team of employees and volunteers, you will participate in the day-to-day running of our busy retail sites at Addenbrookes Hospital.

In This Role, You Will Be Responsible For


Support the Retail Manager with the day-to-day running of the shop/café
Help ensure operational excellence and consistently high standards across the service.
Support the leadership, supervision and development of the retail team and volunteers.
Help the team deliver excellent customer service to patients, visitors, staff and customers.
Maintain high standards of presentation, cleanliness, stock availability and merchandising.
Support the Retail Manager to drive sales and profitability through effective retail operations.
Identify and maximise sales opportunities within the shop/café.
Assist with ensuring the service operates in line with RVS policies, procedures, mission and values.
Help create a welcoming and positive environment for customers and colleagues.
Support the Retail Manager with the smooth and efficient running of the service in their absence.


Hours: 35 hours per week (Monday - Friday)

Our retail sites are open on bank holidays.

There may also be opportunities for paid overtime in busy periods or to cover absence.

What You'll Bring As Our Deputy Manager

As Deputy Manager, you’ll work alongside the Retail Manager to support the smooth day-to-day running of the shop/café. You’ll help lead and support the team to deliver excellent customer service, maintain high standards and create a welcoming environment, while also supporting sales, stock management and the overall commercial performance of the service.

Job Requirements

This is what we're looking for:


Experience of working in a retail, café or customer-facing environment.
Strong customer service skills, with a friendly, helpful and professional approach.
Experience of supporting or supervising a team, including employees and volunteers.
Ability to coach, mentor and motivate others to deliver high standards.
Good understanding of sales, budgets and cost control, with a commercial mindset.
Strong organisational skills, with the ability to plan resources and prioritise workloads.
Good understanding of health and safety, food hygiene, stock control and relevant policies and procedures.
Confident using systems, management information and reports to support day-to-day operations.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the confidence to deal with issues as they arise
Ability to take responsibility and lead the service in the absence of the Retail Manager.
A commitment to delivering operational excellence and excellent customer service, in line with Royal Voluntary Service values.


Job Responsibilities

This is what you'll be doing:

As Deputy Retail Manager, you’ll work closely with the Retail Manager to support the smooth and successful running of the shop/café. You’ll help lead the team, maintain high standards, deliver excellent customer service and support the overall sales and operational performance of the service.


Support the Retail Manager to deliver key performance targets, sales budgets and effective cost control.
Help ensure the shop/café maintains excellent standards, with a strong focus on friendly, efficient and welcoming customer service.
Ensure the service operates in line with Health & Safety, Food Hygiene, Data Protection, Stock Control and other relevant policies and procedures.
Support and communicate with employees and volunteers, addressing any issues and providing guidance where needed.
Provide coaching, mentoring and training to help the team develop and deliver operational excellence.
Help plan and coordinate employee and volunteer resources to meet the needs of the shop/café.


This Is How You'll Be Doing It

Decision-making

Is able to deal with day-to-day operational decisions across all areas of responsibility, working within set parameters of policies and procedures. Is able to make confident decisions whilst understanding escalation routes when required. Empowering the team to make routine decisions.

Analysis and initiative

Ability to understand and interpret data and utilise, for application on a day-to-day basis.

Ensure compliance with legislation, and protection of the health, safety and well being of all employees.

Planning and delivery

Planning: Information cascades, setting tasks and monitoring outcomes. Short term planning and routine processes. Physical: Rotas and resource planning. Financial: Facilitate stock management process. Particular emphasis on accurate receipt of delivery and replenishment.

Working relationships

You’ll build positive and effective relationships with volunteers and staff through regular, two-way communication, exchanging information and providing direction both formally and informally. You’ll work closely with the Retail Manager as part of the management team, seeking advice and supporting your own development. You’ll also work with Head Office to access specialist advice, support and learning. You’ll also support the Retail Manager with the implementation of the employee and volunteer journey within the site.

Supporting and enabling others

You’ll support employees throughout their journey, from recruitment and induction through to training, performance management and exit. You’ll use relevant training and development tools to support the team and encourage a positive and collaborative working environment.You’ll also support volunteers throughout their volunteer journey, helping them to feel capable, safe and confident in their roles.

Knowledge and expertise

You’ll have experience at supervisory level within a similar retail environment, together with knowledge of EPOS systems and general administrative processes. You’ll have excellent customer service skills, be confident using standard IT systems and have good literacy and numeracy skills.

This is all the other great stuff you'll be getting:


Salary of £13.49 per hour (FTE £24,552 per annum)
26 days’ holiday (pro rata) plus paid statutory Bank Holidays (pro rata)
Up to 2 days paid volunteer leave a year, through our employer supported volunteer scheme
Ten weeks’ company sick pay following successful completion of probation

Enhanced Family Leave schemes

Opportunities to discuss flexible working
Opportunities to develop new skills and progress your career
The chance to make a positive, lasting impact that changes lives, communities, and society
