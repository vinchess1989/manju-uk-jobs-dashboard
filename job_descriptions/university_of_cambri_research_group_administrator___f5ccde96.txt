We are seeking a PHAB Research Administrator to provide research and administrative support to the Public and Mental Health, Ageing, and the Brain (PHAB) Group in order to ensure the smooth and efficient running of research and related activities.

The postholder plays a central role in supporting the operational and research activities of the Public and Mental Health, Ageing, and the Brain (PHAB) Group. This includes providing comprehensive administrative and secretarial support to staff and students, coordinating meetings, placements, and recruitment processes, and maintaining accurate records of equipment, workspace allocation, and research documentation. The role also involves financial administration, website and database maintenance, and logistical support for events such as monthly PHAB meetings. Furthermore, the post-holder will liaise with researchers and other staff involved in in the East of England Applied Research Collaboration (ARC). By ensuring efficient coordination and documentation across multiple functions, the postholder contributes directly to the group's mission of advancing research in public mental health, including healthy ageing across the life course among diverse populations nationally and internationally.

For further information and to apply please read the further particulars.
