Description

Who we are

Sagentia is a leading global regulatory, science and technology consultancy. It excels in research and development, safety and regulatory services, and technical programme delivery. The business has five practices operating internationally across the highly regulated markets of medical, defence, aviation, consumer, food and beverage, and industrial

Opportunity

This role is an exciting opportunity to work within a dynamic marketing team that spans all five practices: Sagentia Innovation, Medical, Regulatory, Defence, and Aviation.

The Marketing Assistant will work closely with Marketing Managers and Executives in each practice. The role will suit an organised person who enjoys being a key team member, communicates effectively, and can multitask. Based in Harston, the successful candidate will occasionally travel to other UK locations (London, Epsom, and Bristol offices).

This role will suit someone looking for an administrative role in a marketing context. Should they wish to pursue a career in marketing, they will have the opportunity to learn from a marketing team and progress within it. The core function of this role is to support the marketing team in delivering their marketing plans by alleviating them of administrative tasks and activities.

Key Responsibilities

Marketing administration and support:
Source and order branded merchandise and literature. Maintain stocks and handle distribution
Maintain building branding, reception literature, and client tour areas
Maintain photo library folder, including sourcing new images and assisting with photo shoots
Assist with membership and subscription registration and renewals
Updating email signature banners
Raise purchase orders, record spend and set up new suppliers
Handle and log inbound email and website enquiries
Support internal communication by keeping the intranet and internal screens up to date
Collating analytics of marketing activity

Event administration:
You will be responsible for booking and supporting marketing events that business development personnel attend. Event coordination might include:
Closely monitoring and communicating about the overall event plan
Researching events
Supporting event business case preparation and approval
Event booking, planning, logistics, and supporting marcomms and promotion
Post-event follow-up tracking and reporting on the ROI
Content production support:
Always working within our brand guidelines, you will work with the marketing team and external agencies to:
Support content production and visual graphics in PPT, Canva and sometimes other applications
Writing skills would be an advantage and provide an opportunity to get involved with content editing and creation
Support the development, creation, printing, and distribution/shipping of marketing materials such as merchandise, case studies, sales slides, brochures, whitepapers, infographics, video, photography, animations, business cards, and any branded collateral.
Email campaign administration:
Use email templates and broadcast systems to prepare and send email campaigns and newsletters
Manage contact lists, provide email delivery reports, update contact statuses, monitor and report on follow-up activities.
CRM data maintenance and cleansing as and when required


Skills, Knowledge and Expertise

We expect candidates to have a good academic background and be very strong in PowerPoint and Excel. Given that this role will require some work within our Defence team, the candidate will need BPSS clearance, which we can facilitate.

Collaborative team player - Ability to work effectively as part of a team with good communication skills
Able to build strong relationships with internal stakeholders and external partners
Good organisational and interpersonal skills
Ability to prioritise tasks
Ability to multitask and operate effectively and efficiently under pressure in a fast-moving environment
Excellent working use of PowerPoint, Word, and Excel
Experience with finance systems such as Deltek (or willingness to learn)
Excellent attention to detail and an eye for design
A willingness to learn and able to exercise initiative
Ability to work under tight deadlines
Prior experience of marketing in a B2B or services organisation
