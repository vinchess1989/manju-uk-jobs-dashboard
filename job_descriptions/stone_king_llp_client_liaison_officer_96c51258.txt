About Us

At Stone King, we're a purpose-driven law firm with a clear ambition: to deliver positive societal impact through the work we do with and for our clients. Since 1785, we've combined a proud heritage with a forward-looking approach, embracing change and investing in our future.

We work with many organisations and individuals who share our commitment to making a difference, including charities, social enterprises, faith-based organisations, educational institutions, businesses, public sector organisations and private individuals. We are nationally recognised for our expertise across the sectors we serve and are consistently ranked by Chambers and Partners and The Legal 500. Many of our lawyers are recognised as leading practitioners in their fields, reflecting the depth of expertise, sector knowledge and client service we provide.

We foster an inclusive and supportive culture where different perspectives are valued, people are empowered to develop and thrive.

The Team

Our Trusts & Estates team supports individuals, families, trustees and beneficiaries through a wide range of private client matters, combining technical expertise with a highly personal approach. From estate administration and trust management to succession planning, tax considerations and powers of attorney, we guide clients through complex and often sensitive situations with empathy, professionalism and clarity. By building strong relationships and providing exceptional service throughout the client journey, we help clients make informed decisions, protect their interests and plan confidently for the future. Our work ensures that clients and their families receive trusted support when they need it most.

The Opportunity

We are looking for a talented and motivated Client Liaison Office to join our Trusts and Estates team. This is an exciting opportunity to work with a diverse client base, providing high-quality legal advice and building trusted relationships within one

You'll work alongside supportive and experienced colleagues, collaborating across teams to deliver excellent client service while continuing to develop your own skills and expertise.

This role offers the opportunity to:


Act as a dedicated point of contact for clients, developing trusted and professional relationships throughout their engagement with the firm
Deliver a highly personalised service experience for high-net-worth and international clients, anticipating needs and ensuring clients feel supported and valued
Proactively coordinate communications between clients, lawyers, intermediaries and external advisers, clarifying responsibilities and next steps to ensure matters progress smoothly and efficiently, while respecting client confidentiality
Develop and maintain trusted relationships with intermediaries, acting as a responsive liaison contact and proactively coordinating introductions, referrals and handovers so clients receive a joined-up, personalised service from the outset and throughout the relationship
Initiate regular contact with clients and, where authorised, their intermediaries, providing timely updates, managing expectations and following up enquiries and outstanding actions without waiting to be prompted
Anticipate and identify potential service issues, take ownership of coordinating their resolution and escalate matters promptly where appropriate to protect and enhance the client experience
Support the development of long-term client relationships and encourage engagement with the firm's wider services where appropriate
Gather client feedback and identify opportunities to enhance service delivery and client satisfaction
Assist with the organisation of client events, relationship-building activities and targeted initiatives that strengthen client engagement
Manage the onboarding of new clients, ensuring all relevant documentation, compliance checks and engagement processes are completed efficiently
Facilitate the smooth transfer of clients into the firm and between practice areas where required
Coordinate consultations, meetings and schedules across the Private Wealth teams
Track onboarding and client engagement activity, maintaining accurate records and management information
Proactively monitor client and intermediary referral pipelines, follow up outstanding information and actions, and identify and address potential delays to ensure progress within agreed timescales
Work closely with fee earners to ensure clients experience a seamless transition from enquiry through to active matter management
Contribute to the continuous improvement of onboarding and client service processes


This is a hybrid role, with an expectation of around two days per week in the office.

About You

We're interested in hearing from candidates who can demonstrate the skills, experience and behaviours required to succeed in this role.

Requirements

Essential


Experience coordinating client onboarding, client administration or customer relationship activities
Experience delivering a premium client service within a legal, professional services, wealth management, private banking or family office environment
Strong organisational and diary management skills with the ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities effectively
Experience handling sensitive and confidential information with discretion
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Ability to work accurately with a high level of attention to detail
Proven ability to build and maintain effective relationships with clients, colleagues and external professional contacts, with the confidence to liaise with intermediaries and coordinate activity across multiple stakeholders
Ability to work independently and use initiative to anticipate needs, prioritise follow-up activity and progress actions without prompting, whilst exercising sound judgement and knowing when to escalate issues


Desirable


Experience supporting senior professionals within a legal or professional services setting
Familiarity with private client services or international client matters
Experience supporting high-net-worth individuals, trustees, family offices, executors or international private clients
Experience using client relationship management or case management systems
Experience identifying and implementing process improvements


Personal Attributes

Essential


Demonstrates emotional intelligence, discretion and professionalism when working with clients experiencing sensitive family, estate, succession and wealth-related matters
Demonstrates a client-focused approach and commitment to delivering outstanding service
Builds credibility and trust quickly with clients and colleagues
Communicates clearly, professionally and confidently with a broad range of stakeholders
Demonstrates empathy, discretion and professionalism when dealing with sensitive client matters
Highly organised and able to remain calm under pressure
Demonstrates a proactive, solutions-focused approach: anticipates client and intermediary needs, initiates contact, follows through on commitments and takes ownership of issues through to resolution
Works collaboratively across teams and supports a positive and inclusive working environment
Displays integrity, accountability and attention to detail in all aspects of work


Desirable


Confidence engaging with high-net-worth individuals and international clients
Ability to influence and coordinate activity across multiple teams and stakeholders
Experience contributing to service improvement initiatives
