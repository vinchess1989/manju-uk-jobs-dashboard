We’re looking for a PR & Social Assistant to support our team across media relations, social content and influencer campaigns. If you enjoy following the news, writing, spotting social trends and discovering creators, this is the role for you. You’ll help bring purposeful brands’ stories to life, with guidance from experienced colleagues as you develop your skills. We’re looking for someone friendly, organised and curious, with strong writing skills, experience working with others and a basic understanding of the media and social platforms.




The day-to-day

Support the team in writing press releases, news stories, articles, case studies and product pieces
Pitch press releases and feature ideas by phone or email to national, regional and trade journalists, across print, broadcast and online, to interest them in covering their clients’ stories
Respond to phone calls and emails from journalists
Help arrange interviews and editorial meetings between journalists and clients’ spokespeople
Monitor media coverage, social performance and influencer content, and help prepare clear reports for the team and clients
Occasionally attend client events and industry conferences
Brainstorm fresh ideas for PR, social media and influencer campaigns
Help plan content calendars, write captions and create simple visual and short-form video content, with the team’s guidance
Schedule approved social content and support community management by monitoring comments and messages and flagging queries to the team
Research relevant influencers and creators, checking audience fit, engagement and content quality, and maintain accurate contact lists
Support influencer outreach, product gifting, campaign briefs and content tracking, keeping records of deliverables and deadlines
Keep up to date with social trends, platform developments and creator content, sharing relevant opportunities with the team




Key skills

Excellent communication: You need to have excellent written and spoken English.
Copywriting skills: You need to be hot on spelling, grammar and proofreading, as well as have a creative flair for producing engaging copy.
Presentation skills: From communicating ideas to your team to presenting stories to journalists, you need to organise information in a succinct and interesting way to capture their attention. 
Strong interpersonal skills: Whether it’s over the phone, by email or in person, PR is all about people, so you need to be approachable, friendly and empathetic, and be able to relate to people on different levels within the agency, with clients and with the media.
Excellent telephone manner: You will need to speak to journalists, clients and third-party individuals on the phone, so you need to have the confidence to pick up the phone and speak to them and maintain a positive, friendly and upbeat tone.
Time-keeping: You will often be working to tight media deadlines as well as meeting the demands and pressures of your team and clients, so you need to be able to work quickly and efficiently, without compromising on quality.
Resilience: You need to be confident to speak to a wide range of individuals and resilient enough to handle criticism and rejection. You also need to be tenacious in continuing to pitch stories, as securing media coverage can take time.
Social media awareness: An interest in platforms such as Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn, and an understanding of how content and tone differ across them
An interest in influencers: Curiosity about creators, their audiences and what makes a brand partnership feel relevant and authentic
Content creation: Confidence trying tools such as Canva and basic video editing tools, with a willingness to learn
Organisation and attention to detail: Keep contact lists, content calendars and campaign trackers accurate, and communicate clearly with colleagues




The role

Supporting the team with research, reporting, writing, social content and influencer campaign coordination
Developing practical skills across PR, social media and influencer marketing, with support and feedback from the team
Learning what makes a strong PR story, engaging social content and a successful creator partnership
Learning how to communicate professionally with journalists, clients, influencers and talent managers
Following news, social trends and creator content to identify opportunities for clients
Being a reliable team player who brings enthusiasm, meets deadlines and keeps campaign administration organised




Key information




You will be reporting to an Account Manager
This job is fully remote with quarterly team meet-ups in central locations (usually London), with travel paid for by us. Employees must be living in the UK with the right to work (for legal reasons).
Salary: £26,250 per year
Permanent, full-time role
Hours: 37.5 hours per week, working 7.5 hours a day, Monday to Friday




Why Lem-uhn?

A happier type of agency with an open dialogue to assess your workload and ensure it’s manageable within work hours.
The opportunity to progress quickly and learn about all aspects of a PR agency within a small and growing agency.
You’ll be working on purposeful clients that are making a positive difference in the world making your work more meaningful.
Flexible Hours: Our core hours are 9 am - 3 pm with a flexible approach allowing you to work you’re 9 am - 5.30 pm during the hours that work best for you.
36 days holiday in total - 23 days holiday plus 2 wellness days that can be taken with only a day's notice plus a Christmas shutdown.
Work From Anywhere: You can work outside the UK for up to 45 days per year.
Less Admin Than Other Agencies: We use efficient practices to take the boring tasks out of PR to ensure our intelligent and amazing employees are using their time well rather than wasting it. What can be automated should be, and you should be helping to build the processes to do so.
Carbon Negative: We acknowledge that there is a huge issue with our carbon impact on the environment, and as an agency, we offset all of our employees’ impact with that aim in mind, meaning you’ll be carbon-negative by working with us.
A Menstrual Policy: We believe people who experience debilitating menstrual pain shouldn’t be penalised. We provide flexible working solutions plus up to ten days' leave for debilitating periods or menopause symptoms and pregnancy loss.
