About The Role

As a part-time HR Advisor based at our Cambridge site, you will act as the first point of contact for all HR-related matters, providing proactive, professional support to managers and employees.

This is a part-time position, working 25 hours per week. We offer flexibility around how these hours are worked, and the working pattern can be agreed with the successful candidate to support both business needs and work-life balance.

Your Key Responsibilities Will Include


Building strong partnerships with people managers, providing coaching, guidance and practical HR support to ensure the consistent application of policies and best practice, while helping to create and maintain a positive workplace culture where Domino is a great place to work.
Providing expert advice and guidance on employment law, employee relations matters, supporting managers to make informed and consistent people decisions.
Analysing and interpreting people data, including employee engagement results, to identify trends and opportunities, working alongside HR Business Partners to support meaningful people initiatives.
Supporting the full employee lifecycle, including attraction, recruitment, onboarding and offboarding, ensuring a positive and professional employee experience throughout.
Working collaboratively with the wider UK and Global HR team to deliver key people initiatives, projects and business priorities, ensuring consistency and alignment across the organisation.


As with all roles at Domino, you'll have access to a wide range of development opportunities, enabling you to broaden your experience, build your expertise and progress your HR career.

About You

We're looking for someone who:


Has strong knowledge of UK employment law and is confident providing practical, commercially focused HR advice.
Has proven experience in a generalist HR role, with the ability to manage a broad range of people matters across the employee lifecycle.
Can build credible relationships and positively influence stakeholders at all levels, using sound judgement to achieve the best outcomes while knowing when to escalate issues where appropriate.
Demonstrates professionalism, integrity and discretion, consistently modelling Domino's values and Bold behaviours.
Has experience working within a fast-paced manufacturing or operational environment (desirable).
Holds a CIPD Level 5 qualification or is currently working towards it, or has equivalent experience


What You Can Expect

As a talented individual working at Domino, you can expect a competitive salary and a generous benefits package, including:


25 days of basic holiday (pro rata), plus options to buy or sell additional days, ensuring you have ample time to relax and recharge.
A discretionary bonus scheme, a testament to your hard work and dedication.
Life insurance, providing security and peace of mind for you and your loved ones.
Medical insurance with the option of purchasing a health cash plan, dental insurance, travel insurance and cycle to work scheme, ensuring your health is always a top priority.
Join our green car scheme, a step towards a more environmentally friendly commute, with free EV charging onsite.
Pension plan, starting with 8% employer contributions.


We are a highly successful company, we’re large enough to offer career development opportunities yet small enough for your contribution to be readily recognised and rewarded.

About Us

At Domino Printing Sciences we do more. Our expertise isn't just in providing cutting-edge variable data printing technology; it's in revolutionising how manufacturers embrace sustainable packaging and spearhead advancements in coding automation. We’ve been a part of the global variable data printing story for a long time, and our solutions help ensure the success of the world’s leading brands. Our goal is to be the leading global variable data printing brand through applying our expertise and reliable solutions to help the world’s manufacturers be sustainable and cut waste, while attracting, informing, and protecting their consumers. But we also know that it’s not just about the work we do—it’s about fostering a collaborative, dynamic and welcoming environment where everyone can flourish, contributing to Domino not only as a pioneer in the industry but also as an exceptional place to work.

Positions may be at an advanced stage of the recruitment process and can close at any time.
