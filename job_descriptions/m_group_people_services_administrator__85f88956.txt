Right across infrastructure, there’s a requirement to not only maintain, but also renew and reimagine. Whatever stage you’re at in your career, with us you’ll have an opportunity to grow and develop. Where will you be working?

As an organisation, there is little we don’t do and plenty to get involved in. Our Group Support roles are vital in making sure we can help over 14,000 people deliver essential infrastructure seamlessly across water, energy, transport and telecom.

We are looking for a proactive and customer-focused People Services Administrator to join our People Services team. This role is key to delivering an efficient, high-quality HR administration and advisory service across the employee lifecycle. Supporting employees and managers across the business, you will provide first-line HR guidance, process people-related transactions, and ensure a positive employee experience through accurate, timely and professional support.

Please note: This is a 12-month fixed-term contract with hybrid working, and office locations in Warwick, alternatively we'd be open to a candidate based in Stevenage.

This role can be worked either over 4 days per week or 5 days per week (36 hours). The final working pattern will be agreed with the successful candidate at the point of offer, subject to business needs. Attendance on site will be required for a minimum of 2 days per week to support collaboration and team engagement.

Want to come and be a part of it?

What will you be doing?

As a People Services Administrator, you will be responsible for providing first-line support through the HR Helpdesk and carrying out a wide range of HR administration activities. From managing onboarding and contractual changes to maintaining employee records and responding to HR queries, you will ensure all processes are completed accurately, compliantly and within agreed service levels. You will work closely with colleagues across the People function, escalating complex matters where appropriate and contributing to wider HR projects and initiatives.

What You’ll Bring


Strong administrative and organisational skills.
Excellent attention to detail and accuracy.
Good communication and customer service skills.
Ability to manage a busy workload and meet deadlines.
Strong IT skills, including Microsoft Office.
Ability to handle sensitive information confidentially.
Positive, proactive and team-oriented approach.
Flexible and willing to learn new processes and systems.
Previous administration or customer service experience is desirable.
HR administration experience or CIPD Level 3 is advantageous but not essential.
