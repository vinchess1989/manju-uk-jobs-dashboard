Social Media & Digital Content Assistant – Remote

Job Title: Social Media & Digital Content Assistant

Location: Remote, UK

Salary: £24,000–£28,000 per annum (depending on experience)

Job Type: Full-time or Part-time, subject to availability

About the Role

Apply4U is hiring on behalf of our client for a creative and organised Social Media & Digital Content Assistant to support online marketing activities from home.

This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys creating engaging content, managing social media platforms and helping businesses build their online presence. Whether you have previous digital marketing experience or are looking to develop your skills, this role offers an opportunity to grow in a digital-focused environment.

Key Responsibilities

Create and schedule engaging content for social media platforms.
Assist with writing captions, posts, blogs and other digital content.
Prepare simple graphics and visual content using tools such as Canva.
Maintain social media calendars and ensure content is published on schedule.
Monitor audience engagement, comments and messages.
Research social media trends, relevant topics and competitor activity.
Track basic performance metrics and help identify opportunities to improve engagement.
Work with the wider team to support digital marketing campaigns.

What We’re Looking For

Good written communication and attention to detail.
An interest in social media, content creation and digital marketing.
Basic knowledge of platforms such as LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook or TikTok.
Good organisational skills and the ability to manage tasks independently.
Familiarity with Canva or social media scheduling tools is an advantage.
Previous experience is beneficial, but transferable skills and a willingness to learn are also welcome.

Why Apply?

Remote working opportunity.
Potential full-time or part-time flexibility, depending on the vacancy.
Opportunity to develop digital marketing and content creation skills.
Build experience across social media, branding and online engagement.
