Right across infrastructure, there’s a requirement to not only maintain, but also renew and reimagine. Whatever stage you’re at in your career, with us you’ll have an opportunity to grow and develop. Delivering essential infrastructure services for life, while being safety first, and client and customer centric in a friendly, fun and respectful environment where you are encouraged to thrive.

Where will you be working?

At M Group Highways, we’re supporting the safe transportation and movement of people across the UK.

We don’t just build roads and bridges- we’re building a better future, creating lasting social value that gives back to the communities in which we work.

You’ll be joining our Cambridgeshire Highways Commercial team as a Commercial Assistant, as the UK’s number one highway maintenance contractor, our teams use the latest innovations to create effective solutions. We offer a range of services including planned and reactive maintenance and highway improvement schemes.

Want to come and be a part of it?

What You'll Be Doing


Extract data from electronic and paper returns and enter it into commercial systems
Gather, process, and analyse information to support reporting and documentation
Applying for payment from the Client for works undertaken
Administrative tasks associated with Subcontractor Payments
Identify and escalate missing, unclear, or contradictory information
Liaise with operational staff to request and follow up on overdue returns
Follow governance and compliance requirements in line with M Group policies
Complete ad hoc administrative tasks assigned by your line manager
Work to agreed timescales, managing deadlines and raising workload concerns promptly
Complete mandatory training and stay updated with refresher courses
Follow established procedures, governance, and compliance requirements
Continuously develop knowledge and skills through training and hands on learning


What You'll Bring


No prior industry experience required
Good general education (e.g., GCSEs or equivalent)
Strong IT skills, especially Microsoft Office and Excel
Ability to manage tasks, learn new systems, and adapt to changing project phases
Ability to prioritise workload effectively and organise tasks to support commercial activities
Strong attention to detail across data, documents, and system inputs
Experience in infrastructure or construction environments (desirable)
Familiarity with finance, purchasing, stock management, or commercial management systems (desirable)
Confidence using digital or business systems (desirable)
