Job description

Dreams Come True is an ambitious UK charity creating life-changing dreams for children living with serious illness, disability and disadvantage. We work with supporters and partners to turn generosity into experiences that bring joy, confidence and lasting memories.

This is an exciting opportunity to help shape how our charity looks, sounds and connects with people. As our Creative & Content Officer, you will create engaging social media, graphic design and website content that brings our impact to life, strengthens our brand and supports fundraising.

You will plan, create and publish content across our social media channels; design high-quality digital and print materials; keep website content accurate and engaging; and produce simple short-form video. Working from clear briefs and alongside colleagues across the charity, you will turn stories, evidence and fundraising messages into accessible content for different audiences.

You may be at the start of your marketing, communications or creative career, or ready for a second role that broadens your experience. What matters most is a strong portfolio, creativity, good organisation and the ability to adapt content for different channels while working within brand, accessibility, consent and safeguarding requirements.

If you want to build your creative experience in a varied role and use your skills to help more children experience the power of a dream, we would love to hear from you.
