Cambridge Start up - VC backed




The Talent Collective are working with a spin out Cambridge University Start up, looking for an graduate or experienced professional to join as an Office & Operations Coordinator.




This isn't a typical Office role, we're looking for someone who has what it takes to work and help build an early stage startup. There won't be a specific list of duties, instead you'll get involved in every aspect that helps an office run successfully from onboarding new starters to running company events.




There will be real opportunities to progress in this company, a career path could develop within operations or HR long term.




About You

Either a graduate with 1-2 years of office experience, looking to make impact and grow a career with an exciting startup or an experience office administrator looking to step up
1+ years experience in office administration, procurement, HR would be highly desirable
Curious and eager to learn
Highly organised
Analytical and commercially minded
An excellent communicator
Comfortable working with ambiguity
Detail-oriented without losing sight of the bigger picture




What You'll Do




Office space planning, supporting with arranging desks and equipment for

new hires and general support with other facilities management

Managing incoming deliveries, including tracking and maintaining accurate

records.

Preparing meeting rooms, materials and documentation for meetings.
Planning and coordinating company events and maintaining the company

events calendar.

Using Microsoft 365 & Google Workspace confidently to manage documents,

emails, calendars and spreadsheets.

Support with HR systems, especially onboarding
Supporting the administration of procurement activities and systems, including

purchase orders and supplier lists.

Maintaining accurate and well-organised office records and documentation,

including digitising of paper correspondence.

Providing general administrative support across the business as required.




You don't need to meet every requirement to apply.




Salary £30,000 - £35,000

Based onsite in Cambridge (5 days per week)

Interviews are taking place immediately for this role so please ensure you are available.
