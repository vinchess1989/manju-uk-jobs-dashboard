Here at the Mick George Group, we are currently recruiting a Order Processor to join the team within Trade waste and Brokerage.

Location: Mick George Head Office - Huntingdon

Working Hours: Monday - Friday between 7am – 6pm on a shift pattern, Saturdays as and when required.

Pay: To be discussed

Employee Assistance Programme

Long-service rewards

Job Purpose

To accurately process a high volume of customer orders for various entities across the Mick George Group, alongside answering an agreed volume of external customer calls.

Main Responsibilities

Inputting and processing customer orders on the system in an accurate manner
Confirming bookings professionally via email to all customer orders
Maintaining various order inboxes
Ad hoc administration tasks relating to order processing
Assist with customer’s phone & email orders/enquiries
Be able to develop relationships, building new opportunities with both new and existing customers and ensuring high levels of customer retention at all times
Keep up to date with and ensure there is an understanding of Mick George’s products and business structure
Ensure a proactive, flexible and positive approach at all times when handling customer requests
Undertake all relevant training when required

Skills/Qualifications

Ability to work in a high volume, fast paced office environment
Ability to work under pressure
Highly organised with a polite and confident telephone manner
Computer literate, with Microsoft Office knowledge
Driven and pro-active individual
Flexibility to get tasks completed as needed with excellent attention to detail
