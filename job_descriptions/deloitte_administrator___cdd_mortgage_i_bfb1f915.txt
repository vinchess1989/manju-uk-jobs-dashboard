The Role

The Administrator / Account Review Officer supports the Broker Intermediary mortgage channel, through which new and existing customers can apply for a mortgage via a mortgage broker.

As part of the mortgage application process, Customer Due Diligence (CDD) checks are required. These checks will normally be completed through an automated customer due diligence process.

Where the automated CDD process is unable to complete, for example due to missing information, incomplete fields or delays in information being updated, the Account Review Officer will undertake the required manual intervention to enable the CDD assessment to be completed.

The role is specifically focused on supporting CDD requirements for mortgage applications submitted through the Broker Intermediary channel.




Key Responsibilities

The Account Review Officer will:

Pick up mortgage applications where the automated CDD process has been unable to complete and manual intervention is required.
Review the application to identify missing or incomplete information preventing the CDD check from being completed.
Access the relevant mortgage application information submitted by the broker.
Review and validate the available customer and application information.
Update missing or incomplete information within the relevant customer due diligence process using information available from the mortgage application.
Ensure information is entered accurately and completely before progressing the CDD assessment.
Re-run or progress the appropriate CDD process once the required information has been completed.
Review the resulting CDD risk outcome and take the appropriate next action.
Where the CDD result is Low or Medium Risk, complete the relevant CDD activity to allow the mortgage application to progress.
Where the CDD result is High Risk, refer or create the appropriate case for Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) to be undertaken by the relevant specialist team.
Maintain clear and accurate records of actions completed, ensuring an appropriate audit trail is available.
Follow required CDD, financial crime and operational procedures throughout the process.
Escalate exceptions, system issues or cases that cannot be progressed through the defined process.




Skills and Capabilities

The role requires:

Strong attention to detail and accuracy.
Ability to review customer and mortgage application information and identify missing or incomplete data.
Ability to work across multiple systems and accurately transfer relevant information between them.
Ability to follow clearly defined operational processes.
Good judgement and the ability to recognise when a case requires escalation or further review.
Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage a workload of individual cases effectively.
Ability to work accurately within a process-driven and controlled environment.
Confidence using multiple systems and applications.
Understanding of the importance of Customer Due Diligence and financial crime controls within customer onboarding.
Ability to maintain complete and accurate records to support audit and control requirements.
