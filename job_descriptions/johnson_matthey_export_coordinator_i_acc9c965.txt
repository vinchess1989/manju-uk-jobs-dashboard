Vacancy: Export Coordinator

Location: Royston, UK

We are currently recruiting for an Export Coordinator, the key purpose of this role is to arrange transport in accordance with both customer and JM requirements. The role requires the production of all shipping documentation, including Export Invoices, Dangerous Goods documents, Certificates of Origin, and Letters of Credit.

This is a hybrid role with the expectations of being on site once a week.

At Johnson Matthey, your work will matter. As a Export Coordinator, you’ll join a world leader in platinum group metals with over 200 years of history and innovation. You’ll help tackle the world’s biggest challenges - cutting emissions, powering modern society and enhancing lives - as we continue to apply, refine and recycle metals that matter, for a healthier and more resilient future.

Your responsibilities:


To arrange for the safe and secure transport of all shipments (UK and International) with appropriate approved freight forwarders or carriers
To arrange for the safe and secure collection of refining materials from our customers for delivery to the appropriate site
Ensuring shipments are processed in a timely manner to meet customer required delivery dates.
Deal with all carrier or freight forwarder issues relating to shipments.
Prepare all documents through internal systems to accompany the shipment (i.e. Commercial Invoice, Delivery Notes, Packing Lists, Letters of Credit, Certificates of Origin and Dangerous Goods Notes etc. required by the customer or Customs and compliance bodies as appropriate)
Liaise with carriers / forwarders on issues of reported delays, losses or damage to any Johnson Matthey shipments and advise internal customers.
Provide advice and guidance to sales and customer service teams on the cost and risk implications of alternative shipping methods (Road, Sea, Air) INCO terms.
Check and approve freight invoices for their own shipments.
Ensure all documents for export and imports are compliant in terms of customs legislation and export control procedures.
Maintain good relationships with sales and customer service teams and notify them of any potential delays to shipment.
Take part in any regular reviews or carriers or freight forwarders.
Provide an exceptional customer service to internal and external customers.



Requirements for the role:


Good IT skills with microsoft office, Outlook and Teams (Essential)
Good at forming relationships with other functional teams (Essential)
Good working knowledge of export operations including documentation requirements (Essential)
Understanding of Known Consignor, Regulated Agent (Essential)
Previous experience working in customer focused role (Essential)



Even if you only match some of the skills, we’d love to hear from you to discuss further!
