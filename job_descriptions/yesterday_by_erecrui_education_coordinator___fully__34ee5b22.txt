Full job description

We are seeking a Education Coordinator for a UK-based private mental health and forensic services company. This position is for a 12-month fixed-term contract based remotely, working from home.

Our client provides psychological and psychiatric expert witness assessments to solicitors and local authorities in criminal and family court proceedings, alongside educational psychology services for local authorities and schools. Their values are very important to them. They are principles which underpin their business strategy, staff recruitment, client service, communication style and how we treat their network of professional psychologists.

About the role
As Education Coordinator you will support the Education department in delivering consistently high standards of customer service. This role requires strong coordination and organisational skills, the ability to prioritise effectively, keen attention to detail and the confidence to build positive working relationships, ensuring that appointments and assessments are scheduled and completed in line with expectations, and that all responsibilities are managed efficiently and on time.

Key responsibilities:

Managing client accounts such as schools, MATs and local authorities
Liaising and developing positive working relationships with various stakeholders within schools, MATs and local authorities
Managing availabilities and bookings, recording all appointments on behalf of Education and keeping relevant stakeholders up to date with progress
Managing a caseload and adjusting as required by the demands of the client account
Effectively and efficiently managing communications and the sharing of information/documents between the relevant people and professionals
Timely management and responses to all enquiries
Proofreading, formatting and filing confidential reports from Educational Psychologists, ensuring they meet required standards and deadlines. This includes working with sensitive or occasionally difficult material, so the ability to manage this content professionally and maintain personal wellbeing is essential for the role
Working to agreed processes to ensure accuracy, compliance and meeting legal requirements as required
Understanding that safeguarding is everyone’s responsibility, and therefore recognising and flagging potential safeguarding concerns in line with completed training and company processes
Keeping all relevant IT systems up to date as appropriate
Actively contributing to improving processes, policies and procedures to help the company operate efficiently
Achieving agreed targets and KPI’s to contribute to company objectives and business plan

The rewards
As a progressive, responsible company, they are committed to supporting employees’ work–life balance. The role of Education Coordinator is a fully remote position and the hours of work are 9am to 5pm. No weekends or bank holidays.

The salary on offer is £26,000 per annum
25 days paid holiday plus bank holidays, pro rata
Company pension scheme
24-hour Employee Assistance Programme
Occasional company social events

As a progressive, responsible company, we are committed to supporting employees' work-life balance and therefore promote employees maintaining appropriate boundaries for their wellbeing, using strategies and seeking support when needed.

About you
For the role of Education Coordinator you need to have / be:

Good standard of IT knowledge i.e. Microsoft / Accounts Packages and use of databases
Proficiency in basic digital tools (e.g., email, spreadsheets, learning platforms)
Educated to A-level standard
Exceptional administrative skills
Superb written and verbal communication and listening skills
Strong interpersonal skills and ability to build rapport
Excellent telephone manner and responsive to queries
Highly effective planning, organisational and prioritisation skills and attention to detail
Commitment to delivering great customer service
Great multi-tasker, problem solver, able to think creatively and willing to ‘get the job done’
Demonstrates empathy for the client group, including parents/guardians
Self-motivated, flexible with a ‘can-do attitude’
Capable of handling and communicating confidential information sensitively
Ability to work independently as well as part of a wider team
Resilient and capable of juggling priorities and working under pressure, adhering to strict deadlines
