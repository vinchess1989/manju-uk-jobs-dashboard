Private Patient Coordinator | Cambridge | Full Time | Permanent | Monday - Friday | 37.5 per week | Potential weekend working to meet business needs

At Spire Cambridge Lea Hospital, we are looking for a proactive and customer-focused Private Patient Coordinator to join our team. This is a fantastic opportunity for someone who enjoys building relationships, supporting patients through important healthcare decisions, and working in a fast-paced environment where no two days are the same.

As the first point of contact for many of our self-pay and insured patients, you'll play a vital role in guiding them through their healthcare journey, providing expert support, clear information, and a seamless experience from enquiry through to treatment.

What You'll Be Doing


Supporting patients with enquiries via telephone, email, and face-to-face interactions.
Guiding self-pay and private medical insurance patients through their treatment options and pathways.
Converting enquiries into appointments, diagnostics, and admissions through a consultative and patient-centred approach.
Providing accurate quotations, booking appointments, and processing payments.
Building strong relationships with consultants, medical secretaries, and hospital colleagues to ensure outstanding patient experiences.
Supporting patients with financing options and helping them make informed decisions about their care.
Using a range of digital systems and tools to manage enquiries effectively and efficiently.


What We're Looking For

We're Keen To Hear From Individuals Who Are

✔ Passionate about delivering exceptional customer service

✔ Confident communicators who can build rapport with a wide range of people

✔ Comfortable handling sensitive and sometimes complex conversations

✔ Organised and able to manage multiple priorities effectively

✔ Experienced in working within a customer-focused, sales, healthcare, hospitality, or service environment

✔ Positive team players who thrive in a collaborative and fast-paced setting

✔ Confident using multiple systems and digital platforms

Why Join Spire Cambridge Lea Hospital?

At Spire, we're committed to providing outstanding personalised care for our patients and creating a supportive environment where our colleagues can thrive.

In Return, You'll Benefit From


A welcoming and supportive team environment
The opportunity to make a genuine difference to patients' lives
Working within one of the UK's leading independent healthcare providers
A rewarding role where patient care and customer experience are at the heart of everything you do

Our people are our difference; it's their dedication, warmth and pursuit of excellence that sets Spire Healthcare apart.

We commit to our employee’s well-being through work life balance, on-going development, support and reward.

Spire Healthcare is a leading independent healthcare group in the United Kingdom, running 38 hospitals and over 50 clinics across England, Wales and Scotland. It provides diagnostics, inpatient, day case and outpatient care, operates a network of private GPs and provides workplace health services to over 800 corporate clients. It also delivers a range of private and NHS mental health, musculoskeletal and dermatological services.

For us, it's more than just treating patients; it's about looking after people.
