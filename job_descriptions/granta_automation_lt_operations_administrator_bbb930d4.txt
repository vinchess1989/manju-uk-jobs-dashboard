Job Title: Operations Administrator

Department: Operations

Location: Office based role - Granta Automation, Cambridge Road, Linton,
Cambridge, CB21 4NN

Salary: £27,000-£32,000

Starting Date: ASAP

Status / Type: Full time

Days: 5 days per week (Monday – Friday)

Working hours: 40 hours per week (37.5 hours per week excluding lunch break) 08:00-16:30 with 30 minutes lunch break

Position Statement
This role is to work as an Operations Administrator to produce automated palletising systems. The role includes quoting, purchasing and booking service visits.

It will demand good task management and a flexible proactive approach to help juggle the workload in the most effective way. You will be working closely with the operations team and taking over information from them, so good communication skills and competence at writing and speaking English is important.

Performance Objectives

1. To be able to keep the purchasing, servicing, support and fleet administration organised and up to date

Compliance Expectations
1. Respect Company’s values and uphold the Mission Statement
2. Always work safely, following all relevant safety procedures

Roles & Responsibilities

1. Arrange and follow up on service visits for existing customers - Daily

2. Produce quotes for parts and services - Daily

3. Follow up on service tickets - Daily

4. Send invoices to customers when work has been completed including support contract invoicing - Daily

5. Process the stock movements - Daily

6. Follow up on existing quotes for customer parts or service contracts - Weekly

7. Follow up on service contracts - Weekly

8. Scan in all service reports and produce quotes post service - Weekly

9. Place orders for consumables, tools, equipment, third party services and travel - Weekly

10. Order confirmations – amend the prices & delivery dates on the database & resolve queries - Weekly

11. Fleet management – adding servicing and MOTs onto Google Drive, booking in MOTs, servicing and/or buying and selling vans - Weekly

12. Progress chasing supplier parts - Weekly

13. Placing orders for main project parts and contacting suppliers - As required

14. Loading of BoMs - As required

15. Any adhoc administrative tasks to support the company - As required

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
1. All quoting and purchasing to be completed in a timely manner

2. All service visits to be proactively booked and all reports kept up to date

3. All administrative work to be carried out to a high standard

4. Good integration with team members

Software knowledge and level of proficiency
Microsoft Excel - Reasonable understanding

Microsoft Outlook - Reasonable understanding

Microsoft Access - Beneficial

Qualification / Certificates / Licenses
1. Clean driving license
2. Good general education

Essential Selection Criteria

1. Positive attitude, prepared for open thinking and change

2. Able to work as part of a team

3. Reliable at completing tasks

4. Organised

5. Honest

6. High level of attention to detail

7. Proficient at writing and speaking English

8. Ability to work well under pressure and meet deadlines

9. Happy to assist in other departments when required

10. Strong knowledge of quoting, purchasing and stock

11. Previous experience of office or administrative work

Ideal Selection Criteria

1. Manufacturing/Production experience is desirable, but not essential, as training will be provided

2. Good knowledge of an MRP system

Job Type: Full-time

Pay: £27,000.00-£32,000.00 per year

Benefits:

Company pension
Cycle to work scheme
On-site parking

Ability to commute/relocate:

Cambridge CB21 4NN: reliably commute or plan to relocate before starting work (required)

Application question(s):

What experience do you have so far with sales support and writing proposals?
Have you had any CAD experience? (not a deal breaker as we can easily train)

Work authorisation:

United Kingdom (required)

Work Location: In person
