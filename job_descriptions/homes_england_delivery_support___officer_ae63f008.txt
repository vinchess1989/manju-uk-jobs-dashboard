Job Summary

Working in a team with the Senior Delivery Support Manager and Delivery Support Manager the Delivery Support Officer will play a critical role in supporting our multifunctional teams contribute to the agency’s housing objectives and performance targets. You will be joining our small but enthusiastic, friendly, talented and experienced team of 5 people, who are committed to making positive change in ensuring effective performance monitoring and the achievement of regional goals.

Delivery Support is dedicated to delivering a first-class customer service, taking an innovative and creative approach to programme queries. The Delivery Support Team believes in working in a collaborative and flexible way to foster good decision making and problem solving within their team.

Job Description

This is a rare and exciting opportunity to join Delivery Support in a fast growing, national organisation with real social purpose. Working with key stakeholders across all teams, you have the opportunity to contribute to continuous improvement initiatives that enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of Delivery Support, helping the agency achieve its housing objectives and organisational goals.

Person specification

You'll be comfortable supporting the multifunctional teams with programme and system guidance. You will be proficient in Microsoft tools, able to utilise excel to an advanced level and have experience with Power BI.

Delivering on a mission as bold as ours isn’t always easy. You will be resilient and comfortable managing stakeholders at a senior level, and your discussions will always be shaped by a good understanding of the governance and quality assurances impact of what we do as a Delivery Support team. You will enjoy working in an innovative, fun and engaging way and be able to negotiate and persuade.

Whether you background is in stakeholder management, finance, business operations, or a related field, you will understand the importance of producing accurate and reliable management information, managing stakeholder expectations and meeting agreed timescales. You will have excellent organisational and communication skills and be able to work collaboratively within a team, building positive relationships and contributing to an inclusive environment. You will be comfortable managing multiple priorities and presenting data and insights to a range of audiences. You will take a proactive approach to problem solving and continuous improvement, identifying opportunities to enhance reporting, automate processes and maximise the value of Power BI and other analytical tools across the team.
