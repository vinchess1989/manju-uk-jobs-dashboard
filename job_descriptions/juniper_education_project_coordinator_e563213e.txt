We're looking for a confident, organised and customer-focused Project Coordinator to join our team, taking ownership of website design and branding projects from initial commission through to successful launch.

You'll work closely with our customers, guiding and supporting them throughout the entire journey of developing their new website or branding products. You'll be responsible for ensuring projects run smoothly, customer expectations are managed and high-quality results are delivered within agreed timescales and company KPIs.

This is a varied, fast-paced role where building strong relationships, communicating effectively and managing multiple projects simultaneously will be key to your success.

You'll act as the main point of contact for your customers, helping them understand the process, providing guidance and recommendations, managing expectations and ensuring they feel supported every step of the way.

This is a remote role, offering the flexibility to work from home. However, for candidates based locally who would prefer to work from an office, we also have offices in Newark and Peterborough, which you would be welcome to attend.

Key Responsibilities

As a Project Coordinator, your key responsibilities will include:


Managing customer website design orders, branding collateral and branding projects from initial commission through to completion and launch.
Taking ownership of your project pipeline, ensuring projects are delivered on time, to the required quality standards and in line with company KPIs.
Building strong relationships with customers, understanding their requirements and providing appropriate recommendations and guidance throughout the project.
Clearly explaining the project delivery process, customer responsibilities, requirements and website functionality to ensure expectations are understood from the outset.
Managing customer expectations and confidently handling objections, including discussions around additional costs for work outside the agreed project scope.
Building effective relationships with internal teams and ensuring communication is clear, timely and consistent.
Coordinating multiple projects simultaneously, monitoring progress at each stage and ensuring tasks are allocated to the right people to meet agreed deadlines.
Monitoring project performance against targets and KPIs, providing regular updates and reports to management.
Identifying opportunities to introduce customers to additional products, services and referral opportunities where appropriate.
Ensuring projects follow agreed processes and frameworks, with all relevant documentation accurately maintained and accessible across the business.
Maintaining a strong knowledge of our products and services so you can confidently support customers and answer queries.
Keeping up to date with Department for Education (DfE) requirements and identifying opportunities to help customers improve their knowledge and understanding.
Investigating and resolving customer concerns or complaints, escalating more complex or serious issues to senior management where appropriate.


About You


Experience working within a project management or coordination environment. A minimum of two years' experience would be desirable but is not essential.
A strong customer service background, with a genuine commitment to delivering an excellent customer experience.
Excellent relationship-building skills and the confidence to communicate with stakeholders at all levels.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to build trust and maintain positive working relationships.
Confidence in hosting customer meetings and presentations, both individually and with larger groups.
The ability to manage multiple projects and competing priorities effectively, while maintaining attention to detail.
Strong organisational skills and the ability to manage your own workload, take initiative and meet deadlines.
The confidence to handle challenging conversations, manage expectations and discuss additional costs where necessary.
The ability to receive feedback and communicate it clearly and effectively to the relevant internal teams.
A collaborative approach, with the ability to work effectively with colleagues across different departments.


Experience or an understanding of the education sector would be an advantage, as would an interest in digital products, website design and branding.
