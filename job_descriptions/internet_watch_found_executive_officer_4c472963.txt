As our Executive Team & Project Coordination Officer, you'll report directly to the CEO and work closely with our Head of Legal & Governance. You'll be the organisational backbone of our senior leadership team, managing complex diaries, coordinating Board and committee business and supporting the strategic projects that drive our mission forward.




What you'll be doing

Managing the CEO's diary, meetings and correspondence
Organising and servicing Board, committee and strategy meetings, including preparing papers and taking accurate minutes
Tracking actions to completion and helping monitor progress against strategic objectives
Supporting governance processes, trustee records, inductions and engagement
Drafting correspondence and communications on behalf of senior leaders
Coordinating travel, events and logistics, and supporting high-profile organisational events and projects
Supporting the wider Executive Team




Who we're looking for

You'll be an experienced Executive Assistant, Project Coordinator or senior administrator who's comfortable working at Executive and Board level. You'll bring excellent minute-taking and governance administration skills, outstanding organisation and the judgement and discretion to handle sensitive information with care. You'll be proactive, calm under pressure and confident building relationships with senior stakeholders and trustees. You'll also be proficient in Microsoft 365 and experienced in using approved AI tools effectively in your work.

Charity sector experience or knowledge of trustee governance would be helpful, but we welcome applicants from corporate, public sector and commercial backgrounds too.

You will not be required to view criminal images. All applicants will be subject to enhanced DBS checks prior to confirmation of employment.
