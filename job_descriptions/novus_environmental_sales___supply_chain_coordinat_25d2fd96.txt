We are seeking an ambitious and organised Sales & Supply Chain Coordinator to join our

growing team. This is an excellent opportunity for an individual looking to build a long-term

career within the environmental services sector.




Reporting directly to the Commercial Manager, you will play a key role in supporting the

commercial and operational functions of the business. The position combines sales

administration, supplier engagement, reporting, quotation support and supply chain

coordination.




This role is designed as a development opportunity for someone who is eager to learn and

progress. Through structured support, training and hands-on experience, the successful

candidate will develop the skills required to ultimately progress into a Technical Sales

Manager position within the business.




Key Responsibilities

Sales Support & Administration

• Provide administrative support to the Business Development team.

• Prepare, maintain and distribute sales reports and performance data.

• Support the management of customer records, opportunities and quotations within

company systems.

• Assist with customer onboarding and account administration.

• Ensure accurate maintenance of pricing, supplier and customer information.




Supplier & Supply Chain Coordination

• Build and maintain positive working relationships with waste treatment and disposal

suppliers.

• Liaise with suppliers to obtain pricing, service information and operational updates.

• Coordinate supplier quotations and service solutions to support customer

requirements.

• Assist in managing supplier performance and service delivery standards.

• Support the development and expansion of Novus Environmental's supplier network.




Quotation & Commercial Support

• Assist in preparing specialist and hazardous waste quotations for customers.

• Work closely with suppliers and internal stakeholders to source compliant and cost-

effective solutions.

• Support tender submissions and pricing exercises where required.

• Help identify opportunities to improve margins and service offerings.




Reporting & Analysis

• Produce regular reports on sales activity, supplier performance and commercial

performance.

• Analyse data to support decision-making and identify business opportunities.

• Assist with forecasting, pipeline management and business reviews.




Customer Service & Relationship Management

• Deliver excellent customer service and maintain strong relationships with both

customers and suppliers.

• Respond to customer and supplier enquiries in a professional and timely manner.

• Support the sales team in delivering high levels of customer satisfaction and

retention.




About You

We are looking for a motivated individual who is keen to learn, develop and build a career

within a commercial sales environment.




Essential Requirements

• Excellent organisational and administrative skills.

• Strong attention to detail and accuracy.

• Confident communication skills, both written and verbal.

• Proficient in Microsoft Office, particularly Excel.

• Ability to manage multiple tasks and priorities effectively.

• Positive attitude and willingness to learn.

• Strong problem-solving and relationship-building skills.




Desirable Requirements

• Experience in a sales support, sales administration, coordination or supply chain role.

• Knowledge of waste management, environmental services or logistics industries.

• Experience working with CRM systems.

• Experience producing reports and analysing commercial data.




Career Development

This role has been created for an individual with ambition and potential. We are committed to

investing in the successful candidate through training, mentoring and development

opportunities.




As your knowledge of the industry, customers and commercial processes grows, you will be

given increasing responsibility and exposure to customer-facing sales activities. The long-

term objective is for the successful candidate to develop into a fully-fledged Technical Sales

Manager, managing customer relationships and generating new business opportunities

across Novus Environmental's specialist waste management services.




What We Offer

• Competitive salary of £32,000.

• Structured career development pathway.

• Ongoing training and mentoring.

• Opportunity to join a growing and ambitious business.

• Supportive and collaborative working environment.

• The chance to make a meaningful contribution to environmental sustainability and

compliance.




Company Benefits

• Enhanced maternity pay.

• Enhanced paternity pay.

• Life Insurance if in our pension scheme.

• Access to our retail discount platform.

• Birthday Leave.

• Onsite free parking*.

• Referral programme.




*Due to the remote location of our site, it is not possible to reach us via public transport.
