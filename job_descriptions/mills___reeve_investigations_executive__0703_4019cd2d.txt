Join our growing Employment Investigations practice and play an important role in supporting complex and sensitive workplace investigations.

The role

Working closely with Neil Pugh, Employment Investigations Specialist, you'll play an important role throughout the investigation process. You'll attend confidential meetings, produce clear and accurate summaries of evidence, support witnesses, coordinate investigation logistics and help prepare reports and documentation.

The work can involve sensitive and emotive matters, including bullying, discrimination and sexual harassment, so discretion, sound judgement and exceptional attention to detail are essential. You'll also need strong interpersonal skills to support witnesses who may feel anxious about giving evidence.

As the practice continues to grow, you'll have opportunities to develop your role, become more involved in fact-gathering meetings, provide direct support to investigators and contribute to the ongoing development of the team and its processes.

The person


Strong administrative and communication skills, including excellent note-taking and the ability to summarise complex or sensitive discussions clearly and accurately.
Highly organised, discreet and detail-focused, with the ability to manage competing priorities, work at pace and build trust with witnesses and clients.
Confident using MS365 and managing documentation securely, ideally with experience in HR/ER, investigations or legal environments, including evidence review and sensitive data handling.


The activities


Take accurate notes during confidential investigation meetings.
Coordinate witnesses and clients throughout the investigation.
Manage evidence and documentation, including reports and bundles.
Support investigators with interviews and fact gathering, with scope to take on greater involvement as your experience develops.
