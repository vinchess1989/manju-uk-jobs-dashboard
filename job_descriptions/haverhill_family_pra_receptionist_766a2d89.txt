Job summary

Haverhill Family Practice are seeking two enthusiastic and reliable Receptionists to join their team on a part-time basis for 20 hours per week, to work the following:

Mornings:

Monday 8am -1pm
Tuesday 8am -1pm
Thursday 8am -1pm
Friday 8am - 1pm

Afternoons:

Monday 1.30pm - 6.30pm
Tuesday 1.30pm - 6.30pm
Wednesday 1.30pm - 6.30pm
Friday 1.30pm - 6.30pm

Main duties of the job

Duties will include:

Welcoming patients via telephone or face to face.
Answering incoming calls in a prompt, friendly efficient manner.
Booking of appointments.
Arranging home visit requests.
Signposting patients to the correct clinician
Receiving and processing daily E-consults from patients
Handing repeat prescription/med3 requests
Registering new patients
Liaising with members of practice team
Managing out-going correspondence
Updating patient demographics
General administration duties
Daily auctioning of tasks

About us

Haverhill Family Practice is a Haverhill based GP surgery offering a wide range of health services. With a CQC rating of "Good" and an experienced clinical team, they aim to provide a high standard of medical care and are always fully committed to patient needs.

Person Specification
Experience
Essential
Ability to work in a polite, confidential and discreet manner, respecting patients and colleagues at all times.
Experience of working with the general public, being sensitive and empathic in distressing situations.
Ability to work as part of a team.
Strong IT and administration skills, with a high level of accuracy and attention to detail.
Previous medical or healthcare receptionist experience is desirable.
