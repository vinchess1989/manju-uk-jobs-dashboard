Job Description

The Resident Engagement Assistant plays a key role in strengthening how tenants and leaseholders shape the services they receive. You will assist with meaningful opportunities for residents to participate in local decision‑making, ensuring the Council meets its responsibilities as a registered landlord.

In this dynamic and people‑focused role, you will assist with engagement events, consultations, surveys and resident‑led projects. You will play a pivotal role in developing content for a range of online and printed communications, including social media, webpages and printed publications.

This role is ideal for someone passionate about inclusive engagement, community voice and delivering positive outcomes for Cambridge’s tenants and leaseholders. The postholder will also have the opportunity to complete a funded community engagement apprenticeship.
