OFFICE ADMINISTRATOR

Maximum Entertainment is a global entertainment company dedicated to creating high quality experiences spanning the entire value chain of video games, including development, publishing, transmedia, sales and operations. Maximum Entertainment provides collaborative resources to its partners through in-house publishing, as well as its development division. With more than 300 titles in its catalogue, Maximum Entertainment has partnered with best-in-class creators and franchises around the world to deliver magic to the gamer in everyone. Maximum Entertainment has offices around the world and employs more than 200 professionals.

The OFFICE ADMINISTRATOR is an impactful role providing services to multiple departments within the group. The person reports to the Global Director of People and is responsible for managing the office administration for St. Ives, Cambridgeshire location, supporting recruitment for the Group’s European locations, as well as administration of contracts for the Group. This is a Part-time onsite position. Working days are Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday. Flexibility on hours can be discussed.

In This Role, You Will

Primary Duties- Fulfil Office Management Functions


Manage and maintain overall office appearance and functionality.
Maintain office supplies and equipment inventories.
Manage logistics for any in-person meetings and events.
Manage relationships with building vendors and service providers.
Plan and coordinate staff engagement initiatives and events, including social activities, team-building sessions, lunch clubs, and game launch celebrations.
Onboarding of new starters.
Help drive process and build strong work relationships to manage overall retention and engagement.
Support the Global People Team.
Management of the relationship with UK's H&S advisors and ensuring compliance to Health and Safety
Provide admin support as needed to Legal team.
Other duties as needed.


Ideally, You Have


Bachelor’s degree or 3+ years of relevant professional experience in an office
Ability to think strategically and implement creative solutions
Strong communication, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build and maintain strong relationships with key stakeholders at all levels in the organisation
Comfortable managing competing priorities with precision and organisation
Proficiency with Excel and Microsoft Office Suite a must
Ability to do errands as needed with a valid driving licence
