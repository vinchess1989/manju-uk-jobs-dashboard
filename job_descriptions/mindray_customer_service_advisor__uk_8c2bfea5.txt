Location: Huntingdon / UK

We’re looking for a proactive and enthusiastic Customer Service Advisor to join our Operations team.

This is much more than an order-processing role. You’ll take ownership of the customer journey, managing orders from receipt through to delivery and, where applicable, installation. You’ll work closely with customers and colleagues across Sales, Finance, Logistics, Warehouse and Service to make sure orders progress smoothly and issues are resolved quickly.

You’ll be joining a busy, collaborative team where energy, initiative and a willingness to learn are just as important as previous experience.

What You’ll Be Doing


Processing and managing customer orders accurately using MSP/SAP.
Acting as a key point of contact for customers by phone and email.
Monitoring orders, delivery dates and ETAs and proactively keeping customers updated.
Taking ownership of customer enquiries and helping resolve issues through to completion.
Working with Sales, Finance, Logistics, Warehouse and Service to coordinate customer requirements.
Identifying order or delivery issues and escalating them appropriately.
Supporting installation-related orders and customer requirements.
Assisting with returns, credits, amendments, cancellations and invoice enquiries.
Maintaining accurate customer and order information.
Helping manage the shared Customer Service workload and priorities.
Looking for opportunities to improve processes and the overall customer experience.
Complete weekly reporting to ensure project data is accurate, up to date, and reflects the latest project status.


What We’re Looking For

We’re particularly interested in someone who brings a positive attitude, energy and initiative.

You’ll Ideally Have


A genuine passion for delivering excellent customer service.
Strong communication skills and a professional, friendly approach.
Great attention to detail.
The ability to organise and prioritise a busy workload.
Confidence taking ownership and solving problems.
A proactive mindset and willingness to ask questions and learn.
The ability to work independently while being a strong team player.
Good Microsoft Office skills.
Previous Customer Service, Sales Administration or Order Management experience would be beneficial but not essential.
Experience with SAP or another ERP/order-processing system is desirable, but a strong willingness to learn is equally important.


What Will Make You Successful?

You don’t need to know everything on day one. We’re looking for someone who is curious, adaptable and keen to develop.

If you enjoy working with people, take pride in getting things right, naturally look for solutions and want to build your knowledge within a fast-paced operational environment, we’d like to hear from you.

NOTE: This job description is not intended to be all-inclusive. Employee may perform other related duties as negotiated to met the ongoing needs of the organization .
