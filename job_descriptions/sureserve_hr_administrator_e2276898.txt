About Sureserve Energy Services Meters:

Sureserve Energy Services Meters Ltd, is a subsidiary of Sureserve, we specialise in installing domestic smart meters across the UK, working directly with 3 of the “Big Six” energy suppliers in the UK.

As a leader in the smart metering industry, Sureserve Energy Services Meters Ltd, is dedicated to advancing energy efficiency and sustainability through innovative metering solutions. Our mission is to empower consumers and businesses with accurate, real-time data, enabling informed energy decisions.

Role Overview:

The HR Administrator provide professional and effective HR administration to support for our business needs, departments, and that of our employees. Confidentiality is crucial for this role as there is access to sensitive information so in accordance with GDPR the job holder must be aware of how to be discreet and always ensure levels of sensitivity.

Key Responsibilities:


Ensure completion of the onboarding process for new starters, including but not limited to evidence of Rights to Work in UK, insurances, driving licences, references, completed application form.
Process DBS applications and bring any relevant factors to the attention of the HR Advisor.
Prepare offer letters and contracts as required.
Prepare and send out identity badges, Induction packs and arrange for Induction with HR on first day of joining.
Ensure that all sickness absence notifications and related return to work documentation is received from managers in a timely manner and this information is recorded accurately on the HR Database
Produce monthly report on Return to Work for Operations Director
Ensure completion of the leavers process and action the leaver notification when a leaver is confirmed.
Carry out Exit Interviews for all leavers and prepare report on role, length of service, reason for leaving.
Ensure any information for payroll is process in a timely manner and sent to accounts department.
Ensure probationary reviews are conducted in a timely manner by setting reminders with managers, send out formal invite letters and outcome letters and update on HR Database accordingly.
To be an effective representative of the Company and to act at all times in a professional manner, promoting a positive Company image.
Undertake any other duties which commensurate with the post at the request of the HR Advisor and HR Business Partner.
Attendance at internal or external company events may be required from time to time.


Qualifications, key skills and experience required:


Experience within an administrative role
CIPD qualification (or studying towards) – Desirable
Have good general IT skills and the ability to work with all Outlook packages.
Has excellent written and oral communication skills.
Has an ability to manage their time and work independently.
Able to maintain a professional stance and appreciate confidentiality.
The ability to work well within a team
Ability to working on your own initiative and handle conflicting priorities
