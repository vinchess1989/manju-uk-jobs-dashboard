Full job description
The Contracts and Procurement Assistant provides vital administrative and coordination support to the contracts and procurement team, ensuring accurate documentation and efficient delivery of contractual and procurement processes.

This includes maintaining contract records, processing variations, updating systems, and supporting governance requirements for contracts across primary care, dental
services, community providers, mental health, acute trusts, voluntary sector and independent sector organisations, working in a flexible way in line with team priorities.

The role also assists with procurement activities, including support to individual procurement processes, management of appropriate databases, coordinates meetings, prepares reports, and handles routine internal and external contract and procurement queries.

The responsibilities include:
1. Administrative and Operational Support: Provide day-to-day administrative assistance to ensure the effective running of programmes, services, or projects. Manage correspondence, maintain records, and support the coordination of tasks and deadlines, ensuring empathy when communicating with other parties.

2. Data and Information Management: Regularly collect, input, and maintain accurate data in relevant systems. Support the preparation of reports, spreadsheets, and dashboards to inform monitoring and assurance activities which may present complex or contentious information and require comparison or analysis of a range of data and options.

3. Project and Programme Support: Assist in the coordination of project activities by preparing materials, scheduling meetings, taking minutes, and following up on agreed actions.

4. Quality and Improvement: Contribute to service improvement activities by helping gather feedback, maintaining documentation, and supporting small improvement
initiatives. Use motivational and persuasive communications to achieve tasks. Provide feedback on service improvements as part of the continuous improvement process.

5. Governance and Compliance: Ensure all work complies with organisational policies and procedures, including information governance and confidentiality. Maintain clear audit trails and accurate documentation at all times. Propose changes to working practices and policies for work area.
