Are you looking to join a growing, values-led organisation with a clear social purpose?

At SettleParadigm, we’re proud to be the largest housing group in the region, managing over 30,000 homes across Buckinghamshire, Bedfordshire, and Hertfordshire.

Everything we do is about delivering excellent services, high quality homes, neighbourhoods we can be proud of and maximising number of new affordable homes we build. Through our merger we’ve brought together shared values, skills and ambition, so we can build more affordable homes and make an even bigger difference in the communities we serve

If you're ready to grow your career in a supportive, inclusive environment while helping to shape stronger communities, we’d love to welcome you on our journey. Together, we’re building a better future.

We’re excited to be recruiting Customer Hub Advisors to join our friendly, supportive team. These roles are open to both internal and external applicants.

At Lifestone Homes, our Customer Hub is the first point of contact for residents. We are a close and collaborative team who work together to provide a great customer experience. Every conversation matters, whether it’s about repairs, payments, tenancy changes or simply helping someone find the right information. If you enjoy helping people and want to make a real difference every day, this could be a great opportunity.

About The Role

As a Customer Hub Advisor, you’ll be the first person our customers speak to. You’ll listen carefully, understand what they need and work with colleagues across the organisation to get things sorted.

You’ll handle a wide range of enquiries, from repairs and tenancy changes to payments, complaints and safeguarding concerns. Some calls will be simple, others more challenging, but you’ll approach each one with patience, empathy and professionalism.

This is a role where you’ll learn a lot, build strong relationships and play a key part in helping us deliver a service residents can trust.

Key Responsibilities / Typical Day In The Hub


Responding to customers by phone, email and live chat
Supporting tenancy changes such as successions and assignments
Booking repairs and working with Property Services
Managing mutual exchange requests
Processing garage lettings and terminations
Raising purchase orders and invoices
Recording accurate information on our systems
Handling complaints fairly and professionally
Taking payments safely and correctly
Spotting safeguarding concerns and escalating them


What we’re looking for

Must haves


Experience delivering excellent customer service
Clear and confident communication skills
Ability to complete admin tasks accurately and consistently
A calm, supportive approach, even when conversations are challenging
Confidence to ask questions, listen actively and find the right solution
Ability to manage your workload during busy periods
Willingness to take part in a practical recruitment event


Nice to haves


Experience in a front‑line customer service or contact centre environment
Understanding of social housing
Interest in working towards a CIH qualification
Confidence using multiple systems or learning new digital tools
