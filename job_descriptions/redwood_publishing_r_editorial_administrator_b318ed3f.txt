Theresa Duncan

A well-established academic publisher in the Cambridge area is looking for an organised and assertive Editorial Administrator to join its collaborative editorial team. This highly interesting and varied role will help guide research articles through submission, peer review and publication, while providing an excellent service to authors, academic editors and reviewers around the world. This role offers hybrid working, with 3 days in the office and 2 days working from home.




Some of the key responsibilities include:

Administering submissions using an online manuscript-tracking system.
Checking new and revised articles against submission requirements.
Coordinating peer review, including inviting reviewers and monitoring deadlines.
Preparing editorial decision letters and respond to enquiries.
Checking accepted manuscripts before they move into production.
Maintaining accurate records and keep articles progressing through the workflow.
Supporting editorial meetings, reports and occasional journal projects.




About you

The successful person will have:

Previous administrative or office-based experience (ideally in a publishing or scientific environment but this is not essential).
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Strong organisation and attention to detail.
Confidence using databases and other IT systems.
A proactive, professional and team-oriented approach.




Experience in publishing, science or higher education would be desirable, but is not essential as training will be provided. We are looking for someone who has office experience and is looking for an opportunity to develop their skills and experience, in a highly varied and exciting publishing role.




This is an excellent opportunity to work in a fun, collaborative environment and develop a career in scientific publishing within a supportive, purpose-led organisation within a role that is diverse and interesting.




Our client offers a generous salary plus excellent company benefits including a generous pension scheme, 27 days holiday, plus bank holidays, well person checks and one day birthday leave.

For further information on this highly desirable role, please send your CV and a short cover letter to: info@redwoodrecruitment.com




Early applications are welcomed, as this role is available from the end of November.

Candidates must have the legal right to work in the UK
