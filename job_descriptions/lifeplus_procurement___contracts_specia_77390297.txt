About the role

Do you enjoy negotiating great commercial outcomes, building strong supplier relationships and helping colleagues make informed business decisions?

We're looking for a Procurement & Contracts Specialist to join our team and play an important role in how Lifeplus purchases goods and services across the organisation.

In this role, you'll work closely with colleagues from across the business, helping them source suppliers, manage contracts and make commercially sound decisions. You'll lead procurement activities from initial supplier engagement through to contract award, ensuring we achieve value for money whilst balancing risk, compliance and operational needs.

You'll also take ownership of contract management activities, helping to ensure key agreements remain up to date, commercial obligations are managed effectively and renewal opportunities are identified in a timely manner.

This is a varied role that combines procurement, supplier management, contract management and stakeholder engagement, making it ideal for someone who enjoys working across different teams and influencing business-wide decisions.

A little more about what you'll do

Managing procurement activities


Lead procurement exercises for a variety of goods and services across the business.
Manage supplier sourcing, tendering and quotation activities.
Support Requests for Information (RFIs), Requests for Proposal (RFPs) and supplier evaluations.
Provide recommendations that help colleagues make informed purchasing decisions.


Managing contracts


Maintain oversight of contracts throughout their lifecycle.
Keep the central contracts register up to date.
Monitor renewals, notice periods and key commercial obligations.
Help reduce risk and ensure contracts continue to meet business needs.


Working with suppliers


Build and maintain positive relationships with suppliers and service providers.
Negotiate pricing, commercial terms and contractual arrangements.
Help identify opportunities to improve value and efficiency.
Support fair and objective supplier selection processes.


Supporting colleagues across the business


Partner with colleagues across multiple departments to understand business requirements.
Provide guidance throughout procurement and contract related activities.
Work closely with Finance, Legal and other stakeholders to support compliant decision-making.
Help colleagues navigate procurement processes with confidence.


Improving how we work


Identify opportunities to improve procurement and contract management processes.
Support the development of consistent procurement practices across the organisation.
Help drive initiatives that improve efficiency, governance and commercial outcomes.
Share ideas and recommendations that contribute to continuous improvement.


What we're looking for

We're not looking for someone who has done everything before. We're looking for someone who enjoys building relationships, finding practical solutions and helping colleagues make informed commercial decisions.

You'll need


Experience in procurement, purchasing, supplier management or contract management.
Strong communication skills and confidence working with a variety of stakeholders.
Experience managing supplier relationships and supporting commercial negotiations.
Good organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.
Strong attention to detail and the ability to balance commercial opportunities with potential risks.
Confidence analysing information and making recommendations that support business decisions.


It would be helpful, but isn't essential, if you also have


Experience working within an international or multi-site organisation.
Experience supporting procurement process improvements or efficiency initiatives.
Experience working closely with Legal, Finance or compliance teams.
A professional procurement qualification such as CIPS.
