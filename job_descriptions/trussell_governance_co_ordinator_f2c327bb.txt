Job description

Role Purpose

The Governance Co-ordinator roleplays a central role in monitoring governance and consistent operational standards across food banks. This role ensures that risk, compliance, and governance processes are followed, quality checks are completed on time, and Area Managers, Network Leads, and volunteers receive timely and accurate information to support decision making. It may also be required to support other members of the Specialist Services team.

This role is part of Trussell’s Food Bank Resilience programme, the goal of which is to support every food bank to develop and sustain the core offer of a warm welcome, food and advice for every person, for as long as their services are needed, while orienting their organisations towards an end to the need for food banks.  This role is focused on the successful delivery of the overall programme outcomes, contributing to the fulfilment of our long term vision of a UK without the need for food banks.

Key Responsibilities

·       Oversight of the administration of food bank governance health checks

Support effective governance monitoring. This includes issuing documentation, updating records, initiating reminders and following the escalation process to ensure information accuracy is logged to Salesforce for each food bank to achieve timely completion of Governance Health Checks, demonstrating a safe and legal food bank.

 

·       Production of records for the effective management of Governance Health Checks
Enable oversight of governance compliance across the food bank network. This includes producing management information to enable review of the Governance Health Check process to ensure network-wide compliance within the agreed risk framework of Trussell so that network-wide governance compliance can be monitored and effectively addressed.


·       Management of Salesforce for Governance Health Checks

Ensures systems effectively support governance and monitoring reporting.  Coordinate with the Salesforce team to provide information on Governance Health Check process updates and ensure these are effectively captured within the system.  This enables effective use of the system and preparation of clear, accurate and timely reports and dashboards to assist with trend analysis and anomaly identification, supporting strategic decision making.


·       Supervision of the Quality Assurance Volunteer Team

Support an effective and consistent service through supervision of the Quality Assurance Volunteer team. This includes providing pastoral support, setting clear expectations through leading weekly team meetings, reviewing the team’s outputs, and proactively escalating any risks identified to the Risk and Governance Manager (where appropriate), to ensure the Governance Health check system is effective and the team is an integrated part of Trussell.


·       Monitoring and managing Quality Assurance communications.

Ensure compliance queries are handled consistently and effectively. Manages the Quality Assurance inbox, being first point of contact for compliance queries, triaging information appropriately, and logging all communication in Salesforce. This ensures transparency and effective management of queries received to the inbox.

·       Maintaining a register of all Specialist Services Digital Hub documentation

Ensure governance documentation remains accurate, current and accessible. Conducts regular audits, prompts document owners when updates are required, and coordinates with the network events team to ensure timely publication of updates, enabling food banks to access accurate resources that support their Trustee duties and facilitate legal compliance.

Person Specification

Core Knowledge

·       Some understanding of charity governance and what constitutes a safe and legal charity.

·       Strong organisational skills, document control processes and ability to maintain methodical records.

Essential Skills

·       Strong communication and influencing skills, both written and verbal

·       Developed knowledge of MS Office applications, including Word, Excel, Outlook and PowerPoint

·       Understanding of how to use Form Assembly and DocuSign.

·       Consistent attention to detail

Key Experience

·       Experience of using Salesforce (or similar CRM), including accurate record keeping and report creation.
