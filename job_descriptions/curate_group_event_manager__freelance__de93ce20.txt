About Lumos Experiences

Lumos Experiences, is one of the most distinctive live entertainment concepts, creating atmospheric candlelit musical concerts that transform unexpected spaces into something genuinely special.

We're growing fast, running multiple shows per week across new locations, and are looking for a confident, guest-focused Event Manager, ideally based near Cambridge or Norwich, to lead our shows front-of-house in East Anglia.

About The Role

Job type: Freelance

Shift pattern: Fridays and Saturdays; shifts are typically 8 - 10 hours

Onboarding: Full training provided, including a paid training shift before your first solo event

Work location: In person, on site

Day rate: £160 - £200 per day (based on length of event)

This freelance role is perfect for anyone with experience organising events or leading front-of-house who thrives in live event environments.

As an Event Manager, you’ll be the on-site lead for our concerts (Fridays and Saturdays), responsible for set-up, the guest experience, team coordination and the smooth running of the show.

This is a hands-on event lead role. You’ll need strong attention to detail, the ability to think on your feet, the confidence to lead a small front-of-house team with warmth, and the organisation to keep the evening running exactly to plan.

What You'll Do

Event lead and ownership


Act as the on-site lead for all event operations
Take overall responsibility for the success of the evening
Ensure the event runs on time and to Lumos standards
Make confident, informed decisions in real time


Guest experience


Oversee the full guest journey from arrival to departure
Ensure a warm, professional and efficient check-in
Monitor audience comfort, flow and atmosphere
Handle guest enquiries and resolve issues discreetly
Uphold the immersive, premium tone of the experience


Team leadership


Brief and lead front-of-house staff and assistants
Delegate roles clearly (setting up concert, ticketing, ushering etc.)
Maintain morale and professionalism throughout the evening
Step in hands-on during busy periods


Show flow and coordination


Manage event timings (doors, performance start and interval, where applicable)
Liaise with musicians on readiness and cues
Ensure smooth transitions throughout the evening, with a team member always present in the room


Venue liaison


Act as the main point of contact for the venue on the night
Maintain positive relationships with venue staff
Ensure compliance with venue rules and access times
Manage closing procedures and the venue handover


Issue resolution


Calmly resolve last-minute challenges and guest complaints
Make quick, solutions-focused decisions
Escalate serious issues appropriately


Post-event


Oversee a safe and orderly guest exit
Confirm the venue is left in the agreed condition
Complete event reporting and a feedback summary
Communicate key notes back to the operations team


What We’re Looking For


Previous experience in event management, hospitality or front-of-house leadership
Strong attention to detail and complete reliability
A calm, confident decision-maker who’s comfortable leading a team on the night
Warm, professional communication skills and a real passion for guest experience
Happy to work weekend afternoons and evenings at venues across the region
A full UK driving licence and your own car, and willing to drive up to 2.5 hours for an event
Self-insured and happy to work on a freelance basis
Knowledge of H&S / first aid
Ideally based in or around Cambridge or Norwich
Comfortable with manual handling and lifting tasks
A love of live music and immersive experiences is a big plus


Why you’ll love working with us


You’ll join a small, friendly team that transforms beautiful venues into unforgettable live music experiences
You’ll be part of a creative, growing company, with the opportunity for more hours
It’s a flexible, freelance role


If you’re excited by the idea of running candlelit concerts in some of the most beautiful churches and venues in the region, we’d love to hear from you!
