Role Overview

Behind every successful development programme, workshop, mentoring initiative or learning platform is a well-organised operation that makes it easy for people to learn, grow and apply new skills.




At GÉANT, learning plays an important role in helping our people, teams and leaders build the capabilities needed to support a highly international organisation operating at the intersection of technology, research and education. As part of the GÉANT Learning & Development (GLAD) team, you'll help create the conditions that enable continuous learning and professional growth across the organisation.




This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys bringing structure to complex activities, working with a wide range of stakeholders and making processes run smoothly behind the scenes. You'll develop your expertise as an L&D professional while gaining opportunities to build skills in facilitation, presenting, eLearning design and development, and programme delivery.




What You'll Do:

Learning Delivery & Coordination

Support the design, development and delivery of internal learning activities and development programmes
Prepare learning materials, resources and participant communications
Provide support to facilitators and learners before, during and after learning activities
Coordinate learner registrations, joining instructions and post-session follow-up
Contribute to talent, mentoring and knowledge-sharing initiatives across the organisation




Learner & Stakeholder Experience

Act as a key point of contact for learning-related enquiries and requests through the GLAD inbox
Build positive relationships with learners, facilitators and internal stakeholders
Help resolve operational issues and ensure learning activities run smoothly
Promote learning opportunities and encourage engagement across the organisation




Learning Operations & Systems

Maintain learning processes, guidance documents, workflows and operational procedures
Administer learning platforms, licences and user access, including eAcademy, O'Reilly, LinkedIn Learning and iHASCO
Manage learning resources, documentation, SharePoint sites, webpages and knowledge repositories
Support governance, compliance, audit and record-keeping requirements across learning activities




External Learning & Supplier Management

Coordinate externally delivered workshops, programmes and development activities
Act as the primary point of contact for learning suppliers, facilitators and external partners
Maintain records of approved providers and support onboarding and scheduling activities
Coordinate contracts, procurement processes and invoice administration




Data, Reporting & Continuous Improvement

Produce reports on learning participation, engagement and programme activity
Monitor mandatory training completion and support organisational compliance requirements
Maintain accurate learning records and programme documentation
Analyse learning data, feedback and trends to identify opportunities for improvement
Track actions arising from learner feedback and support ongoing enhancement of learning services




About You:

We're open to candidates from a range of backgrounds. You may not have worked in a role exactly like this before, but you should be able to demonstrate many of the skills, experience and capabilities needed to succeed.




Please consider the responsibilities of the role carefully and how your experience aligns with the following areas:

Experience coordinating projects, programmes, events, services or activities involving multiple stakeholders
Strong administrative and organisational skills, including maintaining accurate records, documentation and processes
The ability to manage competing priorities while maintaining a high level of attention to detail
Confidence using a range of digital systems and tools to manage information and support daily operations
Clear and professional communication skills when working with internal and external stakeholders
Experience improving processes, solving operational challenges and identifying practical solutions
Ability to work independently while contributing positively to a collaborative team environment
A proactive, customer-focused approach with a strong sense of ownership and accountability
Confidence using Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel




Experience That Could Be Beneficial:

The following experience could be helpful, but is not required for every candidate:

Supporting learning, development, talent or people-related activities
Coordinating training sessions, workshops, events or facilitation activities
Facilitating virtual sessions using Zoom, Microsoft Teams or similar collaboration platforms
Administering learning platforms, digital systems or online knowledge resources
Working with external suppliers, facilitators, training providers or service partners
Collecting, analysing or reporting on data, survey results or stakeholder feedback
Supporting procurement, contract administration or invoice processes
Using SharePoint, content management systems or knowledge repositories
Working within an international, membership-based or not-for-profit organisation
Experience with systems such as Moodle, O'Reilly, iHASCO or Power BI




We recognise that people build experience in different ways and do not expect candidates to meet every point listed above. If your background aligns with many of these areas and you're interested in the opportunity, we'd like to hear from you.




Working Arrangements

The role follows our flexible-hybrid working model, with employees working flexibly between a remote and office environment.

There may also be occasional travel for meetings, events, project activity or stakeholder engagement, depending on the needs of the role.




What We Offer:

You'll receive excellent benefits at GÉANT – we offer a generous holiday allowance and a 5% end of year bonus/allowance as standard.

The other benefits you receive will depend on the country you are employed in, but you can rest assured that these will include an appropriate pension scheme and medical insurance.

For full details of our benefits package please see our careers website - Why Work for GÉANT.

If you need any reasonable adjustments during the recruitment process, please let us know. This could include adjustments to the application process, interview format, timing, accessibility needs, or anything else that would help you participate fully.
