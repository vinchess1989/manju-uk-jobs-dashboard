HR Assistant / People Assistant

Location: Peterborough, Stoke-on-Trent or Chesterfield (Hybrid)

Are you an organised, proactive administrator who thrives in a busy environment? Looking to build your career in HR? We'd love to hear from you!

We're looking for an enthusiastic and detail-oriented HR Assistant to join our busy HR team within a large, growing insurance business.

This is a fantastic opportunity for someone who enjoys variety, working with people and keeping things running smoothly behind the scenes. Whether you already have some HR experience or you're an experienced administrator looking to take your first step into HR, this could be the perfect next move.

You'll be joining a supportive team where no two days are the same, playing an important role in delivering a great employee experience across the business.

What you'll be doing:

As an HR Assistant, you'll provide essential administrative support across the entire employee lifecycle, ensuring our people receive an efficient, accurate and professional HR service.

Your responsibilities will include:


Employee lifecycle administration: Supporting everything from new starter offers, contracts and onboarding through to internal moves, promotions and leaver processing.
HR queries: Managing incoming requests through our HR ticketing system, ensuring queries are resolved accurately and within agreed service levels.
Employee changes: Processing changes to working hours, flexible working arrangements, maternity, paternity and shared parental leave.
HR documentation: Preparing employee correspondence, including contractual changes, employee relations letters and references.
HR systems and records: Maintaining accurate employee information, updating HR systems and ensuring records are handled confidentially.
Payroll support: Processing employee changes and ensuring information is accurate and submitted within deadlines.
Working collaboratively: Supporting HR Business Partners and Employee Relations colleagues with day-to-day people matters.
Continuous improvement: Identifying opportunities to streamline processes and improve the service we provide.
Compliance and reporting: Supporting audits, maintaining accurate documentation and ensuring HR processes meet internal and regulatory requirements.


What we're looking for:

We're looking for someone who is organised, reliable and comfortable juggling multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

Ideally, you'll bring:


Strong administration experience, ideally gained in a busy, high-volume environment.
Excellent attention to detail and a commitment to getting things right first time.
The ability to prioritise your workload, manage deadlines and work independently.
Strong communication skills and a friendly, professional approach.
Confidence using Microsoft Office, particularly Word, Excel and Outlook.
Experience using HR systems, databases or ticketing platforms.
A proactive mindset and a willingness to learn, contribute and support colleagues.
An understanding of the importance of confidentiality when handling sensitive employee information.


Previous HR experience would be great, but it's not essential. We're equally interested in hearing from strong administrators who have the right attitude, transferable skills and a genuine interest in developing a career in HR.

Why join us?

You'll be joining a large, evolving business where the HR team plays a key role in supporting our people and driving improvements.

This is an opportunity to gain exposure to a wide range of HR activities, develop your knowledge of the employee lifecycle and build valuable experience within a collaborative and supportive team.

If you're someone who enjoys being busy, takes pride in delivering great service and wants to develop your career in HR, we'd love to hear from you.
